## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. NBFCs Seek RBI Rethink on Revolving Credit Curbs**
NBFC lenders are pressing the RBI to reconsider its push to restrict them to term loans only and bar revolving credit products. The RBI has been signaling this for nearly two years and has already removed revolving credit references from its regulations. If enforced strictly, this could significantly impact operational flexibility for many NBFCs, particularly those offering cash credit/overdraft products. Only NBFCs authorized to issue credit cards would be exempt.

## 🏛️ RBI & Regulatory

**1. RBI Releases NBFC Upper Layer List for 2026-27 (Aug 6)**
RBI published the updated list of **17 systemically important NBFCs** in the Upper Layer under the Scale Based Regulation framework, based on March 31, 2026 financials:
- **Tata Sons** and **Tata Capital** retained in the list
- Tata Sons' CIC deregistration plea still under review
- All 17 UL-NBFCs face enhanced prudential, liquidity, and governance norms
*Source: [RBI Press Release](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63301), [Fortune India](https://www.fortuneindia.com/business-news/rbi-retains-tata-sons-on-upper-layer-nbfc-list-deregistration-plea-still-under-review/152469)*

**2. Simplified Upper Layer Framework — ₹1 Lakh Crore Threshold (Effective Jun 24)**
RBI's landmark amendment replaces the complex parametric scoring methodology with a single criterion:
- Any NBFC with **assets ≥₹1,00,000 crore** based on latest audited balance sheet = Upper Layer
- **Government NBFCs** now brought under Scale-Based Regulation; their concentration norm exemptions withdrawn
- This could reclassify entities like **REC, PFC, IRFC** (Jefferies noted potential regulatory upgrade)
*Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13407&Mode=0), [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-simplifies-upper-layer-nbfc-classification-₹1-lakh-crore-asset-threshold.html)*

**3. NBFCs Restricted to Term Loans — Revolving Credit Ban**
RBI's draft directions propose NBFCs can only provide **term loans**, effectively barring revolving credit products (cash credit, overdraft). Only NBFCs authorized to issue credit cards are exempt. Draft: 'Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies' Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation) Second Amendment Directions, 2026'. NBFCs are lobbying against this.
*Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/topic/nbfc), [ET BFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/tag/NBFC)*

**4. RBI New NBFC Registration Category (Feb 2026)**
New category of NBFCs — entities with **no public funds and no customer interface** — exempt from mandatory registration under Section 45-IA, subject to compliance conditions. A calibrated departure to reduce compliance burden for small, privately funded, non-retail NBFCs.
*Source: [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/rbi-amendments-2026-a-new-category-for-nbfc-registration-and-exemptions/)*

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive: Gold Loan Sector (Tuesday Focus)

**Gold Loans — The Undisputed Star of NBFC India**

**The Numbers:**
- NBFC gold jewellery loans surged **69.3% YoY** to ₹3.42 lakh crore in June 2026 — **fastest growth across ALL lending segments**
- Muthoot Finance standalone gold loan AUM: ₹1.63 lakh crore (+44% YoY in Q1 FY27)
- Gold loan AUM grew **50% in FY26** for Muthoot alone
- Gold loans overtook vehicle loans as the **largest securitised asset class** for the first time in April-June 2026

**Securitisation Boom:**
- Q1 FY27 securitisation issuances: **~₹60,000 Cr** (+22% YoY) — **strongest ever first quarter**
- **NBFCs accounted for 98%+** of all securitisation issuances
- Gold loans driving the supply side — investors demanding gold-backed paper

**Why It Matters for Muthoot Fincorp:**
1. **Regulatory tailwind**: RBI's tighter norms on unsecured lending are pushing consumers toward gold-backed borrowing — the organised sector benefits most
2. **Securitisation pipeline**: Strong investor appetite for gold loan ABS/Pass-through certificates means better ALM management
3. **Competition risk**: Banks are re-entering gold loans aggressively (higher ticket sizes), compressing yields
4. **Gold price sensitivity**: Fitch noted gold price movement as a key rating driver; range-bound gold could slow AUM growth
5. **RBI gold loan guidelines**: New norms expected to benefit organised players by improving transparency and customer confidence

**Key Watchpoints:**
- Jefferies cut Muthoot FY27 profit estimates — margin pressure from competition
- Gold loan securitisation is becoming a **two-way market** — originators (NBFCs) selling, banks/HFCs buying
- Government's **CGSMFI-2.0 extension** indirectly supports gold loan MFIs (MFIs diversifying into gold loans)
- Active MFI borrower base shrinking (101M from 133M) but those remaining have more responsible lending
