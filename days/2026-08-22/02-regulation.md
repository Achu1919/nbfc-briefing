### 1️⃣ [NEW_REG] Five Master Directions Issued Simultaneously — July 31, 2026

**What:** RBI consolidated all NBFC supervisory, audit, compliance, and cybersecurity directions into 5 new Master Directions, effective immediately.

**The Five Directions:**

| # | Direction | Reference |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Miscellaneous Supervisory Directions** — CFSS, Fair Practices Code, Nomination, **PCA Framework** | RBI/DoS/2026-27/467 |
| 2 | **Cybersecurity, Technology: Risk, Resilience & Assurance Framework** | RBI/DoS/2026-27/461 |
| 3 | **Statutory Audit Directions** — Appointment, tenure, rotation, eligibility of SAs/SCAs | RBI/DoS/2026-27/465 |
| 4 | **Compliance Function Directions** — CCO appointment, independence, reporting, tools | RBI/DoS/2026-27/466 |
| 5 | **Supervisory Returns** (consolidated return filing framework) | RBI/DoS/2026-27/468 |

**In Plain English:** This is RBI's biggest housecleaning exercise for NBFC regulation. All the scattered circulars, guidelines, and clarations from the past several years have been consolidated into one structured document per topic. Think of it like RBI tidying up its filing cabinet — same rules, but now they're in one place instead of scattered across dozens of old circulars.

**Impact on NBFCs:**
- **CFSS (Core Financial Services Solution)** mandatory for UL/ML NBFCs with 10+ branches — must implement a CBS-like centralised system. Quarterly progress reports to RBI.
- **Fair Practices Code tightened** — RBI specifically called out 5 unfair interest-charging practices (see detail below).
- **PCA Framework introduced for NBFCs** — first time ever (see Teaching Session below).
- **Statutory Audit** — Joint audit mandatory for NBFCs with ₹15,000+ crore assets. Auditor tenure: 3 years continuous, 6-year cooling off. Max 8 concurrent NBFC mandates per audit firm.
- **Compliance Function** — CCO mandatory for ML+ NBFCs with enhanced independence requirements.

**Muthoot Fincorp Note:** ⚠️ **Directly impacted on multiple fronts:**
- As a UL NBFC with hundreds of branches, MFL **must implement CFSS** and report quarterly progress. This is a significant IT/infrastructure investment.
- **Fair Practices Code** scrutiny is critical for gold loan operations — interest charging from disbursement date (not sanction) must be verified.
- **PCA Framework** applies to MFL as NBFC-UL. Must maintain CRAR above 15%, Tier 1 above 10%, and NNPA below 6% to avoid restrictions.

- **Statutory Audit** — as a large NBFC, MFL already under joint audit. Must verify auditor eligibility against new criteria.

### 4️⃣ [NEW_REG] PCA Framework Details — The Biggest Change for MFL

**What:** RBI introduced a Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) Framework specifically for NBFCs — never done before.

**In Plain English:** PCA is like RBI putting an NBFC on "academic probation." If your financial ratios fall below certain danger levels, RBI can restrict your dividends, force you to raise capital, restrict new lending, and even make it public. It's designed to intervene early before an NBFC becomes a crisis.

**Risk Thresholds for Non-CIC NBFCs (including MFL):**

| Indicator | Threshold 1 (Caution) | Threshold 2 (Warning) | Threshold 3 (Critical) |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **CRAR** | <15% but ≥12% | <12% but ≥9% | <9% |
| **Tier 1 Capital Ratio** | <10% but ≥8% | <8% but ≥6% | <6% |
| **NNPA Ratio** | >6% but ≤9% | >9% but ≤12% | >12% |

**Corrective Actions:**

| Threshold | Mandatory | Discretionary |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| **1** | Restrict dividends; Promoters infuse equity | Special supervisory actions, strategy, governance, capital, credit risk, HR, operations changes |
| **2** | Above + Restrict guarantees/contingent liabilities for group companies (CICs) | All common menu items |
| **3** | Above + Restrict capital expenditure (except tech upgradation within Board limits) + Restrict/reduce variable operating costs | All common menu items |

**Exit:** 4 continuous quarterly financial statements showing no breach (one must be annual audited), plus RBI supervisory comfort on sustainability of profitability.

**RBI may publish** when an NBFC is placed under PCA and when it exits — creating market discipline.

**Muthoot Fincorp Note:** As a UL NBFC, MFL is **directly subject to this framework**. The thresholds are:
- CRAR must stay above 15% (MFL typically maintains higher)
- Tier 1 must stay above 10%  
- NNPA must stay below 6% — **this is the most relevant for a gold loan NBFC** where NPAs are typically very low, but the IRAC discipline must be ironclad

**FP&A Action Items:**
1. Set up internal monitoring dashboards for CRAR, Tier 1, and NNPA at monthly/quarterly intervals
2. Define internal early warning triggers at least 200 bps above each threshold
3. Ensure no NPA upgrades happen without full arrears repayment (cf. Muthoot MCred penalty)
4. Prepare board-level reporting on PCA risk indicators quarterly

**Source:** RBI/DoS/2026-27/467, Chapter V — https://rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13586

## 📚 Daily Teaching Session: NBFC Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) Framework

### What is it?

The PCA Framework is RBI's early intervention mechanism for NBFCs showing signs of financial stress. Think of it as a **traffic light system** — green (normal), amber (caution), red (critical) — where RBI can progressively restrict an NBFC's operations if its key financial health indicators deteriorate.

Until July 31, 2026, PCA existed only for **banks** (introduced in 2017, revised in 2023). Now RBI has extended it to NBFCs for the first time, signalling that the largest NBFCs are systemically important enough to warrant bank-style supervisory intervention.

### The Technical Details

**Legal Basis:** Sections 45-JA, 45-K, 45-L and 45-M of the RBI Act, 1934.

**Applicability:**
- NBFCs-ND (non-deposit taking) in Middle, Upper, and Top Layers
- NBFCs-D (deposit taking) — all layers
- Excludes: Core Investment Companies (CICs), Primary Dealers, Housing Finance Companies
- NBFCs not accepting public funds are excluded

**Indicators Monitored:**

For **NBFCs-D and NBFCs-ND (excluding CICs)**:

| Indicator | What It Measures | Why It Matters |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **CRAR** | Total capital as % of risk-weighted assets | Overall capital adequacy |
| **Tier 1 Capital Ratio** | Core equity capital as % of RWA | Loss-absorbing capacity |
| **NNPA Ratio** | Net NPAs as % of net advances | Asset quality — the health check |

For **CICs** (different set):

| Indicator | Threshold 1 | Threshold 2 | Threshold 3 |
|-----------|------------|------------|------------|
| ANW/RWA | <30% but ≥24% | <24% but ≥18% | <18% |
| Leverage Ratio | ≥2.5x but <3x | ≥3x but <3.5x | ≥3.5x |
| NNPA | >6% but ≤9% | >9% but ≤12% | >12% |

**Trigger Mechanism:**
- Based on **audited Annual Financial Results** and/or **RBI Supervisory Assessment**
- RBI can impose PCA mid-year if circumstances warrant
- Breach of **any one** indicator triggers the corresponding threshold
- Escalation: if already at Threshold 1 and breach another indicator at Threshold 2, the higher threshold applies

**Corrective Actions — The Escalating Response:**

**Threshold 1 (Caution):**
- ❌ **Mandatory:** Restriction on dividend distribution/remittance of profits; Promoters/shareholders to infuse equity and reduce leverage
- ⚡ **Discretionary:** Special supervisory actions, strategy changes, governance changes, capital actions, credit risk controls, HR measures, profitability measures, operations changes

**Threshold 2 (Warning):**
- ❌ All Threshold 1 mandatory actions PLUS restriction on guarantees/contingent liabilities (for CICs)
- ⚡ Full discretionary menu

**Threshold 3 (Critical):**
- ❌ All Threshold 1 and 2 mandatory actions PLUS:
  - Restriction on capital expenditure (except technology upgradation within Board-approved limits)
  - Restriction/reduction in variable operating costs
- ⚡ Full discretionary menu

**Exit from PCA:**
1. No breaches in any parameter for **4 continuous quarterly** financial statements
2. One of these must be the **Annual Audited Financial Statement**
3. Subject to **RBI supervisory comfort** including assessment of sustainability of profitability
4. RBI may **publish** the exit decision

### Why It Matters

**For the NBFC Industry:**
- Creates a **pre-emptive framework** — RBI can intervene before a crisis, not after
- **Market discipline** — if RBI publishes PCA lists, investors and counterparties will react
- **Capital planning** becomes critical — you cannot let CRAR drift downward
- **Asset quality management** — NNPA above 6% triggers the first warning

**For Depositors:**
- PCA creates an additional layer of protection for depositors in NBFCs-D
- Dividend restrictions mean profits go to strengthening the balance sheet, not to shareholders

### Key Numbers/Thresholds Summary

| Parameter | Normal | Threshold 1 | Threshold 2 | Threshold 3 |
|-----------|--------|------------|------------|------------|
| **CRAR** | ≥15% | 12%–15% | 9%–12% | <9% |
| **Tier 1** | ≥10% | 8%–10% | 6%–8% | <6% |
| **NNPA** | ≤6% | 6%–9% | 9%–12% | >12% |
| **Dividend** | Allowed | Restricted | Restricted | Restricted |
| **Capital infusion** | Not required | Required | Required | Required |
| **Capex restriction** | None | None | None | Restricted |

### Recent Changes

The PCA Framework was introduced on **July 31, 2026** as part of the Miscellaneous Supervisory Directions. This is **entirely new for NBFCs**. Previously:
- Banks had PCA since 2017 (revised 2023)
- NBFCs had no equivalent formal framework
- RBI could take ad-hoc supervisory action but no structured escalation path
- Now NBFCs are treated at par with banks for early intervention

The framework is part of RBI's broader move to bring NBFC regulation closer to bank regulation under the SBR framework.

### Quiz Yourself

**Q1:** An NBFC has a CRAR of 11.5% and Tier 1 ratio of 7.5%. Which PCA threshold has it breached?
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**Threshold 2.** CRAR of 11.5% falls in the 9%–12% range (Threshold 2), and Tier 1 of 7.5% falls in the 6%–8% range (Threshold 2). The higher of the two applies — so the NBFC is at Threshold 2. Mandatory actions: dividend restriction + promoters must infuse equity.
</details>

**Q2:** An NBFC's NNPA rises to 8.5% in Q1 and stays there for 4 consecutive quarters. Can it exit PCA?
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**No.** Even though it stayed at the same level for 4 quarters, NNPA at 8.5% is still in Threshold 1 territory (>6% but ≤9%). To exit PCA, it needs to breach NO thresholds for 4 continuous quarters — meaning NNPA must fall to 6% or below. Simply maintaining a breached level doesn't count as recovery.
</details>

**Q3:** Can an NBFC under PCA at Threshold 1 still declare dividends?
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**No.** Restriction on dividend distribution is a **mandatory** corrective action at Threshold 1 itself. The NBFC must first restore its financial health and exit PCA before dividends can resume.
</details>
