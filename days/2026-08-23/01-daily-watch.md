## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Weekly Preview & Sector Health Check (Sunday)

**Theme of the week: the gold-loan race is on.** Nearly everyone is moving into — or scaling — gold lending: Tata Capital (via Yogloans), Aditya Birla Capital (1,000-branch build-out), Poonawalla Fincorp, plus banks (Federal, CSB, South Indian). CRISIL projects gold-loan NBFC AUM to **cross ₹4 lakh crore by March 2027** (~40% CAGR). Experian confirms the shift: gold loans are now ~41% of retail credit sourcing (from 18% in FY23), and finance companies' gold-loan sourcing share **doubled to 44%** while PSU banks fell to 37%. JPMorgan's bull case (10% of system credit in 5 years) and its contrary risk flag (margin compression, bank competition) frame the debate. **For incumbents like Muthoot Finance**, the watch-items are: yield/margin sustainability (~18–18.5% steady-state yield, NIM ~10.4%), gold-price direction (MCX ~₹1.62L, elevated), and whether new entrants' higher-LTV pricing forces a competitive response. Gold prices are driven by US Treasury liquidity actions and geopolitical (US–Iran) uncertainty — both surprise-spike risks.

**Sector health check by segment:**
- **Gold loans:** strongest growth (69% YoY system-wide), lowest credit risk (~0.2% bad-loan), but intensifying competition pressuring margins.
- **Microfinance:** recovering; borrow overhang clearing; NBFC-MFIs consolidating market share (44%); West Bengal the exception; larger MFIs raising targets.
- **Housing/affordable housing:** +11.4% YoY growth (June), steady; margin pressure on affordable-housing financiers flagged by 360 ONE.
- **MSME/commercial:** services credit +17.6% YoY; commercial real estate +33.8%; MSME lenders (Paisalo, etc.) tapping NCD markets.
- **Vehicles:** +15.2% YoY; CV asset quality is the one soft spot flagged this season (CareEdge).
- **Funding/CoF:** NBFC funding stays active — a large NCD issuance wave this month (see below); bank funding to NBFCs improving. ICRA sees CoF down 10–30bps in FY26.

**Funding pulse (this week):** **Indel Money** launched its biggest-ever public NCD issue — up to **₹500 cr**, coupons **9.00–11.50%**, tenors 400 days to 6 years, rated **A- (India Ratings)**, open Aug 18–31. **Paisalo Digital** closed a ₹300 cr NCD early (first tranche of a ₹900 cr programme). **Northern Arc** shareholders approved a ₹5,000 cr NCD issuance at its AGM (Aug 18). **Bajaj Finance** allotted ₹498 cr of secured NCDs (7.79% coupon, 2036 maturity); **Home First** considered a ₹150 cr NCD; **Poonawalla** approved ₹200 cr. SEBI is also set to raise the cap on annual debt ISINs to ease refinancing stress for NBFCs/corporates.
