## Regulatory Landscape This Week

No new RBI/SEBI circulars or notifications were issued over the weekend. Two draft consultations remain open with approaching deadlines:

**1. Draft NBFC Credit Facilities Amendment Directions, 2026 (Revolving Credit Ban)**
- **Status:** Feedback deadline **August 28, 2026** (3 days away)
- **What:** RBI proposes NBFCs may only offer term loans; revolving credit restricted to credit-card-authorized NBFCs only
- **Industry pushback:** Bajaj Finance, Tata Capital, Shriram Finance met Aug 14; FIDC drafting formal representation; Vinod Kothari Consultants submitted detailed supply-chain-finance carve-out request (Aug 20)
- **Key clarification:** DG Murmu (Aug 19, CNBC-TV18 Banking Transformation Summit): "Revolving credit as such was not permitted earlier and even now for NBFCs. What we are proposing is greater clarity." Said RBI's aim is not to prevent existing business activities
- **RBI-NBFC meeting (Aug 22):** RBI officials met NBFC heads; focused on draft guidelines and broader compliance. RBI stressed robust internal audit, SROs as first line of defence, and alignment with digital lending framework
- **MFL impact:** Gold loans are term loans — low direct impact. Check any MSME working capital, channel financing, or inventory funding products for draft definition exposure
- **Source:** RBI Press Release 2026-2027/825 (Aug 6); NDTV Profit (Aug 17); CNBC-TV18 (Aug 19); ET (Aug 16)

**2. Draft Interest Rates on Loans and Advances Directions, 2026**
- **Status:** Feedback deadline **September 11, 2026**
- **What:** First-ever harmonised interest rate framework for all REs (banks, NBFCs, cooperatives, AIFIs)
- **Key provisions:**
  - Interest at monthly rests, daily reducing balance, actual/actual day count
  - APR ceiling mandatory for microfinance and small-value loans (≤₹50,000)
  - Non-credit-risk spread components frozen for 3 years (BL NBFCs exempt)
  - External benchmark linkage optional for NBFCs (not mandated)
  - Migration deadline: April 1, 2029 for existing loan book
  - Effective date: April 1, 2027 for new loans
- **MFL impact:** Gold loan calculation methodology must align (likely already compliant if monthly rests/daily reducing). Microfinance portfolio needs board-approved APR ceiling. Three-year spread freeze applies to ML-layer NBFCs. Start gap analysis now
- **Source:** RBI Press Release 2026-2027/877 (Aug 12); Business Standard (Aug 12); ET (Aug 12)

## Teaching Note: Spread Freeze Under the Draft Interest Rate Framework

The draft proposes that for floating-rate loans, the spread over the benchmark has components — credit risk premium, operating cost, term premium, and business strategy premium. The credit risk premium can change only when a borrower's credit profile changes after a documented review. All other components cannot be revised for three years from first disbursement or last revision (whichever is later). Lenders may reduce these components earlier for customer retention on non-discriminatory grounds.

**Why this matters for FP&A:** If MFL reprices floating-rate gold loans or MSME loans, the non-credit-risk portion of the spread is locked for three years. This limits pricing flexibility in a rising-rate environment but also provides stability in a falling-rate environment (you can't be forced to reduce spreads). The gap analysis should map the current loan book by product, benchmark type, and spread composition to understand migration complexity.

**Base Layer NBFCs are exempt** from the three-year freeze and three-month reset frequency requirements. MFL, if classified as Middle Layer, would need to comply.
