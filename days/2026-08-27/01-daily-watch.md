## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — MSME & Commercial Lending *(Thursday focus)*

1. **The revolving-credit fight (live):** FISME formally asked RBI on Aug 25 to not ban NBFC revolving credit outright — it says working capital for productive enterprises is "economically different from consumer revolving credit". RBI's own concern is evergreening and app-based lending abuse. **Comments close Aug 28**; the final direction will define how NBFCs can serve MSME working capital going forward. NBFCs are only ~10–11% of MSME credit outstanding (PwC), so the systemic risk is limited, but for MSME-focused NBFCs (flexi-loan products) this is existential. MSMEs = ~31% of GDP, ~35% of manufacturing output, ~half of merchandise exports. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/banking/rbi-proposal-to-curb-nbfcs-revolving-credit-may-choke-msme-funding-says-industry-body-fisme/133510399))

2. **Who is actually growing (MSME Pulse, July 2026 — TransUnion CIBIL + SIDBI):** individual entrepreneurs are outgrowing entities — business-oriented loans to individuals grew **1.8x between Mar-2023 and Mar-2026 vs 1.5x for entity borrowers**; individuals are now ~28% of outstanding commercial balances (commercial credit ~₹65.8 lakh crore). Proprietorships and self-employed borrowers are the engine — exactly the segment NBFCs serve better than banks. ([TransUnion CIBIL](https://www.transunioncibil.com/lp/msme-pulse-jul-2026) · [MediaBrief](https://mediabrief.com/msme-credit-reaches-₹65-8-lakh-crore-as-individual-business-borrowers-gain-share-sidbi-transunion-cibil/))

3. **Portfolio rotation (Kotak IE, Aug 26):** with costs rising, NBFCs are deliberately shifting mix to higher-yield retail and MSME loans — Aditya Birla Capital and Tata Capital lead; prime-segment competition is intensifying as PSU banks get aggressive, while below-prime competition comes via DSA-sourced new entrants. The "asset-side rate war" may pause if a rate-hike narrative firms up — incremental rates could inch up in select segments. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-may-turn-to-higher-yield-retail-msme-loans-to-protect-margins/133527009))

4. **Secured-lending tilt (The Week, Aug 24):** NBFCs are rebuilding around secured products — gold loans >70% YoY growth, LAP, and double-digit growth in microfinance, unsecured business and personal loans; Home First guides ~25% growth FY27-28, MAS Financial targets 20–25% AUM growth. ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/08/24/nbfcs-india-secured-lending-trends.html))

5. **Watch-list for MSME books:** PSB MSME GNPA still highest by segment at 4.1%; CareEdge flags "vulnerable MSME borrowers" alongside vehicle and unsecured retail as key monitorables for FY27; the CGSMFI-2.0 microfinance guarantee scheme (cap raised to ₹1,000 cr for large NBFC-MFIs, ₹770 cr sanctioned so far) **expires Aug 31 — watch for a renewal announcement within days**. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/bank-asset-quality-improvement-may-slow-as-legacy-npas-shrink/133361667) · [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/finance/govt-extends-validity-of-credit-guarantee-scheme-for-microfinance-institutions-upto-august-31/articleshow/131634185.cms))
