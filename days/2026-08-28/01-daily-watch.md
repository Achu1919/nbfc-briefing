## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive (Friday) — Auto Finance & EV Lending

**The vehicle-finance market is healthy, segmented and shifting.** CRIF High Mark's "Wheels & Ambition" report (data as of June 2026):

- **Growth mix:** Used-car loans are the **fastest-growing segment — 26.2% five-year CAGR** (Jun'21→Jun'26), the "fastest-formalising opportunity"; **commercial vehicles +20.1% CAGR** (portfolio ~₹7.4 lakh cr) remain the clearest growth engine; two-wheelers +19.7% but are the *volume* product — **3.6 crore borrowers** (from 2.3 cr), 80% new-to-product; auto loans +17.6% to **₹9.9 lakh cr** with premiumisation reclaiming momentum. Vehicle finance is 11.4% of retail credit — flat for five years, so share gains come from mix, not share. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/cv-loans-grow-at-20-cagr-used-car-finance-emerges-fastest-growing-segment-report/133449662) · [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/personal-finance/used-car-loans-grow-fastest-as-indias-vehicle-finance-market-expands-beyond-metros-report-19975987.htm) · [CRIF PDF](https://www.crifhighmark.com/media/7384/wheels-finance-report-august-2026.pdf))
- **Premiumisation:** auto loans >₹15 lakh now **29.8% of originations** (from 27.6% in Q1FY25); average auto ticket ₹8.6 lakh; premium/electric two-wheelers >₹1 lakh rose from 38% → 43% of 2W originations; average auto-loan exposure grew at 9.2% CAGR. **For lenders this raises ticket and re-leveraging risk** — auto multi-loan borrowers up to 6.8%.
- **Who lends where:** NBFCs hold **65–70% share of two-wheeler and CV financing**; PSBs lead new auto (33.6%); private banks dominate used cars (41.8%) with SFBs rising; NBFCs ~30% of auto, ~40% of used-car.
