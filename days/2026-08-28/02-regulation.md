# NBFC Regulatory Daily Report — Friday, 28 August 2026

## Regulatory Update

### Deadline Today: Revolving Credit Ban Comment Window Closes

**What:** The RBI's draft amendment barring NBFCs from offering revolving credit products has its comment deadline today, 28 August 2026.
**Issuer:** RBI, Department of Regulation
**Date released:** 6 August 2026
**Deadline:** 28 August 2026 (today)
**Reference:** Draft Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies — Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026 (Press Release 2026-2027/825)

**In Plain English:** The RBI wants to ban NBFCs from offering any credit product that isn't a term loan. No overdrafts, no flexi loans, no cash credit lines that refill when you pay them back. Only NBFCs authorized to issue credit cards (SBI Card, BoB Cards) are exempt.

**Impact on NBFCs:**
- Term loan definition: fixed principal, repaid in installments or bullet, and the limit cannot be restored/replenished upon repayment
- Anything not meeting all three conditions = revolving credit = banned
- Existing demand/call loan chapter deleted
- Takes effect immediately upon finalization

**Muthoot Fincorp Note:** Gold loan product is already a term loan. But the replenishment clause creates ambiguity around renewals — when a customer repays and immediately re-pledges the same gold. The draft says the sanctioned limit "cannot be restored" upon repayment, which could be interpreted to block renewals entirely. This is the single biggest operational question for gold loan NBFCs. MFL's regulatory team should consider submitting feedback on this point if they haven't already.

**How to submit:** Connect 2 Regulate portal, or email feedbackcrg@rbi.org.in with subject line "Feedback on draft Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies — Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026".

## Daily Teaching: The Revolving Credit Ban — What It Means in Detail

**The Draft:** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies — Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026.

**Core Change:** Inserted paragraph 108A: "NBFC shall only offer credit products which are in the nature of term loans and shall not offer any revolving credit products."

**Definitions Added:**
- Term loan: fixed principal, disbursed in one or more installments, repaid per predetermined schedule (installments or bullet). Once repaid, the limit cannot be restored.
- Revolving credit: any fund-based facility that doesn't meet the term loan definition.

**Deleted:** Demand/Call Loans chapter; paragraph 5(5) from Chapter II.

**SBR Impact:** Applies to ALL NBFCs regardless of layer (BL, ML, UL). No differentiation.

**Why RBI Is Doing This:** Revolving credit and flexi-loan products have grown rapidly, especially through digital lending apps. The RBI is concerned about overleveraging, where borrowers continuously draw down on the same limit without fresh assessment. By mandating term loans, each borrowing event requires a discrete sanction decision.

**Gold Loan Specifics:**
- Gold loans with bullet repayment (up to 12 months under new gold lending directions) should qualify as term loans
- Renewal/re-pledge: the critical ambiguity. If a borrower repays and re-pledges, is that a new sanction or replenishment of the old limit? The draft says "limit cannot be restored upon repayment" — language that needs clarification
- Multiple simultaneous gold loans to one borrower already flagged for stricter audit under gold lending directions

**Timeline:** Comment window closes today (28 August 2026). Amendments take effect immediately upon final notification.
