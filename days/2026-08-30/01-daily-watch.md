## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **Revolving-credit ban fight lands on RBI's table — feedback window closed Friday.** FIDC formally submitted its response on Aug 27 to the *Draft RBI (NBFC – Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026*, urging the RBI to reconsider the blanket ban on revolving credit products. FIDC says products like supply-chain finance, working-capital loans, MSME loans, vehicle dealer lines, loans against securities, personal loans and fintech-focused facilities may not fit the proposed "term loan only" definition, and warns a large share of outstanding credit could be affected — pushing smaller MSMEs and retail borrowers toward informal finance. Reuters reported the industry's plea on Aug 28. The Aug 28 comment deadline has passed; the final directions will determine whether this is a manageable compliance change or a structural hit to MSME finance models (and Bajaj Finance's flexi-loan book). [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indias-nbfc-body-asks-rbi-ease-restrictions-revolving-credit-2026-08-28/) · [KNN India](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/fidc-urges-rbi-to-reconsider-nbfc-revolving-credit-curbs-flags-msme-impact)

2. **Manappuram's Bain-era overhaul: first professional CEO named.** Manappuram Finance appointed **Ashish Singh** (head of retail liabilities, IDFC FIRST Bank; ex-Fullerton, ICICI) as MD & CEO for 5 years effective **Jan 1, 2027**; promoter **V.P. Nandakumar** — MD since incorporation in 1992 — becomes non-executive chairman. This follows Bain Capital's ₹4,385 cr investment for an 18% stake and joint control (Bain gets CEO/KMP nomination rights; promoters to hold ~28.9% on a fully diluted basis). Incumbent CEO Deepak Reddy (who resigned in July) exits Dec 31. Effectively ends the family-led era at India's #2 gold lender. [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/manappuram-ends-family-led-era-as-bain-brings-in-a-professional-ceo/articleshow/133157554.cms) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/manappuram-charts-leadership-transition-after-bain-deal-with-ashish-singh-126081201788_1.html) · [Exchange filing](https://www.manappuram.com/sites/default/files/2026-08/OUTCOMEFINALsigned.pdf)

- **Gold loans:** strongest momentum — Muthoot +48% gold AUM, Manappuram +98%, Bajaj +112%, Shriram +45% (Q1); gold above ₹1.6 lakh keeps LTV headroom wide; 15% household-gold monetisation = long runway (Jefferies). Watch: gold-price volatility (BofA/whalesbook caution) and competition from banks.
- **Vehicle finance:** largest earnings-upgrade contributor; GST cuts, CV replacement cycle and used-vehicle financing supporting growth; CV = 47% of Shriram's mix.
- **Housing:** steady affordable/mid-income disbursements; PNB Housing GNPA 0.95%, CRAR 28.3%; Aadhar Housing rating upgrade signals HFC credit-profile strength.
- **Microfinance:** still the sick segment — bank funding down ~70% since FY24; CGSMFI-2.0 closing Monday at 17% utilisation; watch for a possible scheme extension or rule tweaks post-deadline (MFIN has been pressing for a rework).
- **MSME/commercial:** recovery expected from Q3FY27 (Bajaj commentary); the revolving-credit draft is the swing factor for working-capital products.
- **Rural/agri:** El Niño/deficient monsoon is the key risk flagged for rural books (Motilal Oswal; MPC noted uneven monsoon).
