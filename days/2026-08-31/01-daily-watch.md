## 🏛️ RBI & Regulatory — Headlines Only

- **Draft NBFC (Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026 (6 Aug):** NBFCs to offer only term-loan-type products; revolving credit banned except card-issuing NBFCs. Feedback closed 28 Aug; FIDC/FISME formal objections, led by MSME working-capital concerns.
- **Concentration Risk Management Fourth Amendment (25 Aug):** Upper-Layer **IDF-NBFCs** now subject to the same large-exposure limits as NBFC-IFCs — immediate effect.
- **Responsible Business Conduct Third Amendment (6 Aug, effective 1 Jan 2027):** comprehensive recovery-agent conduct framework — agency due diligence, IIBF certification for agents, code of conduct, device-restriction norms for financed phones.
- **Loan pricing draft (12 Aug):** common pricing rules across banks/NBFCs/HFCs — max 3-month benchmark resets, spread-revision curbs, APR disclosure for ≤₹50,000 micro-loans; comments till 11 Sep; migration by 1 Apr 2029.
- **NBFC-UL list 2026-27 (6 Aug):** 17 Upper-Layer NBFCs incl. Tata Sons (de-registration pending; listing pressure mounts), Bajaj Finance, Shriram, Tata Capital, LIC Housing, Chola, Muthoot Finance, Aditya Birla Capital, HUDCO, Mahindra Finance, L&T Finance, Bajaj Housing, HDB, Piramal Finance; PNB Housing & Sammaan Capital carry over as UL.
- **CGSMFI-2.0 (govt, Jun):** scheme valid **till 31 Aug 2026** (i.e. today) or ₹20,000 cr of guarantees, whichever earlier; large NBFC-MFI/MFI cap raised ₹300 cr → **₹1,000 cr**.
- **MPC (3–5 Aug):** repo held at 6.5%; hawkish minutes; Kotak flags possible 50bps hike → 5–15bps funding-cost drag for NBFCs (25–50% of bank borrowings are repo-linked).
- Gold-loan framework effective 1 Apr 2026 (tiered LTV 85/80/75%) and Lending Against Gold & Silver Collateral Directions (Jun 2025) continue to reshape the segment; RBI clarified small-ticket (≤₹2.5 lakh) loans get lighter credit-appraisal requirements.
