## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Gold Loan Sector (Tuesday rotation)

**The fastest-growing retail asset class is now a crowded battlefield — with a price correction starting to test it.**

- **The numbers are extraordinary:** RBI data (June 2026): NBFC gold loans **₹3.41 lakh crore, +69.3% YoY** (May: +69.9%) — the fastest-growing major retail segment, even as NBFC credit growth overall slowed. Yet the NBFC share of total gold loans is **~50.3% (Mar-25)** vs 69.4% in 2020 — banks are aggressively closing the gap.
   - [ET — NBFC gold loans ₹3.41 lakh cr in June](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/nbfc-gold-loans-continue-near-70-growth-reach-rs-3-41-lakh-crore-in-june-rbi/articleshow/133050925.cms)
   - [Indian Express — gold loans lead NBFC retail credit](https://indianexpress.com/article/business/nbfc-gold-loan-growth-june-2026-rbi-credit-data-10824066/)
   - [ET — banks vs NBFCs, market nearly 50-50](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banks-narrow-nbfcs-lead-as-indias-gold-loan-market-turns-nearly-50-50/articleshow/126421874.cms)
- **New entrants are buying their way in:** Tata Capital (88.6% of Yogloans), Godrej Capital (Kanakadurga's ₹280 cr book + Indel Money co-lending), **Aditya Birla Capital** (1,000 gold branches in 3 years; 200–300 by Mar-27), Shriram Finance's aggressive expansion — while JPMorgan initiated coverage on Muthoot/Manappuram/IIFL with Overweight on 21 Aug. The market is consolidating around well-capitalised players exactly as the price cycle turns.
   - [Outlook Business — why NBFCs are scrambling for gold loans](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/gold-loans-are-exploding-why-nbfcs-are-scrambling-for-a-bigger-slice)
- **The Muthoot group angle (relevant to your desk):** Muthoot Finance Q1FY27: PAT ₹2,825 cr (+43%), AUM ₹1.92 lakh cr (+43%) — but 360 ONE flagged ~300bps NIM compression on yield pressure. **Muthoot Fincorp** (your company): Q1 PAT **₹705.48 cr (~3x YoY)**; **₹3,000 cr IPO** in SEBI review; and via the group's micro-lender, **gold co-lending with Muthoot Microfin is scaling — ₹100 cr/month disbursed, ~₹1,200 cr targeted for FY27** (portfolio ~₹350 cr → ₹500 cr). Muthoot Microfin's AA− upgrade cuts its CoF to 9.75–9.8%, helping the co-lending economics.
   - [Business Standard — Muthoot Fincorp Q1 profit ~3x to ₹705 cr](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/quarterly-results/muthoot-fincorp-q1-profit-rises-nearly-three-fold-to-705-48-crore-126081600561_1.html)

   - [Business Standard — Muthoot Microfin non-JLG push, gold loans](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/muthoot-microfin-targets-40-non-jlg-share-as-funding-costs-decline-126081700817_1.html)
- **The risk switch is on price:** gold at ₹155,430 is ~**4.6% below the 24 Aug record** (₹1.63 lakh/10g) — after a +20%+ run this fiscal. Regime rules (LTV tiering 85/80/75%, mandatory credit assessment >₹2.5 lakh, standardised collateral handling from Apr-26) are now being tested by actual price weakness. Lower LTV headroom → fewer disbursements at old margins, higher auction-cycle risk on delinquent tickets, and spreads under pressure at the margin — while banks keep undercutting rates (8–11% vs NBFC ~12–21%).
- **Bottom line for the week:** growth stays extraordinary (+69% YoY) but the market is now in a "gold-price-reversal test" phase: watch (1) daily bullion prices near ₹1.55 lakh support, (2) Q2 yield/NIM prints from Muthoot and Manappuram, (3) auction volumes, and (4) whether bank-led price competition forces NBFC gold yields down further. Today's **Augmont listing (+15.3% day one)** shows public-market appetite for the gold ecosystem remains strong even as spot corrects.
