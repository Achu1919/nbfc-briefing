## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **Muthoot Finance board approves merger of subsidiary Muthoot Money (Aug 31).** The amalgamation of the wholly-owned gold-loan subsidiary adds **1,006 branches** to Muthoot's 5,000+ network with **no fresh share issue** (Muthoot Money's share capital gets cancelled). 3. **Aadhar Housing Finance upgraded to 'IND AA+/Stable' by India Ratings (Sep 1)** — from 'IND AA/Positive'. Second agency to move it to AA+ after CARE. AUM ~₹31,400 cr (Jun-30), 628 branches in 22 states, CRAR 43.4%, RoA 4% in Q1 FY27. But the same report expects **large private banks and diversified NBFCs to gain share while Muthoot and Manappuram cede some** — the competitive battle for gold loan yields is the single biggest theme to watch through H2 FY27. *(Fortune India, Sep 1)*

## 💻 Digital & Fintech

- **UPI scale check (July 2026):** 2,365.8 crore transactions worth **₹29.87 lakh crore** across 741 live banks (PIB data) — the rails on which all of this rides.
- **CLOU (Credit Line on UPI):** still **banks + SFBs only — NBFCs remain excluded**. FIDC continues to lobby RBI for a calibrated "Financially Sound and Well-Managed" NBFC category (NLSIU white paper, Mar 2026, argues exclusion isn't statutorily compelled). Meanwhile the ecosystem is positioning: **Hero Fincorp, JUMPP, Snapmint, Stashfin, ZET** all received TPAP certifications between Dec 2025 and Mar 2026, and majors (Bajaj, Aditya Birla, Tata Pay, Navi) are building UPI apps — the market is clearly betting NBFC access opens.
- **Suryoday SFB's CLOU portfolio (with Paytm Postpaid):** 5.3 lakh sanctioned customers, ₹362 cr sanctioned limits, ₹102 cr utilized, ~90% borrowers with CIBIL >725, average ticket ~₹7,000 ("sachet credit"), 63% CMGR — evidence embedded credit is a prime-borrower product, not subprime.
- **RBI 4th Amendment Directions on Credit Facilities (Jun 23):** UPI-linked credit's prudential treatment follows the *underlying facility's* nature, not the payment channel.
- **Bajaj Finance flexi-loan overhang:** urban personal-loan growth slowed below 20% with fintech/NBFC competition; Nomura expects **Q2 FY27 AUM growth moderation** until the RBI's revolving-credit definition is finalized, with gold-loan scale-up + MSME recovery (from Q3) to re-accelerate growth; FY27 AUM growth est. raised to 25%, TP raised to ₹1,270 (ETBFSI, Aug 24).
- **Navi:** UPI volume (reported ~790M transactions/month, up from 3M two years ago) is fueling **Navi Finserv's** expansion beyond personal loans into merchant finance, LAP and loans-against-MF (Mint).
- **L&T Finance:** secured **permanent IRDAI composite corporate agent registration** — removes renewal risk on its insurance distribution channel and supports retail fee income (Sep 1-2).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive (Wednesday: Housing Finance)

**Mixed quarter, divergent strategies.** HFC AUM growth came in at just **5.6% YoY for Q1 FY27** (360 ONE) — dragged by balance transfers out of LIC Housing, which cut its FY27 guidance to 8-10% from 10-12%. But the affordable/emerging segment is where the action is:

- **PNB Housing Finance** is the sector's momentum story: **₹1 lakh crore AUM targeted for Q3 FY27** (currently ₹93,000 cr+); retaining 18-20% growth guidance; shifting mix to 45% affordable+emerging by FY27-end (from ~40%); launched **micro-housing** (ticket ₹9-20 lakh, avg ₹12 lakh, ₹100 cr book target); restarted developer finance conservatively (3% of book year one, max 10%); leverage heading from ~3.8 to ~6 in three years; CRAR 28.26%; funding at ~7.35% with repo-linked mix rising; H2 margin recovery expected as NIM has bottomed. Stock +24% YTD (best in peer group; BSE Financial Services -4% YTD), still only ~1.6x P/B. *(ET, ETBFSI, newsbizkoot)*
- **Aadhar Housing** keeps 20% AUM growth with spreads >5.5% (5.8% exit rate); ~450 branches in emerging markets vs 150 urban; LAP ~27% of AUM (ticket ₹9-10 lakh, ~350 bps risk-adjusted spread); **Blackstone's Fund A→B rollover (Feb) has removed the stake-sale overhang**. Stock -7% over 1 yr, mcap ~₹20,400 cr.
- **Bajaj Housing Finance (2nd-largest HFC):** Jefferies (Hold, TP ₹92) sees 23% AUM CAGR FY26-29 off a ₹1.5 lakh cr base (₹2.6 lakh cr by FY29), 20% EPS CAGR, RoE to 13.6%; average ticket ₹49 lakh (premium borrower focus, 83% salaried); Sambhav (affordable) unit scaling to ₹600 cr/month disbursements by FY27-end; targeting 5% share of industry originations (from 2.6%).
- **Systematix's five Buy calls** (Aug 31) argue affordable HFCs trade 11-57% below long-term averages and offer 28-48% upside — the sector's structural case: mortgage-to-GDP just ~16.6%, affordable AUM projected to ₹14.1 lakh crore by FY27.
- **Watch items:** LIC Housing's growth reset; Star Housing's fraud resolution and stake sale; SRG's HFC→NBFC-ICC conversion (would reduce HFC count); NHB's sharper oversight post-Star.
