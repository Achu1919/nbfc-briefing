## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Thursday: MSME & Commercial Lending

**NBFCs are the growth engine of MSME credit — and policy is doubling down.**

- **Scale & gap:** NBFCs account for ~**25% of commercial-credit origination volume** (~20% of value) in India's MSME credit supply (Jul–Sep 2025). Total formal MSME credit supply stood at ₹32.4 lakh crore (H1 FY25) against an addressable universe leaving a **credit gap of ~₹18.3 lakh crore**. CARE Ratings projects NBFC MSME AUM to **cross ₹5.3 lakh crore by FY26**; segment growth has run at ~14% (NBFC-led) while aggregate MSME credit growth hit 21.8% YoY (Nov 2025).
- **Policy tailwinds (Budget 2026, operational through FY26-27):** ₹10,000 crore **SME Growth Fund** + ₹2,000 crore Self-Reliant India Fund top-up; **CGTMSE guarantee limit doubled to ₹10 crore** (₹1.5 lakh crore incremental credit over 5 years estimated; enhanced cover up to ₹20 cr for start-ups/exporters); TReDS made mandatory for CPSE purchases from MSMEs; GeM–TReDS integration; MSME credit cards up to ₹5 lakh (10 lakh first-time borrowers); investment/turnover thresholds raised 2.5x.
- **Co-lending:** RBI's revised co-lending framework (in force Jan 2026) governs bank–NBFC joint MSME lending; a parliamentary panel (Mar 2026) has recommended broadening these pacts; NBFC-fintech co-lending continues to expand product reach.
- **Industry asks (carried from Budget season):** NBFCs — including Muthoot FinCorp CEO Shaji Varghese — have pressed for **SARFAESI threshold harmonisation** (₹20 lakh → ₹1 lakh for NBFCs, matching banks/HFCs) to shorten recovery cycles; plus a dedicated refinance window for NBFCs (NHB-style).
- **Bottom line for Muthoot:** business/gold-secured MSME lending sits squarely in the fastest-recovering credit segment; funding costs are falling (Microfin at ~10%, AA- rates) while demand from rural/semi-urban MSMEs remains structurally under-served — the runway argument stays intact even as competitive intensity from banks and co-lending grows.
