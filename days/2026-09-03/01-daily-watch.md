## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **JCR upgrades India's sovereign rating to 'A-' from 'BBB+' (Sep 2) — India's first 'A'-category rating in 35+ years.** Japan Credit Rating Agency raised both foreign- and local-currency long-term issuer ratings, with **stable outlook**, and lifted the country ceiling to 'A'. JCR explicitly cited the **improvement in NBFC sector asset quality and capital adequacy** alongside banks (GNPA 1.8% at Mar-26), the IBC, and RBI supervision. Central fiscal deficit 4.4% of GDP (FY26); JCR sees growth above 6% in FY27. **Why it matters:** the first "A" in three decades improves India's external funding optics — supportive for NBFC foreign-currency borrowing costs (see Capri Global) at a time when shadow lenders are racing to lock in rates. NK Singh called it recognition of "deep structural reforms." *(TOI; New Indian Express; Moneycontrol)*
2. **Markets: third straight losing session — but institutions bought the dip.** Sensex closed **76,570.35 (-373.93 / -0.49%)**, Nifty **23,914.45 (-141.35 / -0.59%)** — below 24,000 after an intraday low of 23,786.80 — as US-Iran strikes, crude near **$95/bbl** and a global bond rout (India 10-yr ~**6.99%**) hit sentiment. **Nifty Financial Services fell 0.84% to ~25,786.70**; Bank Nifty 57,172 (-0.41%); auto (-2.04%) and IT (-1.54%) led sectoral losses. **Yet FIIs net bought ₹6,688 cr and DIIs ₹2,813 cr in cash on Sep 2** (combined ~₹9,500 cr) — the selloff is geopolitical/rate-driven, not institutional exit. August: FIIs net sold ₹7,532 cr; DIIs bought ₹58,268 cr. *(India Today; DD News; Moneycontrol; Trendlyne; Lemonn)*
3. **Capri Global Capital closes debut $300 million dollar bond — 2.3x oversubscribed (Sep 2).** Senior secured notes due 2029 at **7.55%** coupon; order book >$700 mn across 64 accounts (US 49%, Asia 39%, EMEA 12%; asset managers 91%). Bookrunners: Barclays, Citi, Deutsche Bank, Emirates NBD, UBS. Expected ratings: **Ba3 (Moody's), BB- (Fitch), CareEdge BB-/Positive** (IFSC). Under the $1 bn GMTN programme; listed on India INX & NSE IFSC; settlement Sep 9. MD Rajesh Sharma: diversifies funding across domestic and global pools. AUM >₹40,000 cr (Jun-30). **Read-through:** mid-size NBFCs can still access dollar markets at workable spreads even as bank supply pauses — a key funding signal for the sector. *(BusinessLine; CNBC-TV18; scanx.trade)*
4. **Jefferies: 4 NBFC picks to keep outperforming Nifty and banks (Sep 2).** **Bajaj Finance TP ₹1,280 (~19% upside, top pick), Cholamandalam TP ₹1,860 (~20%), Aditya Birla Capital TP ₹475 (~15%), Shriram Finance TP ₹1,210 (~8%)** — all Buy. Thesis: NBFC outperformance YTD has been driven by EPS/BVPS upgrades, not multiple expansion (P/Bs are *down* YTD for most large NBFCs); asset-quality visibility is stronger, unsecured vintages from 2024-25 are containing credit costs, NIMs range-bound as rate-hike odds recede, and strong FCNR(B) inflows help funding. Jefferies sees **23-28% EPS CAGR for Bajaj Finance, ABC and Capri Global over FY26-28**. Diversified lenders (Bajaj, ABC) preferred for lower rural/monsoon-El Niño risk. *(ET; CNBC-TV18; NDTV Profit)*
5. **L&T Finance goes all-in on gold: 500 new gold branches/year → 2,500-3,000 network (Sep 2).** COO Raju Dodti: 343 gold branches now → **~800 by Mar-27**, then ≥500 a year for 4-5 years (target 2,500-3,000). Gold book **₹3,829 cr in Q1 FY27 (+182% YoY)** vs total book ₹1.29 lakh cr; ambitions: gold >10% of loan book by 2031 and secured mix to 60:40. **Your Muthoot radar:** the competitive attack on gold lending is broadening — banks (+88% July) and now L&T Finance's branch blitz — even as Motilal Oswal flags margin pressure for the pure-play gold NBFCs (see Trends). *(Fortune India; TOI; ET)*

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — MSME & Commercial Lending (Thursday focus)

**Demand is cooling but credit is booming — a divergence to watch for MSME portfolios.**

- **HSBC India Manufacturing PMI: 52.8 in August — a five-year low** (down from 53.5 in July; third straight monthly decline). New orders and output weakened; **factory employment fell for the first time in 30 months**; input-cost pressures eased. Still in expansion zone (above 50), but momentum is sliding — the key demand-side risk for MSME lending through H2 FY27. *(ET; Moneycontrol; Hindu BusinessLine)*
- **Yet credit offtake is accelerating:** bank credit to industry grew **20% YoY in July** vs just 6.5% a year ago — engineering, gems & jewellery, petroleum and vehicles led; agriculture +17%. July's sectoral deployment data also showed **gold-loan growth moderating** after the new LTV/rules regime. *(Rediff; Business Standard; RBI)*
- **NBFCs are the fastest-growing MSME lenders:** NBFC MSME AUM crossed ~**₹5.3 lakh cr by FY26**, compounding at **32% CAGR over FY21-24** versus 20.9% for private banks and 10.4% for PSBs (CareEdge). *(CareEdge)*
- **Structure shift:** SIDBI's MSME Pulse (July 2026) shows commercial credit **migrating from corporate entities to individual entrepreneurs** — more granular, ticket-size-smaller books where NBFC distribution muscle (branches + co-lending) matters most. *(SIDBI)*
- **Company-level signals:**
  - **Ujjivan SFB** — MSME business tracking FY27 guidance; new MSME head hired as bank pivots to 65-70% secured mix.
  - **Cholamandalam** — CSEL/MSME segments "underwent consolidation and portfolio re-calibration last year"; Jefferies sees rebound driving 21% AUM CAGR FY26-28 and 28% EPS CAGR.
  - **Capri Global** (MSME/construction finance core) — $300 mn dollar raise diversifies funding for growth.
  - **Protium-Clix** — consolidation in the mid-size commercial lending tier (₹1,900-2,200 cr likely valuation).
- **Funding math:** with rupee-bond supply surging (₹10,000 cr+ pipeline) and FCNR(B) at record $127 bn, MSME lenders' cost of funds is stabilising; Jefferies says rate-hike odds have receded near-term, protecting NIMs.
- **Bottom line for the quarter:** credit demand (industry +20%) is outrunning activity data (PMI 52.8) — watch October Q2 results for whether MSME disbursement growth converts into NPAs or earnings upgrades; Jefferies is betting on the latter.
