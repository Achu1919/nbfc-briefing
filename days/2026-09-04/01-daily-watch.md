## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Gold is sliding — direct pressure on the gold-loan engine.**
International gold fell for a **third straight session** and is now ~**22% below its record high** (Hindustan Times/Reuters outlook, Sep 3), even as US-Iran strikes pushed Brent to ~$95–96. Domestic 24K gold in Delhi today (Sep 4) is **₹1,50,570 per 10g (+₹260)**, down roughly **8% from the ~₹1.63 lakh late-August peak**. This matters for the gold-loan sector because LTV headroom shrinks as collateral values fall: a research note (The Signal, Aug 29) already flagged that a ~3% drop breached LTV buffers for borrowers who had borrowed at maximum 75% LTV — expect slower top-up/valuation-driven growth and watch auction volumes. For context on how hot this book ran: Muthoot Finance's consolidated gold book grew **44% YoY to ~₹1,75,000 cr** in Q1FY27, and Manappuram's gold assets jumped **98% YoY to ₹57,006 cr (82% of its book)**. Gold stocks gave back part of their August rally (Muthoot -2.45% on Sep 1; Manappuram FY27 gain still +51% vs Muthoot's ~+2%). **Most relevant to Muthoot Fincorp's own book (~99% gold): watch gold-price levels, LTV policy and competitor auction behaviour this quarter.**

**2. RBI likely to soften its proposed blanket ban on NBFC revolving credit.**
DG Shirish Chandra Murmu met NBFC CEOs and FIDC after the Aug 6 draft circular (feedback deadline Aug 28) drew ~**350 responses**; sources say RBI now leans toward a **partial restriction rather than a blanket ban** — it initially thought only 3–4 NBFCs would be hit, but feedback showed a much wider footprint. Supply-chain finance exemption is the industry's biggest ask; final guidelines are expected in **~2 months**. (Regulatory depth → NBFC Guru.)

**3. ARCIL — India's first standalone ARC listing — opens September 9.**
Price band **₹132–139**, OFS of 5.27 cr shares by Avenue India Resurgence, SBI, Lathe Investment and Federal Bank, raising up to **₹733 cr**; listing ~Sep 17. MD Phanindranath Kakarla (Sep 3): retail stressed assets will outpace corporate growth; ARCIL is adding a **collections-as-a-service** vertical for early-stage stress (SMA accounts) and has already bought a small stressed microfinance pool from Ujjivan SFB.

**4. RBI DG's message at the 7th CII NBFC & HFC Summit (Mumbai, Sep 3): "Growth must never come at the cost of underwriting standards."** Five pillars: governance & culture, liquidity, asset quality, customer protection, digital/cyber. NBFC credit is now **>16.7% of GDP** (vs 15.9% a year ago) and **~27% of scheduled commercial bank credit** (vs 26%). Murmu pushed securitisation to evolve into *genuine risk transfer + capital release*, and urged funding diversification + a deeper corporate bond market. Hero Fincorp CEO Abhimanyu Munjal separately pitched deeper PTC markets, ECB norm review and GIFT City access.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive (Friday) — Auto Finance & EV Lending

- **August sales: record momentum in 2Ws, led by EVs.** TVS Motor posted its **highest-ever monthly total (6,16,540 units, +21% YoY)** and **overtook Hero and Honda as India's top 2W seller in August 2026**; EVs were the rocket — **59,453 e-2W units, +137% YoY**. The broader Nifty Auto fell ~2% on Sep 2 on mixed August OEM updates, so financiers should expect segment divergence (2W/EV strong; PV/CV mixed).
- **Policy tailwind for EV financing:** the **PM E-DRIVE e-2W incentive is extended to 31 Mar 2028**, but the rate is **halved to ₹2,500/kWh** (from ₹5,000) with a ₹2,767 cr e-2W outlay — demand incentive supports volumes while financiers absorb more residual-value risk on cheaper subsidies.
- **Shriram Finance's MUFG playbook (Sep 1):** post-MUFG investment, Shriram is courting **Japanese OEM ecosystems — Suzuki, Toyota, Honda, Yamaha dealers, stockists and component suppliers** (1,490 Japanese manufacturers in India); new-vehicle financing mix has swung from ~90% used to **80:20 used:new**; rolling out supply-chain, inventory and dealer financing from **Oct 1**; +100 branches and 2,000–3,000 hires this year. Gold loans to double to 5% of portfolio. Explicitly staying an NBFC (no bank licence).
- **Asset-quality watch in vehicle finance:** Chola's vehicle finance remains the largest (56% of disbursements, CV traction) but Nomura flags VF GS3 of 4.05% — the highest Q1 print since COVID; Shriram's ICRA AAA rationale leans on its pre-owned CV leadership. As fuel costs spike on Iran-driven crude, transport-operator cash flows are the key monitorable for CV lenders.
