## Regulatory Developments

### 1. [ADVISORY] DG Murmu keynote at 7th CII NBFC & HFC Summit (Sep 3)

**What:** RBI Deputy Governor Shirish Chandra Murmu delivered the keynote at the 7th CII NBFC & HFC National Summit in Mumbai, laying out five supervisory priorities for the sector.

**The five priorities:**
1. Governance
2. Liquidity
3. Asset quality
4. Customer conduct
5. Cyber resilience

**Key points:**
- NBFC sector credit growth at 16.7% — robust, but Murmu warned growth without underwriting discipline creates problems
- Urged NBFCs to diversify funding — flagged reliance on short-term wholesale funding as a vulnerability
- Said RBI will work with market participants to build a deeper corporate bond market (signal for longer-tenor borrowing)
- Actively encouraged NBFCs to use securitisation as a risk-transfer and capital-freeing tool — notable shift in RBI tone
- Review of reforms from 2019 Liquidity Risk Management framework to November 2025 NBFC regulation consolidation

**MFL impact:** Direct nudge on treasury tenor mix. Gold loan portfolio alignment with June 2025 directions (effective April 2026) makes securitisation more feasible.

**Source:** RBI Speeches (https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_SpeechesView.aspx?Id=1575), published Sep 3, 2026.

### 2. [CLARIFICATION] Revolving credit ban may soften (Sep 3)

**What:** RBI DG Murmu met FIDC and NBFC CEOs to discuss the August 6 draft proposal banning revolving credit. Three hundred and fifty feedbacks received. RBI appears to be moving toward partial restriction rather than blanket ban.

**Key details:**
- Feedback closed August 28; 350 responses received
- FIDC submission (August 27) flagged impact on supply chain finance, MSME working capital, loans against securities, TReDS factoring
- FIDC proposed permitting limited redraw within a declining contractual ceiling
- Industry sources say final guidelines could take another two months (~early November)
- RBI reportedly acknowledged the draft would hit more entities than the three or four originally estimated

**MFL impact:** Low direct impact (gold loans aren't revolving credit). Watch for any group entity or MSME lending products that could be caught by the negative definition.

**Source:** ETBFSI (Sep 3, 2026), Business Standard (Sep 3, 2026).
