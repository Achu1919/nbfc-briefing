## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **NSE IPO may get SEBI approval as early as next week — NBFC stock fireworks follow.** SEBI Chairperson Tuhin Kanta Pandey said the regulator is "close to approving" NSE's DRHP. Reports on Friday said approval could come as early as next week. This directly boosted **IFCI**, which surged 8.4% to a record high of **₹103.95** on Sept 4 (close +5%) — IFCI owns >50% of Stock Holding Corporation of India (SHCIL), which holds >4% of NSE. **New India Assurance jumped 17%** on the same NSE-stake logic. **BSE rose ~9% over two sessions** (CAS update + its confirmed Sept 30 entry into Nifty 50). Why it matters: any NSE listing rerates every NBFC with NSE exposure, and the IPO itself will be India's largest-ever — watch PSU-capital-market NBFCs (IFCI, NIA) into next week.
2. **RBI Deputy Governor S C Murmu's blunt message at the 7th CII NBFC & HFC Summit (Sept 3): "Growth must never come at the cost of underwriting standards."** Key data points from the speech: NBFC credit is now **16.7% of nominal GDP** (up from 15.9% a year ago) and **~27% of scheduled commercial bank credit** (up from 26%). He laid out five pillars — governance & culture, liquidity management, asset quality, customer protection, digital/cyber resilience — and pushed securitisation to become a "genuine way to transfer risk and free up capital," not just a liquidity tool. With unsecured retail lending growing fast, this is the clearest signal yet that the RBI is watching credit quality in the current growth cycle — directly relevant to collections/underwriting teams.
3. **RBI may soften its proposed ban on NBFC revolving credit — blanket ban likely to become partial.** After DG Murmu met NBFC CEOs and FIDC (RBI has received ~350 industry responses; 30–40 lenders replied via FIDC), sources say the RBI now believes the draft's impact would hit "a much larger section of the industry" than the 3–4 NBFCs initially envisaged, and may move to a partial restriction with supply-chain-finance carve-outs. Final guidelines are expected in ~2 months. Affects Bajaj Finance/Paytm-style revolving products and digital lending models — big for fintech-NBFC convergence.

## 💻 Digital & Fintech

- **Banks finally scale Credit Line on UPI (CLOU):** HDFC Bank and Axis Bank are preparing for wide implementation of CLOU after an NPCI directive (Sept 1) — small-ticket digital credit embedded in UPI payments; fintech partners will power acquisition. NBFCs remain excluded from CLOU issuance (banks/SFBs only) — but it raises the competitive bar for NBFC consumer credit.
- **RBI's June directions on credit-on-UPI** (prudential treatment follows the underlying loan, not the channel) remain the guardrail the whole BNPL/CLOU ecosystem operates under.
- **Revolving-credit rethink (above)** is the single biggest fintech-NBFC regulatory variable right now — Paytm-scale revolving credit products and supply-chain finance are at stake.
- Context: Kiwi/YES Bank's CLOU ("Kiwi Postpaid") saw ~95% of eligible applicants approved within 2 hours, half of users new-to-credit-card, 50% from Tier II/III; Suryoday/Paytm CLOU crossed ₹362 cr sanctioned limits; CRED (interim CEO Miten Sampat) reporting 15–20% CSAT gains from AI. UPI processed 23.68 bn transactions (₹29.8 lakh cr) in July.

## 📉 NPA & Asset Quality

- **Non-banks now drive ARC purchases — retail stress is the theme.** Security receipts issued against retail loans rose to **35% of total SRs in FY26 vs 25% in FY25**; ARC industry AUM slipped 1.12% YoY to **₹1.33 lakh crore** (March 2026). The Association of ARCs' Hari Hara Mishra says NBFC-originated SR acquisitions "could exceed" banks'; JM Financial ARC's Srinivasan Viswanathan cites NBFCs' flexible structuring and faster decisions; Areion's Suresh Khatanhar flags stricter asset-quality recognition rules pushing HFCs/NBFCs to sell.
- **The stress math (from ARCIL RHP):** retail stressed assets across banks+NBFCs grew at **12.3% CAGR FY20–FY26** (banks 13.5%, NBFCs 9.6%); MSME stress rose from ₹6.8 lakh cr (FY22) to **₹9.2 lakh cr (FY26)** at 7.6% CAGR; MSME credit outstanding ₹82.2 lakh cr (March 2026), MSME NPAs ~₹1.9 lakh cr.
- **ARCs are industrialising retail recovery:** ARCIL adding AI/ML recovery prediction, collections-as-a-service for SMA-1/SMA-2 accounts; it already manages ~3.5 mn NPA customers. Crisil sees ARC cumulative recovery rates reaching ~90% next fiscal (road/realty/power-led), but also warns securitisation of NPAs could disrupt the ARC business model.
- **Microfinance:** MFIN Micrometer (Q1 FY27, released Sept 1–2): portfolio **-6.9% YoY but +1.1% QoQ — first growth after seven quarters of slowdown**; disbursements jumped **+43% YoY to ₹61,718 crore** (up 8.9% QoQ per MFIN); asset quality improving; portfolio contraction partly due to banks reclassifying MFI exposure. Banks are pivoting to lending directly to MFI entities vs individuals.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive (Saturday): Infrastructure & Project Finance

- **The big one — TPG/GIC/ICICI's Aseem takeover (CCI cleared Sept 1):** a private-sector $1 bn green-debt platform is being built around a captive NBFC-IFC with a ₹18–20k cr book. This is the largest NBFC-IFC ownership change since IIFL/others — expect aggressive growth in renewables and transmission financing, plus a potential funding-cost war on AAA infra credits.
- **NaBFID pipeline:** ₹20,000 cr zero-coupon bond approval (CBDT), earlier NIP mandate, and SRO-for-infra-financiers work (NaBFID MD Rajkiran Rai G: "we got all the approvals"; ~15 founding members across banks+NBFCs) — the SRO route means infra lenders get a collective voice with regulators.
- **IIFCL:** raised **$200 mn in two ECB tranches** under its $1.8 bn ECB programme (MD Rohit Rishi); plans ~$1 bn long-tenor (up to 15-yr, MIGA-guaranteed) borrowings.
- **Constl (Space World Group) secured ~$90 mn** from a NaBFID-led consortium for greenfield fibre networks along AI corridors — early proof of "digital infrastructure" as an investable infra-finance asset class.
- **Funding environment:** RBI's earlier decision (June) to lift Upper-Layer NBFC-IFC group exposure limits to 45% of capital and the large-exposure harmonisation for Upper-Layer IDF-NBFCs support concentrated project lending. Rating agencies (Crisil/ICRA/CareEdge) say infrastructure, roads, renewables and construction led ~half of all upgrades in H1 FY27 — infra book quality is the strongest pocket of corporate credit right now.
- **Public infra financiers:** PFC Q1 PAT ₹7,012 cr; IRFC "IRFC 2.0" diversification targets; IFCI's SHCIL/NSE re-rating story; REC exits Nifty 100 (index flows may pressure it short-term).
- **Watch:** Mudra/Kisan Credit Card digitisation and ULI/AA rails (Murmu's summit pitch) are the demand-side boosters for infra-linked MSME lending.
