#### 3. [NEW] Recovery Agent Conduct — Comprehensive Directions

**What:** RBI issued new comprehensive directions on how NBFCs must engage recovery agents.
**Issuer:** RBI | **Date:** Aug 6, 2026 | **Effective:** Jan 1, 2027
**Reference:** RBI (NBFC – Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026
**Source:** [RBI Notification 13672](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13672&Mode=0)

**In Plain English:** RBI has significantly tightened rules for recovery agents engaged by NBFCs. Key requirements: IIBF certification mandatory for agents, Board-approved recovery policy, recovery calls only between 08:00 and 19:00 hours, call recording mandatory (retain 6 months), no contact with borrower's employer, family, or neighbours unless borrower is unreachable, no recovery from military personnel without MoD permission, mandatory grievance redressal for recovery-related complaints.

**Impact on NBFCs:**
- **Compliance cost:** Must verify IIBF certification of ALL recovery agents (current and new)
- **Call recording:** System investment needed — record all recovery calls, store for 6 months
- **Time restrictions:** Recovery operations limited to 8 AM – 7 PM
- **Board policy:** Recovery policy must be formally approved by Board, not just management
- **Training:** All recovery staff (including outsourced) need training on new norms

**Muthoot Fincorp Note:** 🔶 **MEDIUM IMPACT.** MFL has a large recovery network, especially for gold loan defaults. Key actions: (1) Audit all outsourced recovery agents for IIBF certification, (2) Implement call recording system for recovery calls, (3) Update recovery timing windows in branch systems, (4) Board to approve updated recovery policy before Jan 2027. Gold loan recovery (auction of gold) is generally lower-risk than unsecured recovery, but the call recording and timing rules still apply to any telephonic follow-up.

## 🎓 Daily Teaching Session — Day 20 of 30

### Asset Classification & Restructuring for NBFCs

---

**What is it?**

Asset classification is RBI's system for categorising loans based on how likely they are to be repaid. When a borrower stops paying, the loan moves from "Standard" to progressively worse categories. Each category triggers higher provisioning (money set aside from profits to cover potential losses). Restructuring is when an NBFC modifies loan terms (extended tenure, reduced EMI, interest reduction) for a borrower in financial distress — RBI allows this but with strict conditions.

Think of it like a hospital triage system: Standard patients (healthy loans) get basic care; Sub-standard patients (90+ days overdue) need more attention; Doubtful patients (long-term default) might not survive; Loss patients (unrecoverable) are written off.

---

**The Technical Details**

#### Asset Classification Categories

| Category | When Triggered | What It Means | Duration Rule |
|----------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Standard (Normal)** | 0–89 Days Past Due (DPD) | Borrower is current or <90 days late | Stays standard until 90 DPD |
| **Sub-Standard** | ≥90 DPD | Borrower is significantly behind | Remains sub-standard for 12 months after becoming current |
| **Doubtful-1 (D1)** | Sub-standard for ≥12 months | High probability of loss | Remains D1 until 12 months after becoming current |
| **Doubtful-2 (D2)** | D1 for ≥12 months | Very high probability of loss | — |
| **Doubtful-3 (D3)** | D2 for ≥12 months | Almost certain loss | — |
| **Loss** | Uncollectible | Written off or provisioned 100% | — |

**Critical rule for NBFCs (different from banks):** Banks use an "aging" approach (30/60/90 days for SMA, then NPA). NBFCs jump straight to NPA at 90 days. There is NO sub-classification between SMA-0, SMA-1, SMA-2 for NBFC provisioning purposes — the 90-day threshold is the single trigger.

**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE: 2024-10-01] — RBI aligned NBFC-ML provisioning with banks from Oct 2024. NBFC-BL follows simplified norms.

**Source:** RBI Master Direction – IRAC Directions, 2024-25 (RBI/2024-25/12, DoR.MCS.REC.No.60/31.01.001/2024-25)

#### Income Recognition Rules

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Standard assets** | Interest recognised on **accrual basis** (as earned, even if not yet received) |
| **NPA assets** | Interest recognised only on **realisation basis** (only when cash actually arrives) |
| **Interest already accrued on NPA** | Must be **reversed** — taken out of income |
| **Processing fees** | Recognised on receipt basis |
| **Penal charges** | Cannot be treated as income if account is NPA |

**FP&A impact:** When a loan becomes NPA, you must reverse all accrued interest income from that account. This is a P&L hit — not just provisioning, but also revenue reduction. The combined impact (provisioning charge + interest reversal) can be very significant on quarterly results.

#### Provisioning Rates (NBFC-ML)

| Asset Category | Secured | Unsecured |
|---------------|---------|-----------|
| **Standard** | **0.40%** | **1.00%** |
| **Sub-Standard** | **15%** | **25%** |
| **Doubtful-1** | **25%** | **40%** |
| **Doubtful-2** | **40%** | **70%** |
| **Doubtful-3** | **70%** | **100%** |
| **Loss** | **100%** | **100%** |

**Gold Loan Specific Provisioning:**
| LTV at Sanction | Standard Provision |
|----------------|-------------------|
| ≤75% | 0.40% (normal) |
| >75% | 1.00% (higher risk) |
| NPA | 15% (secured) / 25% (unsecured) |

**Note:** If gold price falls below outstanding loan amount, the excess is treated as unsecured and provisioned at 25%.

#### Restructuring Framework

**What is restructuring?**
When a borrower is in financial difficulty but the NBFC believes the borrower can repay with modified terms, the NBFC can restructure the loan. RBI allows this but treats restructured accounts as a risk indicator.

**Key rules:**
1. **Restructured = NPA:** Any restructured account is automatically classified as NPA, regardless of current payment status
2. **Fresh classification:** After 12 months of satisfactory payments post-restructuring, the account can be upgraded to Standard
3. **Provisioning:** Restructured standard assets require additional provisioning of 5% over and above normal provisioning
4. **Disclosure:** Restructured loans must be disclosed separately in financial statements
5. **One-time restructuring (OTR):** Available during exceptional circumstances (like COVID) — Board-approved, RBI notification required
6. **Pre-packaged Insolvency (PIRP):** Available for MSMEs with defaults up to ₹1 crore — a structured alternative to full IBC

**Restructuring Categories:**
| Type | Description | Provisioning |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Restructured Standard** | Modified terms, account still current | Normal provision + 5% additional |
| **Restructured Sub-Standard** | Modified terms, account is NPA | 15% (secured) + additional 5% |
| **Restructured Doubtful** | Modified terms, long-standing NPA | As per doubtful category + additional 5% |

**SNFA (Special Mention Financial Assets) — New from Oct 2024:**
RBI introduced a new category under the Stressed Asset Resolution framework. An SNFA is a stressed asset (incipient stress) that hasn't yet become NPA. NBFCs must have a Board-approved policy for early identification and resolution of SNFAs. Legacy SNFAs (as on Sep 30, 2026) must comply by Sep 30, 2027.

---

**Why It Matters**

1. **P&L impact:** NPA classification triggers interest reversal AND provisioning charge — a double hit to profitability
2. **Capital impact:** Provisions reduce Net Worth, which affects CRAR — large NPA spikes can breach CRAR thresholds
3. **RBI supervision:** NPA trends are a key supervisory indicator — worsening asset quality attracts increased scrutiny (including PCA placement)
4. **Investor perception:** High NPA ratios make it harder to raise capital and increase borrowing costs
5. **MFI/gold loan specificity:** Gold loans are collateral-protected so NPA rates are typically very low (<2%), but MFI loans have higher default risk — NPA management is critical for MFI portfolio

---

| Parameter | Value | Citation |
|-----------|-------|----------|
