# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Monday, 07 September 2026

*Coverage: weekend + Friday close (04 Sep) + overnight breaking news. Sector focus (Monday): Microfinance & SHG lending.*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. RBI may soften its proposed blanket ban on NBFC revolving credit** — RBI Deputy Governor S C Murmu met FIDC and NBFC CEOs after the industry submitted ~350 responses to the draft *NBFC Credit Facilities Amendment Directions, 2026* (which proposed NBFCs may offer only term loans). Sources say RBI initially saw the impact as limited to 3-4 NBFCs, but feedback showed far wider industry impact — a **partial restriction rather than a blanket ban** is now being considered, with supply-chain finance an exemption focus (SCF loans are GST-verified and paid directly to suppliers). Final guidelines are expected in ~2 months; CII will consolidate industry suggestions to the RBI. FISME has separately warned the ban could choke MSME working-capital finance. *Why it matters: this directly shapes every NBFC's product shelf — BNPL limits, cash credit, overdrafts and working-capital lines.* [1,2,3]

**2. FCNR(B) liquidity windfall is the big market story — NBFCs among the biggest beneficiaries** — Banks mobilised ~$130 billion (final tally may be higher; RBI's last official count: $127.22 bn as on Aug 31) in the special FCNR(B) window that closed Aug 31. Private banks took $61 bn (46.9%), PSBs $37 bn (28.5%), foreign banks $32 bn (24.6%). System liquidity surplus hit a **record ₹10.3 lakh crore** on Sep 3; the RBI today (Sep 7) runs a **30-day VRRR of ₹7 lakh crore** to absorb it. Jefferies says NBFC stocks have gained **15% in 3 months** vs Nifty 50's 2%, and expects the liquidity to "keep rates lower" ahead of the festive season; CD rates are already falling and large banks saved 25-60 bps on incremental deposit costs in August. Motilal Oswal has raised FY27 system credit growth to **14.3% (upside 15.5-16%)**. [4,5]

**3. RBI DG's five priorities for NBFCs/HFCs: asset quality must not be sacrificed for growth** — At the 7th CII NBFC & HFC Summit (Sep 3-4, Mumbai), S C Murmu laid out governance, liquidity management, asset quality, customer protection, and cyber resilience as the sector's priorities. "Growth must never come at the cost of underwriting standards" — he urged rigorous stress testing, early-warning systems, dynamic provisioning and AI/ML for borrower-stress detection, and pushed securitisation to evolve from a liquidity tool into genuine risk transfer. He noted NBFC credit is now **16.7% of GDP** (15.9% a year ago) and **~27% of scheduled commercial bank credit** (26%). Hero Fincorp CEO Abhimanyu Munjal used the same platform to pitch deeper bond/PTC markets, ECB-norm review and GIFT City access. [6,7,8]

**4. Rate-hike cycle chatter grows — funding-cost risk for H2 FY27** — Repo is at 5.25% (held unanimously in August; neutral stance), but July CPI ran 4.45% (food 5.52%) and RBI's FY27 forecast is 5.0% peaking at 5.9% in Q3. Union Bank's report (Sep 4) expects 2-3 hikes of 25 bps starting **December 2026** (repo to 5.75-6%), with liquidity absorption (VRRR, I-CRR) before the October MPC; UOB, BofA and MUFG similarly see a ~50 bps tightening cycle starting December; Bandhan AMC sees repo at 6% by April 2027. A hike would raise NBFC funding costs just as the liquidity flush fades. [9,10]

**5. ARCIL IPO — first ARC listing, opens Sep 9** — Asset Reconstruction Company (India) fixed a **₹132-139 price band** for its ₹733 crore IPO (entirely OFS of 5.27 cr shares, no fresh issue). Anchor bidding Sep 8, subscription Sep 9-11, listing Sep 17. Sellers: Avenue India Resurgence (69.73%), SBI (19.95%), GIC-backed Lathe Investment (5%), Karnataka Bank, South Indian & Federal Banks; SBI and Federal Bank reportedly sit on ~281-292% gains on their ARCIL holdings. FY26: income +26%, PAT +15%. GMP currently flat (~0), implying no listing pop expected. [11,12]

**6. CBI books Subhash Chandra over alleged ₹1,322 crore loss to LIC Housing Finance** — FIR registered Aug 31 on LICHFL's complaint: two 2018 facilities (₹500 cr to Vasant Sagar/Pan India Infra; ₹480 cr rental-securitisation facility to Digital Subscriber/Spirit Infrapower) were sanctioned against net-worth certificates claiming ~₹59,113 cr (Mar-17) and ₹40,562 cr (Jul-18); Chandra later told insolvency proceedings his net worth was only ₹31.79 cr in 2024. Outstanding: ₹570.50 cr + ₹507.25 cr. Investigation ongoing; FIR ≠ guilt. [13]

- **Revolving-credit draft vs digital credit:** the proposed term-loans-only rule would cap BNPL-style revolving products and credit-line products offered by NBFCs — the single biggest fintech-lending regulatory watch item right now; ~350 responses submitted. [1,2]

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Microfinance & SHG Lending (Monday)

**The sector has turned the corner — the numbers are now consistent across three sources (MFIN, Equifax-SIDBI, CareEdge):**

- **Growth is back:** industry portfolio ₹3.29 lakh cr (MFIN, Jun-30) — first QoQ growth after seven quarters of decline; Q1 FY27 disbursements ₹61,718 cr (+8.9% YoY), a strong print for a seasonally slack quarter. Equifax-SIDBI's March edition showed ₹78,938 cr disbursed in JFM'26, the highest since JFM'23, and +3% QoQ portfolio growth — the first since June 2024. [28,48]
- **Quality collapsed then rebounded:** 30+ DPD fell from 6.64% (Mar-25) to 2.35% (Mar-26) on the Equifax-SIDBI coverage; PAR 31-180 at 1.6% (Jun-26, MFIN) vs 5.6% a year ago; early buckets (PAR 1-30) at 0.5%. Note: write-offs explain part of the improvement — CareEdge's sample shows write-offs + ARC sales exceeded **17% of the March-2024 book** during FY25-26. Crisil expects microfinance GNPAs (SFB segment) to fall to 3.8-4% by Mar-27 from a 7.6% FY26 peak. [28,36,48]
- **Who is leading:** NBFC-MFIs — share up to ~44% (Jun-26) from 39% (Mar-24), funded by a **+91% YoY jump in bank/wholesale funding**; banks themselves shrank their MFI books 28.5% YoY (partly reclassification). CareEdge expects NBFC-MFI AUM +15% in FY27 and RoTA ~2.4% (from 0.5% FY26), with credit costs normalising to ~3.5% of ATA from 5.4%. Smaller MFIs remain funding-constrained — recovery is "uneven". [28,36]
- **Structural shift — credit deepening, not expansion:** existing-to-credit borrowers are 80% of originations (vs 67% in 2023); ~21 mn borrowers dropped out of the system; delinquencies for 5+ lender borrowers fell from 30.6% to 11.7% (Sep-25→Mar-26). Positive graduation signal: in Equifax's 20-lakh sample, **~48% of MFI borrowers now hold mainstream retail products** (gold, housing, vehicle, unsecured business and personal loans) — ₹14,639 cr of retail outstanding. [48]
- **Monsoon & policy tailwinds:** better-than-anticipated monsoon (only Rajasthan weak among top-10 states) is supporting rural cash flows; MFIN has asked RBI to review the **₹3 lakh household-income cap** (income creep is excluding eligible borrowers, and higher ticket sizes to fewer borrowers is concentrating the book). [28]
- **SHG angle:** no standalone SHG-lending news this week; SIDBI (the SHG/JLG support institution) published its Microfinance Pulse flagging "green shoots," and ARCIL's MFI-portfolio pilot (from Ujjivan SFB) suggests capital is beginning to flow back into stressed-MFI resolution. [48,12]
- **Watch items:** (1) income-cap review outcome, (2) smaller-MFI funding gap, (3) whether the Q1 growth sustains into the festive season given late-monsoon caution, (4) rate-hike risk on borrowing costs, (5) the revolving-credit draft — MFI working-capital lines could be caught. [1,28,36]
