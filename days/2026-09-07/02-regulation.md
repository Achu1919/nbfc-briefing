# NBFC Guru — Daily Regulatory Report
**Date:** Monday, September 7, 2026

## No new RBI/SEBI circulars or regulatory changes related to NBFCs were found today.

### Upcoming Deadline

- **September 11, 2026** — Comments due on RBI's Draft "Interest Rates on Loans and Advances) Directions, 2026" (issued Aug 12). This is a harmonised framework for ALL regulated entities replacing six separate RE-specific circulars. HIGH IMPACT for MFL: includes APR ceiling for MFI loans <=₹50,000, benchmark-plus-spread pricing, and 3-year spread lock. Effective date proposed April 1, 2027.
- **September 30, 2026** — Deadline for Type I NBFCs (no public funds, no customer interface, <₹1,000 crore assets) to apply for de-registration under the Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies — Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2025.

### Recent Developments Still Developing

1. **Revolving Credit Draft** (Aug 6, 2026): RBI proposed barring NBFCs from revolving credit. After 350+ industry responses and a Sep 3 meeting between DG Murmu and FIDC, RBI is leaning toward partial restriction rather than blanket ban. Supply chain financing carve-out being discussed. Final guidelines expected ~November 2026. LOW impact on MFL (gold loans are term-secured products).

2. **DG Murmu NBFC-HFC Summit Speech** (Sep 3, 2026): Five priorities — governance, liquidity, asset quality, customer conduct, cyber. Pushed securitisation for risk transfer. Urged funding diversification. NBFC credit at 16.7% of GDP.
