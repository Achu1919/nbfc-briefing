## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Tuesday: Gold Loan Sector

**The growth machine is intact, but the easy part of the cycle is ending.** Monday's RBI data crystallised the story: NBFC gold-loan books grew **68.5% YoY in July** to ₹3.54 lakh crore (₹1.4 lakh cr → ~₹3.5 lakh cr in ~2 years; industry outstanding up ~136% over two years per earlier Express reporting). Yet three forces are converging:

1. **Banks are the bigger, faster lender now.** Bank gold-loan books (₹5.52 lakh cr) grew 88.1% in July — far above NBFCs' 68.5% — even as their growth decelerates from the extraordinary 136.4% pace. Bank competition is what will compress NBFC pricing power and cap market share.
2. **Gold price normalisation.** MCX gold (~₹1.52L) is ~21% below its ₹1.93L ATH; Muthoot's MD has publicly guided that AUM growth will increasingly come from **tonnage (quantity pledged) and new customers, not price appreciation** — tonnage rises when gold falls. Antique now models ~15% annual gold-AUM growth FY27–FY29 for Muthoot after +50% in FY26 and +43% YoY in Q1 FY27. Average LTV on Muthoot's stock is ~68% (new loans 70–85%), so headroom exists for incremental borrowing against existing pledges.
3. **Demand-side policy overhang.** PM Modi's Sep 1 appeal (repeated overnight Sep 7–8) to defer non-essential gold buying, if sustained, slows fresh jewellery acquisition — the primary source of new pledge customers. Early reads: Sep imports may fall ~15%; jewellers warn of demand halving to ~500 tonnes/yr; physical cash discounts are widening. Muthoot FinCorp's own ₹700 cr NCD (opens today) and the Muthoot Money merger (1,006 branches; appointed date Apr 1, 2027) show the group is funding for continued branch-led expansion regardless.

**House view for Muthoot-watchers**: Q1 was the trough for reported yields (17.9% vs ~20% peak — normalising, not deteriorating; MD expects 18–18.5% steady-state and NIM 10.5–11%); the sector's share-price derating (Muthoot −30% from high, ~11.6 P/E TTM) already prices slower growth; Antique's initiation (Muthoot Hold/₹3,110, Manappuram Buy/₹410) is the first formal coverage stance of the post-rally cycle. Key Q2 signposts: gold price trajectory, tonnage growth, and whether bank-led competition forces visible rate cuts on new gold loans.
