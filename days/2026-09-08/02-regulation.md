## Key Developments This Week

### 1. Banks Reclassifying MFI Loans as Retail — MFIN Flags to RBI

**What:** Banks are shifting microfinance loans from the MFI portfolio into their general retail book, making the sector appear to shrink even as lending continues.

**The numbers:** Bank-held MFI portfolio fell 28.5% YoY to ₹83,080 crore as of June 30, 2026. Banks now represent ~25% of total MFI portfolio (down from 33% a year ago).

**Why it matters:** MFIN has formally complained to RBI that this reclassification hides the true risk profile of these assets. If banks are booking MFI exposure as retail, it evades MFI-specific prudential and reporting requirements. Could prompt RBI to tighten classification norms.

**MFL note:** MFL doesn't depend on bank wholesale lines for MFI funding, but the sector narrative matters for investor sentiment and growth positioning. Watch for any RBI guidance on classification standards.

**Sources:** Business Standard (Sep 1), Moneycontrol, Financial Express, Whalesbook (Sep 2)

### 2. DG Murmu at 7th NBFC-HFC Summit — Five Priorities

**What:** RBI Deputy Governor S.C. Murmu delivered the keynote at the CII NBFC-HFC Summit on September 3, outlining five supervisory priorities:

1. Governance and culture
2. Liquidity management
3. Asset quality and credit risk
4. Customer protection and fair conduct
5. Digital transformation and cyber resilience

**Key points:**
- Pushed securitisation as a tool for NBFCs to transfer risk and free up capital
- Noted credit growth at 16.7% of GDP — warned against growth at cost of underwriting standards
- Urged NBFCs to diversify funding sources beyond bank borrowing and NCDs
- Emphasised use of AI/ML in underwriting and customer service
- Referenced the November 2025 consolidation of NBFC regulations as a foundational reform

**MFL note:** The securitisation push is directly relevant. MFL's large gold loan book could benefit from partial securitisation to improve CRAR headroom or fund growth. Worth a board-level discussion.

**Source:** RBI speech (Sep 3), ET BFSI, Livemint, CorpLawUpdates

### 4. Interest Rate Harmonisation Draft — Comments Close September 11

**What:** RBI's draft "Interest Rates on Loans and Advances Directions, 2026" harmonises pricing rules across all REs (banks, NBFCs, HFCs, AIFIs).

**Key provisions:**
- All loans must be benchmarked (internal or external), plus risk-based spread
- Spread lock for minimum 3 years once fixed
- APR ceiling mandatory for microfinance loans and small personal loans (≤₹50,000)
- Interest at monthly rests, daily reducing balance, actual/actual day count
- External benchmark linkage not mandatory for NBFCs (unlike banks for personal/MSME loans)
- Effective date: April 1, 2027

**MFL note:** HIGH IMPACT. The APR ceiling on small MFI loans and the 3-year spread lock could reshape MFL's MFI pricing model. Comments close in 3 days (Sep 11). If MFL hasn't submitted feedback, there's still time.

**Source:** RBI draft (Aug 12), Press Release 63355, ET BFSI

### 5. Revolving Credit Draft — Still in Consultation

**What:** RBI's August 6 draft proposed barring NBFCs from offering revolving credit (only term loans allowed, except credit card-issuing NBFCs).

**Status as of Sep 3:**
- 350+ responses received by RBI
- DG Murmu met FIDC and NBFC CEOs on Sep 3
- RBI leaning toward partial restriction rather than blanket ban
- Supply chain financing, TReDS, LAS, and BNPL carve-outs being discussed
- Final guidelines expected ~November 2026

**MFL note:** MFL's core gold loan book is already term-loan structured (bullet or EMI). Low direct impact, but any SCF or working capital products within the group could be affected.

**Source:** RBI draft (Aug 6), ET BFSI (Sep 3), Reuters, Hindu Business Line
