## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Gold loans are now India's #2 retail lending segment — but growth is price-driven and repeat-borrowing heavy.** A fresh Motilal Oswal thematic report (Sept 1–7) shows the organised gold-loan market has ~4x'd in five years to **₹18.6 lakh crore** (March 2026), overtaking personal loans to sit #2 behind home loans. It forecasts **~28% CAGR over FY26–28**, book crossing **₹30 lakh crore by March 2028**. **The caveat that matters for Muthoot:** combined gold pledged with Muthoot Finance + Manappuram has stayed flat at ~260–265 tonnes and customer base ~9 million even as loans surged — i.e. growth is coming from higher gold prices and repeat borrowing (**82% of originations in 2025 vs 76% in 2022; 90% from existing borrowers**), not new customers. That raises overleveraging risk. Gold loans are 15.7% of all consumption loans (banks + NBFCs). (*Why it matters to you: this is the core competitive landscape for the Muthoot group.*)

## 🏛️ RBI & Regulatory — Headlines Only

- **RBI Second Amendment Directions, 2026** on NBFC registration/exemptions: introduces an "unregistered" category; existing NBFCs meeting the criteria can surrender their Certificate of Registration via the **PRAVAAH portal until Sept 30, 2026** (one-time window). Draft places **Upper-Layer NBFCs at assets > ₹1 lakh crore**. *(Under implementation — see NBFC Guru for the framework.)*
- **RBI bars banks, SFBs and NBFCs from selling acquired stressed assets back to defaulting borrowers/related parties** — new prudential norm effective **October 2026**.
- **Revised NBFC categorisation framework** flagged as forthcoming by Governor Sanjay Malhotra in the post-policy presser (July).
- **RBI gold-loan framework (April 2026) now live:** tiered LTV (85% ≤₹2.5 lakh; 80% ₹2.5–5 lakh; 75% >₹5 lakh), mandatory credit assessment above ₹2.5 lakh, standardised KFS, 14-day auction notice with ≥90% reserve price, ₹5,000/day penalty for delayed gold return (>7 working days). Lenders (incl. Manappuram) are aligning processes.
- **RBI Cybersecurity / Technology Risk, Resilience & Assurance (CTRRA) Framework** — one of seven parallel circulars issued across regulated entities in mid-2026; NBFCs now within scope.
- **RBI co-lending rules (revised, effective Jan 1, 2026):** banks must retain 10% of every co-lent loan; enhanced transparency for MSME lending.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — WEDNESDAY: HOUSING FINANCE

- **Sector health (ICRA):** AUM of Affordable Housing Finance Companies (AHFCs) grew **17% YoY in FY26 to ₹1,66,716 cr** (Mar '26), helped by LAP growth; AHFC share of total HFC on-book portfolio rose to **~15%** (from 11% in FY21). ICRA expects **19–21% AUM growth in FY27**, backed by the "Housing for All" thrust. Banks + NHB refinance = 59% of AHFC borrowing mix; capital-market funding still limited.
- **Premier HFCs holding up on volumes:** Bajaj Housing Q1 AUM +24% / disbursements ₹19,500 cr; LIC Housing disbursements +14.5%, Stage-3 to 2.14%; PNB Housing AUM +13%, targets ₹1 lakh cr by Q3 FY27.
- **Affordable/micro segment is the growth engine:** PNB Housing expects **40–45% growth in affordable** (vs 10–12% prime); launching **micro-housing** (tickets ₹9–20 lakh, avg ~₹12 lakh, ₹100 cr book target FY27). Aadhar Housing (EWS/LIG, semi-urban) crossed ₹30,000 cr AUM. Sundaram Home doubling TN Emerging-Business AUM to ~₹1,300 cr via Tier-3/4 branches. Capri Global expanding affordable + retail construction finance.
- **Competition & funding:** PNB Housing approved a **₹10,000 cr NCD raise** (Sept 7) and is shifting toward **repo-linked borrowing**; ECBs ~7% of borrowings. Gross Stage-3 across HFCs broadly stable-to-improving.
- **Watch item:** HFCs classified in the NBFC upper layer (like PNB Housing) are monitoring the revised categorisation framework; management expects no material operational change.

## 🏛️ Government Policy (affecting NBFCs)

- **RBI PSL Amendment Directions, 2026** — co-lending/on-lending/securitisation pathways for banks to hit Priority Sector Lending targets via NBFCs/HFCs (policy detail in Guru's report).
- Significant additional central/state NBFC-specific policy in the coverage window: **no major new item** beyond the RBI items listed above. Broader context: household debt ~45.5% of GDP; organised lending formalisation and RBI digital-lending rules continue to shape the sector.
