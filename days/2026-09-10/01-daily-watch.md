- **The RBI revolving-credit draft is THE MSME credit story**: proposed ban on NBFC revolving/flexi credit could hit supply-chain finance, invoice discounting, dealer finance and working-capital loans. Aditya Birla Capital and Tata Capital best placed; Mahindra Finance most NIM-sensitive (92% vehicle book, fixed-rate). *(Source: [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-may-turn-to-higher-yield-retail-msme-loans-to-protect-margins/133527009))*
