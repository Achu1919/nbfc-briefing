## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. RBI moves to ban "revolving credit" for most NBFCs — and may soften it.**
The RBI's draft amendment to the Non-Banking Financial Company Credit Facilities Directions, 2026 proposes that NBFCs may *only* offer **term loans** (fixed principal, fixed amortisation schedule, no re-draw), and would be **prohibited from offering revolving credit** (draw–repay–re-draw) except for card-issuing NBFCs (effectively only SBI Card and BoB Financial Solutions). This would sweep in flexi loans, overdraft-style facilities, cash-credit lines, supply chain finance, factoring and several digital-lending products. **What's changed since:** RBI Deputy Governor S C Murmu has met FIDC and NBFC CEOs after receiving ~350 industry responses, and is now reportedly weighing a *partial* restriction rather than a blanket ban. FIDC's ask: allow "limited redraw within a declining contractual ceiling" provided maturity isn't extended, sanctioned amounts aren't raised and redraws aren't used to regularise overdue accounts. Final guidelines are expected to take another ~2 months. *(Directly relevant: this touches the working-capital/MSME products and any flexi structures — and it is the sector's biggest open regulatory fight right now.)*
- Sources: [Taxguru analysis (10 Sep)](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-proposes-ban-revolving-credit-products-nbfcs.html), [ETBFSI Rethink report (3 Sep)](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-dg-murmu-meets-fidc-nbfc-ceos-may-rethink-blanket-ban-on-revolving-credit/133737739), [Mint on supply-chain finance (2 Sep)](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/nonbanks-seek-clarity-exemption-for-supply-chain-finance-from-rbi-11788338751938.html)

**2. RBI proposes harmonised loan-pricing framework + APR caps (comments close today, 11 Sep).**
The draft framework mandates board-approved pricing policies and **benchmark-linked floating rates for banks**; NBFCs get *discretion* on external benchmarks (so no forced change for Muthoot-type books). Key NBFC-relevant part: **explicit APR ceilings to prevent usurious pricing on small-value loans (≤₹50,000 personal loans) and microfinance loans**. Proposed effective date 1 Apr 2027, with a one-time mapping of existing loans by 1 Apr 2029. **Public comment window closes today.**
- Source: [Livemint (RBI loan pricing proposal)](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/rbi-proposes-tighter-loan-pricing-rules-external-benchmark-norms-apr-caps-and-key-changes-explained-for-borrowers-11786587661188.html)

**3. Jefferies: Manappuram "Buy," Muthoot "Hold" — gold-loan competition is intensifying fast.**
Jefferies' fresh NBFC gold-loan note (10 Sep) confirms **retail gold loans vs jewellery grew ~80% YoY (July 2026)** and total gold loans (incl. agri) **+50% YoY to ₹18.6 lakh crore**. Jefferies: **Manappuram Buy, TP ₹430 (+~34%)**; **Muthoot Hold, TP ₹3,300 (+~17%)**; notes Muthoot's 6,127-branch network and +500–600 gold branches planned in FY27. *Competitive context that matters for Muthoot Fincorp's core gold business.*

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — FRIDAY: Auto Finance & EV Lending

- **Market size:** India's vehicle-financing market is ~**USD 46.3 bn (2026)**, projected to reach **USD 63.8 bn by 2031 (CAGR ~6.6%)**; **NBFCs dominate over banks** thanks to sharper product focus and deeper non-metro presence (esp. used-vehicle finance).
- **RBI July data:** NBFC **vehicle loans grew +15.1% YoY (July 2026)**, broadly steady — the strongest durable-loan performer alongside housing (+11.9%).
- **EV financing is the growth story:**
  - The EV-financing sector **absorbed ₹1,000+ crore of fresh capital across six dedicated green NBFCs** within the last few months.
  - **Cholamandalam** said it **exceeded its institutional EV disbursement targets in FY26**, and sees **EVs disrupting the entry-level 2-wheeler segment**.
  - **Ecofy**, touted as India's first green-only NBFC, finances EVs, rooftop solar and SME clean-energy for **1,25,000+ retail customers**.
  - **Mufin Green Finance** holds ~**₹460 crore** in EV+solar AUM (end-FY26), pivoting to profitability-led growth.
  - **AMU (an NBFC)** is targeting **₹400 crore EV-financing AUM by March 2027**, with EV disbursements growing **35–40%**, driven by commercial-vehicle demand.
  - A specialised green NBFC has built climate-financing AUM of ~**₹410.8 crore** while diversifying into mediclaim/MSME/salary-linked loans (Sep 2026).
- **Structure angle:** **Tata Motors** folding **Tata Motors Finance** into **Tata Capital** consolidates auto-finance distribution under a large diversified NBFC — a structural shift to watch for auto-financing market share.
- **Bottom line:** vehicle finance stays steady-around-15% growth, but the pivot is unmistakably toward **commercial EV and green mobility**, where dedicated NBFCs are scaling fast and used-vehicle/2W segments are where NBFCs continue to out-execute banks.
