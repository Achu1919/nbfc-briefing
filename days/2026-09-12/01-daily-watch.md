## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. RBI Governor's big Friday interview — three things that hit NBFCs directly**
In an exclusive CNBC-TV18 interview on Friday, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra addressed gold loans, the draft revolving-credit ban, and MSME lending. The two most relevant for you at Muthoot / the gold-loan business: **(a)** RBI says gold-backed loans across the system are now ~**₹20 lakh crore**, of which NBFCs hold ~**₹4 lakh crore** — but the Governor declared himself "very comfortable," citing sub-1% GNPA (0.4–0.5%) and conservative LTVs as guardrails, *and* flagged a **liberalization of branch expansion for gold NBFCs**; **(b)** on the draft that would bar NBFCs from offering revolving credit, Malhotra said it was "never the regulatory intent" to allow such lines and called the draft a "reiteration" — but stressed it's **not final** and the RBI will weigh FIDC's opposition before finalising. *(Regulatory depth is NBFC Guru's job — one-liners above are flags only.)*

**2. SRG Housing Finance crashes 20% again — NHB red-flag & fictitious loans**
Shares of SRG Housing Finance Ltd locked down another 20% on Friday to **₹156**, a fresh 52-week low, extending a selloff triggered by reports that the **National Housing Bank flagged suspected fictitious housing-loan accounts worth ~₹300–400 crore** at the affordable HFC. NHB reportedly classified the account as a Red Flagged Account (RFA) and reported it to RBI's CRILC. The company (AUM ₹1,076 cr, +35% YoY; promoter family holds ~59%) categorically denies creating fictitious accounts or diverting funds and says it has received no NHB communication — but confirms **an NHB inspection is underway**. Regulatory/asset-quality scrutiny to watch.

**3. Gold-loan growth still blazing — NBFC gold loans +68.5% YoY in July**
RBI data (published Friday) shows NBFC loans against gold jewellery grew **68.5% YoY in July 2026** (+69.3% in June, +43.9% YoY by another measure), keeping gold-backed lending the single fastest-growing NBFC retail segment — squarely in your sector.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Saturday: Infrastructure & Project Finance

- **Sector credit backdrop:** In Jul'26, **NBFC credit grew 14.9% YoY to ₹59.9 lakh cr** (vs 10.6% a yr ago), led by retail (+21.4%) and agriculture (+18.0%); **industrial credit remained subdued at just 7.4% YoY** — a soft spot for project/infra-linked NBFC books (IDBI research, Sep 8). AUM growth for power-finance companies is the one laggard in Motilal Oswal's coverage universe.
- **ICRA NBFC-IFC outlook:** Healthy infrastructure activity — especially **power and urban infrastructure** — coincides with the recovery of **NBFC-Infrastructure Finance Companies' balance sheets** and the availability of relatively **long-term funding at competitive rates**, supporting fresh project finance.
- **PTC India Financial Services (PFS) — the live infra turnaround story:** Q1FY27 profit fell **70.5% to ₹40.2 cr** (legacy stress), but management signaled a pivot: **₹1,200 cr in loan sanctions recorded in July 2026**; company targets **₹5,000 cr AUM by FY27-end** focusing on the **energy value chain** (power generation, renewables, transmission) while addressing RBI compliance and resolving stressed assets. Post-Budget, PFC/IPDS revamp and infra capex push are tailwinds for its niche.
- **The infra top-tier:** PFC, REC and IRFC — all **NBFC-UL** (assets > ₹1 lakh cr) — remain the sector's giants; their liability costs and NBFC-SBR status shape pricing for the whole infra-finance segment.
- **Funding environment:** Infra NBFCs benefit from the same bond-market conditions as peers — NBFC commercial-paper issuance strong on elevated refinancing needs; long-tenor rupee bonds at competitive coupons (e.g. Bajaj Housing ₹1,985 cr NCD at 7.79%, 2031 maturity; Aavas ₹100 cr at 7.60%). Rising India 10Y (7.02%) and a possible Fed hike are the watch-outs for infra-funding cost.
