## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Microfinance & SHG Lending (Monday focus)

**The sector is healing — but on a smaller, richer base.** After **seven straight quarters of contraction**, the microfinance industry returned to sequential growth in Q1 FY27, though June 2026 book (~₹3.28–3.33 lakh crore) is still **~7% below a year ago** and **~26% below the Mar-2024 peak (~₹4.43 lakh crore)**.

- **Who's growing:** NBFC-MFIs are the engine — **44.3% of industry portfolio**, up from ~39% a year ago (their book +5.2% YoY), disbursements +43% YoY to ₹29,820 cr, funding from lenders **+91.4% YoY (₹21,407 cr / 80% of fresh funding)**. 12 large MFIs control **93.8% of AUM and 98.1% of debt funding** — a sharply two-tier market.
- **Who's shrinking:** Banks' direct microfinance book **fell ~28.5% to ₹83,080 cr** — partly a genuine retreat, partly **reclassification of MFI loans into retail books** (SIDBI-Equifax: 48% of sampled borrowers now use retail products; ₹14,639 cr of graduated retail book). SFBs −6.1%.
- **Borrower-base math is the caution flag:** active loan accounts fell to ~9.9 crore (from 12.5 crore) and unique borrowers to ~63–66 million; ~21–23 million bottom-of-pyramid borrowers have exited formal credit over two years as underwriting tightened (new-to-credit share down to ~20% from 33% in three years). Average ticket rose ~28% to ₹37,584 — and loans above ₹1 lakh grew ~90% in value — meaning the growth is **consolidation around known, better-off repeat borrowers**, not inclusion of fresh small-ticket borrowers (the original MF mandate).
- **Delinquency cured for now:** PAR 31–180 1.6% (vs 5.6%); multiple-lender exposure shrinking (share of borrowers with ≤2 lenders up to ~80.9%). CareEdge: "cleanup of most legacy stress done — credit costs to moderate; but recovery is uneven, small MFIs still face tight liquidity and higher borrowing costs."
- **State concentration:** Bihar (₹53,163 cr), UP (₹39,661 cr), TN (₹38,718 cr) ≈ 40% of the market; East & NE block 36.6% of book; best monsoon in years supporting collections.
- **Policy support:** **CGSMFI 2.0** — Govt credit-guarantee scheme to channel up to **₹20,000 crore** into MFIs — is the key liquidity bridge to watch for the under-served small players. MFIN has formally sought a review of the **₹3-lakh annual household-income threshold** that defines a microfinance borrower.
- **Bottom line for us (an NBFC with rural/micro footprint):** the recovery is real on a quality basis and opens co-lending/guarantee capacity; but the structural slides toward bigger-ticket repeat credit and a shrinking borrower funnel deserve a cautious growth-into-quality posture, and the NBFC-MFI share gains mean risk is concentrating into fewer, mostly large balance sheets.
