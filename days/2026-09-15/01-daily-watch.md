## 🏛️ RBI & Regulatory — Headlines Only

- **RBI issued NBFC – Resolution of Stressed Assets (Second Amendment) Directions, 2026** — prudential norms for "Specified Non-Financial Assets" (SNFAs) an NBFC takes over when settling borrower claims; legacy SNFAs must comply by 30 Sep 2027; disposal cap 7 years. Affects all NBFCs with non-banking assets on books. [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13567&Mode=0)
- **RBI released draft to revise NBFC Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) categorisation** — Upper Layer defined as assets > ₹1 lakh crore; draft Second Amendment Directions, 2026. (Earlier flagged by Governor post-policy.) Deep dive in the **NBFC Guru 7 AM report**. [moneycontrol.com](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/tags/nbfc.html)
- **RBI kept Tata Sons on the Upper-Layer list**, saying it won't block its registration-surrender application. [financialexpress.com](https://www.financialexpress.com/about/nbfc/)
- **90-day NPA norm now fully applicable to all NBFCs (from 31 Mar 2026)** — consolidation via IRACP Directions 2025 effective 28 Nov 2025. Structural driver of higher reported NPAs. [panchalsk.com](https://panchalsk.com/blog/nbfc-asset-classification-and-provisioning)
- **RBI Dy Gov Shirish Chandra Murmu:** NBFCs should prioritise asset quality over loan growth; flagged agri and commercial real estate stress. Deep dive in NBFC Guru's report.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — GOLD LOAN SECTOR (Tuesday focus)

India's organised gold-loan market reached **₹18.6 lakh crore (~₹18.6 trillion)** in March 2026 (+~50% YoY), now the **second-largest retail asset class, overtaking personal loans**. Motilal Oswal projects **28% CAGR over FY26–28 to ~₹30 lakh crore by March 2028**. [financialexpress.com](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/banking-finance/new-entrants-turn-up-heat-on-incumbent-gold-loan-nbfcs/4331386/)

**The competitive battle is the defining theme.** New entrants' gold loans grew ~2.5× YoY to ~₹61,900 cr (June 2026), taking ~7.6% of retail gold loans (up ~50bps from Mar 2025). Jefferies estimates new players (led by **Bajaj Finance — ~35% of new branch additions**, ~110 branches/month, targeting ₹300 bn gold AUM by FY27) could add **~3,700 branches in FY27** vs ~1,500 in FY26 — roughly 20% of the top-four incumbents' combined network. [ndtvprofit.com](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/manappuram-finance-muthoot-finance-the-gold-rush-is-moving-beyond-the-south-jefferies-spots-a-new-battle-for-growth-12026211)

**The fight is moving beyond the South** — new branches target North, West and Central India (Maharashtra, MP) where incumbents were historically less dominant. Muthoot (6,127 branches) is adding 500–600, incl. Lucknow, Jaipur, Delhi, Jalandhar; Manappuram is adding 500 (60% South/Central incl. Maharashtra; 25% Bihar/WB/Odisha). **Bajaj Finance** added 194 dedicated gold branches in Q1FY27 (total 1,701) and targets ~2,800 by FY27-end; **L&T Finance** plans 500 gold branches/year; **Aditya Birla Capital** targets 1,000 in 3 years; **Shriram** wants to double its gold book to ₹20,000 cr; **Tata Capital** (via Yogakshemam) and **Godrej** entered inorganically. [thehindubusinessline.com](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/specials/current-account/big-nbfcs-join-the-gold-loan-mela/article71464252.ece)

**Yield & margin pressure:** Muthoot's gold yield fell ~300bps YoY to **17.93%** (Q1 FY27) as it took "calibrated pricing actions"; NIM slipped from 12.15% to 10.4% sequentially. New entrants price aggressively (AB Capital gold loans from ~0.81%/month ≈ 9.72% p.a.). Manappuram instead **expanded** yields to ~18% after earlier over-correcting pricing. [askfuzz.ai](https://askfuzz.ai/discover/news/connect-the-dots/gold-loan-wars-muthoot-vs-manappuram-in-a-5-5-lakh-crore-battle)

**The swing factor = gold price.** Gold fell **~6%** after a 15% August rally (higher US yields, hawkish Fed, strong payrolls); Jefferies calls gold the single biggest driver/source of the earnings move. Medium-term they see central-bank buying and fiscal concerns as supportive. [businesstoday.in](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/manappuram-finance-muthoot-finance-shares-jefferies-gives-buy-call-on-this-gold-loan-stock-sees-34-upside-554490-2026-09-10)

**Regulatory overhang (reference only):** RBI's April 2026 gold-loan framework introduced tiered LTVs (85% ≤₹2.5 lakh; 80% to ₹5 lakh; 75% above), mandatory credit assessment above ₹2.5 lakh and standardised auctions; RBI also removed prior approval for >1,000 branches (Feb 2026) — a structural positive for branch-led incumbents.

**Bottom line for Achu:** expect intensifying deposit-of-funds and yield pressure on gold specialists near-term, but the category is still underpenetrated (only ~9–10% of household gold pledged). Muthoot's scale/trust advantage and Manappuram's digital-and-upmarket reset are the two distinct ways the market is playing it.
