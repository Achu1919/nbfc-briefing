## 🎓 Daily Teaching Session — Tuesday refresher: Specified Non-Financial Assets (SNFA)

Curriculum is in post-Day-30 refresher mode. Topic picked because it **goes live in 16 days**.

### Specified Non-Financial Assets (SNFA) — IRAC 3rd Amendment

**What is it?**

An SNFA is **immovable property** (land, building) that the NBFC took onto its own books because a borrower defaulted — typically via SARFAESI or a bilateral settlement — instead of keeping the loan as an NPA. Gold jewellery that stays **pledged** is **not** an SNFA. A branch building you bought to use is not an SNFA either (that is a fixed asset).

RBI’s worry: NBFCs were parking seized property in a grey zone — still counting it inside GNPA/NNPA, or booking unrealised interest on a dead loan, or sitting on it for years without a disposal clock. The July 2026 amendments create a **separate asset class** with its own income, classification and auction rules.

---

**The Technical Details**

Two linked amendments, both dated **16 July 2026**, both effective **1 October 2026** [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-01]:

1. **RBI (NBFC – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026** — RBI/2026-27/189, DOR.STR.REC.170/21-04-048/2026-27. Inserts Chapter VII-A (paras 138A–138O) and the SNFA definition (para 15A).
   Source: [RBI circular index Id=13567](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=13567)

2. **RBI (NBFC – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Third Amendment Directions, 2026** — RBI/2026-27/197, DOR.STR.REC.162/21-04-048/2026-27. Inserts paras 40C–40D on income recognition.
   Source: [RBI Notification Id=13575](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13575&Mode=0)

Applies to **all layers** (BL / ML / UL) that acquire immovable assets in satisfaction of claims, including HFCs. MFL is ML.

### How you may acquire one

- **Only from an NPA account.** You cannot take property from a standard borrower to “clean” the book.
- Acquisition is deemed only when **title is in the NBFC’s name** and the NBFC can deal with the asset on its own (para 138C).
- Full or partial extinguishment of the exposure, **non-recourse**.
- **Partial** extinguishment = **restructuring** of the residual loan (para 138E–138F). Residual exposure follows the stressed-asset / IRAC restructuring rules — including extra 5% specific provision if Chapter VI-A resolution is used.
- Board policy must cover: SNFA as a **share of total assets**, eligibility, delegation, recovery steps **before** acquisition, and a **maximum disposal period not exceeding 7 years** (para 16A / 138J).

### How you must dispose

- Public auction, using SARFAESI auction principles (para 138K).
- **Cannot sell back** to the borrower or IBC-related parties — even after the asset has stopped being an SNFA because you put it to own use (para 138L–138M).
- Put to own use → reclassify to **Fixed assets** from that date; it ceases to be SNFA.
- Report SNFAs on CIMS (HFCs to NHB) in the Annex-2 format (para 138O).

### How you account (this is the FP&A bit)

| Treatment | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Balance-sheet line | Separate head **“Specified Non-Financial Assets”** — **not** inside loans / GNPA / NNPA / PCR (para 138N) |
| Accrued but unrealised interest / charges on the extinguished loan | **Do not recognise as income** on acquisition (IRAC para 40C) |
| Legacy SNFAs on books as of **30 Sep 2026** that already recognised such income | Reverse through P&L by **30 Sep 2027**, to the extent still unrealised |
| Income from the property (rent, sale surplus) | **Non-interest / other income**, in the year realised (para 40D) |
| Upkeep expense | P&L in the year incurred |
| New SNFAs from **1 Oct 2026** | Full playbook from day one |
| Legacy stock | Full compliance by **30 Sep 2027** (para 138B) |

Parent IRAC still applies to the **loan book**: NPA = overdue >90 days; ML standard provision 0.40%; gold loans with LTV >75% standard provision 1%. Source: RBI (NBFC – IRAC) Directions, 2025, RBI/DOR/2025-26/356 [UPDATED:2026-07-01]. SNFA is **outside** that ratio.

---

**Why It Matters**

- GNPA/NNPA can **fall** the day you take title — the asset leaves the NPA stock. That is a presentation win, not a credit win. PCR also moves because the numerator/denominator both lose the loan.
- P&L can **hurt**: reversing previously booked interest on legacy SNFAs is a one-time hit, latest FY27.
- Holding property for years without a 7-year clock is no longer allowed. Forced auction into a thin local market = haircut vs carrying amount.
- Selling it back to the same promoter group is banned. Related-party “rescue” structures die.
- Supervisory reporting (CIMS) means RBI will see the stock. Mis-tagging gold or branch premises as SNFA, or leaving SARFAESI stock inside GNPA, is an inspection finding.

---

**Recent Changes**

Previously: no dedicated SNFA chapter. Seized immovable sat in “non-banking assets / assets acquired in satisfaction of claims” with uneven income recognition and no hard disposal clock.

Now (from 1 Oct 2026): named asset class, NPA-only gate, title test, 7-year outer limit, public auction, related-party ban, **out of GNPA/NNPA**, interest reversal on legacy stock by 30 Sep 2027.

Draft was 5 May 2026; final 16 July 2026 after feedback.

**Quiz yourself:**

1. A gold-loan customer defaults and MFL auctions the jewellery under the gold-loan directions. Is the unsold gold an SNFA? **No.** SNFA is immovable property acquired in satisfaction of claims. Gold stays pledged jewellery / auction inventory under the gold-loan conduct rules.
2. MFL takes title to a borrower’s warehouse on 15 Oct 2026 against a ₹4 cr NPA, extinguishing ₹3 cr and leaving ₹1 cr on the books. What happens to the ₹1 cr? **It is a restructuring** of the residual exposure. The warehouse is an SNFA (out of GNPA). The ₹1 cr stays a loan and follows restructuring IRAC, including extra provision if a Chapter VI-A plan is used.
3. Accrued interest of ₹12 lakh on a loan that became an SNFA in 2025 was booked in FY26 and is still unrealised. Deadline to reverse? **30 September 2027**, through P&L, under IRAC para 40C.
