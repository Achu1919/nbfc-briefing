# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Tuesday, September 15, 2026

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **Broad financial-services selloff yesterday — worst session in months.** Nifty Financial Services fell **1.83%** to close at **25,076.65**, with wide selling across credit-card, NBFC and housing-finance names. Nifty fell 1.19% to 23,118.60 (its **lowest close in five months**), Sensex -1.04% to 74,003.82, wiping out ~₹9 lakh crore of market cap. Gold-loan heavyweight **Muthoot Finance slid 2.29% to ₹2,730.40** on the day, and **Manappuram fell 3.13% to ₹318.70** — a sharp reversal after gold-financier stocks had been outperforming. Triggers: elevated crude, US 10-yr yield near the 5% stress line, an FOMC decision today and a record IPO week pulling secondary-market liquidity.

2. **IIFL Finance became the first Non-PSU NBFC to raise funds via a tokenised bond** — ₹25 crore, 2-year paper at **9.1%**, executed on MSEI under SEBI's Regulatory Sandbox / Demat 2.0 pilot, to be listed on NSE. It follows REC (₹500 cr, 20-mo, 7.30%) and L&T (₹500 cr, 3-yr, 7.40%); the three pilots total ₹1,025 crore. Institutional investors only for now — a signal NBFCs are testing blockchain-based bond issuance/settlement as a future funding channel.

3. **Sammaan Capital (formerly Indiabulls Housing) sold a ₹5,000 crore stressed legacy loan portfolio to ARCIL** at ~52% haircut (~₹2,400 crore), in one of the largest such deals of the year. Structure is 15:85 with Arcil paying ~₹360 crore cash and issuing security receipts for the rest. The pool spans real-estate, large-ticket mortgage and LAP loans that are overdue but not yet NPA. It pairs with the IHC/Avenir majority-stake infusion (₹5,652 cr for 41.5%, up to 66.65% on warrant conversion) as Sammaan pivots to its growth book (gold, personal, small-business loans). CEO Gagan Banga targets AUM going from ~₹65,000 cr to ~₹1.3 lakh cr in three years.

## 📉 NPA & Asset Quality

- **NBFCs are now driving ARC (asset reconstruction) volumes.** FY26 ARCs acquired ₹2.05 lakh crore of new dues; retail acquisitions surged **~29% YoY to ₹2.47 lakh crore** and retail SR issuances +21% to ₹58,826 cr; cumulative redemption/recovery ratio improved to **62%** (from 58%). ARCIL's fastest-growing segment is **retail NPAs originated by NBFCs**; it handles ~32 lakh retail NPA accounts across 24 states.
- NBFC trades to ARCs typically involve recent-vintage NPAs / stressed-but-not-NPA pools, with quicker execution and flexible 15:85 cash-SR structures (as in the Sammaan deal).
- **Microfinance asset quality snapped back:** PAR 31–180 days fell to **1.6%** as of June 30, 2026 (from 5.6% a year earlier); NBFC-MFI PAR down to 1.5%. Recovery aided by better-than-expected monsoon and shift toward borrowers with formal credit history.
- Watch-item: LIC Housing Finance's ongoing recovery contest with Essel Group's Subhash Chandra — CBI registered an FIR (Aug 31) over an alleged **₹1,322 cr wrongful loss to LICHFL** tied to two 2018 loans (₹980 cr total) backed by allegedly inflated net-worth certificates; Chandra's personal-insolvency plan offers creditors ₹6.25 cr against ~₹22,000 cr admitted claims (LICHFL among dissenting lenders).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive (Wednesday) — Housing Finance

- **Growth is the sector's core problem.** Across the largest HFC (LICHFL, ₹3.22 lakh cr book), loan-book growth of just ~4% YoY trails the broader credit cycle, even while disbursements jump 14.5%. The squeeze is from banks using deposit franchises to price mortgages aggressively and from ~₹500 cr of net balance-transfer outflows in one quarter. Management's response: hold ~2.6% NIM, grow toward 8–10% (aspirational ~12%), and diversify into LAP, lease-rental discounting and developer finance (₹15,000 cr combined FY27 target for LAP+LRD; ≥₹4,000 cr developer finance; direct-assignment/co-lending to kick in from Q2).
- **Asset quality is the bright spot:** LICHFL's Stage 3 fell to 2.14% (from 2.62%), credit costs guided at just 10–15 bps for FY27. Yet headline risk persists in large-ticket/entity lending — the ₹1,322 cr LICHFL fraud FIR shows how real-estate-linked, personal-guarantee loans can sour and turn recovery into a multi-forum fight (CBI + NCLT + ARC).
- **Funding angle:** repo held at 5.25% (Aug 10) keeps HFC funding costs predictable — LICHFL's cost of funds fell 22 bps YoY to 7.28% — but a US-yields-led rate scare is exactly what hit Fin-Services stocks on Sep 15. Housing-finance stocks are cheap on earnings (LICHSGFIN trades ~5x vs industry ~19x; P/B 0.7), so the re-rating depends on converting faster disbursements into sustained book growth without margin erosion.
