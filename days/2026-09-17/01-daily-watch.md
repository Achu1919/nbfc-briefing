# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Sep 17, 2026

*Publication: Sep 17 (Thu) • Coverage: Sep 16 + overnight Fed/Tata developments • Sector focus: MSME & Commercial Lending*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **🪙 Indian markets brace for Fed fallout.** The US Federal Reserve raised rates **25 bps to 3.75%–4%** on Sep 16 — the **first hike since July 2023**, unanimous 12–0 — and signalled **another hike later this year** (median policy rate ~4.1% by end-2026). Fed Chair Kevin Warsh struck a hawkish tone, saying inflation "is still too high." US 10-year yields pushed back above 5%, the dollar index jumped 0.6% to 100.21, and the Dow fell ~750 pts (S&P -0.5%). For India this means **pressure on the rupee (settled at 95.95/USD), domestic bond yields and NBFC funding costs**, plus continued FII outflow risk. FIIs have already pulled **~₹2.37 lakh crore** from Indian equities in 2026 YTD (through Sep 11) vs ₹1.66 lakh crore in all of 2025. Brent crude ~$108/bbl adds to import-cost/inflation pressure. Expect a soft open today for rate-sensitive NBFC names. *(Sources: CNBC TV18, The Hindu, InvestWhat, Business Today)*

2. **🏛️ Tata Sons board meets TODAY (Sep 17)** with three decisions converging: the **RBI's rejection of its NBFC deregistration** (forcing compliance with Upper-Layer NBFC rules incl. a potential listing), **chairman N. Chandrasekaran's succession** (his term ends Feb 2027), and a governance deadlock at the Tata Trusts. RBI has **filed a caveat in Bombay High Court** to be heard first if Tata Sons challenges. A listing could unlock value for the 7 listed Tata cos holding 11.9% of Tata Sons (Tata Chemicals hit 20% upper circuit last week). Legal options: RBI reconsideration, restructuring to exit UL-NBFC, or court challenge. *(Sources: Fortune India, Times of India, Outlook Business)* — Regulatory depth in NBFC Guru's report.

3. **💻 Fibe (ex-EarlySalary) IPO cleared by SEBI** — fresh issue up to **₹750 crore** + OFS of ~4.007 crore shares (TPG Rise Fund, Norwest, Eight Roads selling). Critically, **₹562.6 crore of fresh proceeds is earmarked for the NBFC arm EarlySalary Services** to build its capital base and fund onward lending — a lending-firepower raise, not a founder exit. FY26 net profit **doubled to ₹257.5 crore** (from ₹113.7 cr) on revenue of ₹1,584.6 cr (+31%). Third lending-tech IPO of 2026 after Kissht & Aye Finance. Price band not yet set. *(Sources: CIOL, StartupFeed, Moneycontrol, ET)*

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — MSME & Commercial Lending (Thursday)

- **MSME credit is the growth engine:** the Economic Survey 2025-26 found MSME credit was the **principal driver of industrial lending growth in H1FY26**. *(ET BFSI)*
- **Big structural opportunity:** India's ~63 mn MSMEs represent a credit opportunity of **>₹18 lakh crore**. NBFCs grew MSME lending at a **32% CAGR (FY21–FY24)**, outpacing private banks (20.9%) and PSU banks (10.4%); NBFC MSME AUM is set to **cross ₹5.3 lakh crore by FY26** (CareEdge). NBFCs' agility in underwriting MSME and their reach in semi-urban/emerging markets keep them ahead in this segment.
- **Credit-growth backdrop:** all-India credit growth projected ~13.7–14.3% for FY26, led by retail and MSME. *(ICRA–ASSOCHAM)*
- **Latest pulse (SIDBI–Equifax MSME Pulse, updated Sep 10):** MSME commercial lending data + business loans to individuals shows continued diversification, though the July 2026 edition flags a **moderating borrower base** — higher ticket sizes to fewer borrowers, a margin-vs-risk watch-item for commercial lenders.
- **Caveat for the day:** the Fed's hawkish stance keeps wholesale borrowing costs elevated, which disproportionately pressures MSME NBFCs dependent on bank/ECB funding. Watch unsecured retail and MSME segments if rates stay high and crude holds >$100. *(Mint/analyst commentary)*
