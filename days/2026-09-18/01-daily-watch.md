# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — September 17, 2026

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **RBI rejects Tata Sons' bid to exit NBFC framework — mandatory listing back on the table.** The Reserve Bank has **rejected Tata Sons' application to surrender its registration** as a Core Investment Company (CIC) / upper-layer NBFC, implicitly directing India's biggest holding company to comply with upper-layer listing rules. Tata Sons was classified as an **Upper Layer NBFC in September 2022** with a three-year listing deadline (which lapsed in **September 2025**); it remains unlisted. After becoming debt-free, Tata Sons applied to surrender its CIC registration in 2024, but RBI **re-placed it on the upper-layer list in August 2026** and has now refused the exit — closing the holding firm's route to stay private. This dominated Tata Sons' **board meeting on September 17**, alongside chairman N. Chandrasekaran's succession and Tata Trusts governance. Why it matters to you: Tata Sons is the parent of **Tata Capital** — a forced IPO at the holding-company level is a major market event for the entire Tata financials complex. (Sources: News18, Outlook Business, Business Standard, Fortune India)

2. **Shriram Finance: EV financing surges 5-fold; CV demand resilient despite price hikes.** Umesh Revankar, Executive Vice-Chairman, told TOI/ETAuto (Sept 17) that monthly EV disbursements have **grown five-fold to ~₹250 crore** this year (from ₹50 crore last year), with a target of **₹500 crore/month by next year**. Commercial-vehicle demand remains strong despite commodity-driven price hikes, as rising vehicle values support freight rates and existing owners. SFL plans **~150 new branches and 2,000–3,000 hires this year**, targeting **18–20% AUM growth in FY27** and aiming to double the book in five years. The **MUFG 20% stake (~₹40,000 crore preferential issue)** gives capital cover until 2030. (Source: ETAuto/TOI, Sep 17)

3. **Markets hit by Fed hike + ₹22,569-crore NSE IPO.** The US Federal Reserve **raised rates by 25 bps** (Sept 16) and signalled a possible further hike; **Brent crude ~$106/bbl** amid Strait-of-Hormuz risk. India's benchmark indices closed near-flat Thursday as the mega **NSE IPO (₹22,569 crore)** — India's largest ever — began absorbing secondary-market liquidity. Financials were mixed-to-weak: **Bank Nifty fell 0.42%** on selling in HDFC Bank/ICICI Bank, while **insurance stocks surged** (HDFC Life +5.05%, SBI Life +4.06% — top Nifty gainers). (Sources: ThePrint, DSIJ, Upstox, India Today)

## 🏛️ RBI & Regulatory — Headlines Only

- **Draft RBI (NBFC – Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026** (released Aug 6; comment window closed **Aug 28**): proposes **barring NBFCs from offering revolving-credit facilities** — a term-loan-only regime, with credit-card issuers exempt. Would hit flexi loans, overdrafts/LAP maxgain-style products, digital credit lines, MSME cash credit, supply-chain finance and some BNPL flows. Kotak Inst. Equities expects a **shift to bullet loans, higher borrower costs and lower lender returns** — more a market-share shift than borrower losses. Industry is seeking carve-outs (notably secured/SME/SCF). *(Pending final notification — watch NBFC Guru.* Deep dive in NBFC Guru's 7 AM report.)

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — AUTO FINANCE & EV LENDING *(Friday focus)*

- **EV financing is the clearest new growth engine.** Shriram Finance — India's largest CV financier — reported monthly EV disbursements **up 5× to ~₹250 crore**, heading toward **₹500 crore/month** in the next year, with demand across two-wheelers, three-wheelers, cars and increasingly heavy CVs. Revankar flagged experimentation with **strap-on batteries** for long-haul runs (e.g., Mumbai port–Pune), and expects electrification to spread through commercial-vehicle fleets. (ETAuto/TOI, Sept 17)
- **CV demand staying firm despite price hikes:** commodity-driven price increases aren't denting demand because rising replacement values lift freight rates and existing owners' asset values — and higher financed values support loan-book growth. SFL sees CV demand as "neither stressed nor unusually accelerating." (ETAuto)
- **Funding tailwind:** the **MUFG 20% stake (~₹40,000 cr)** covers capital needs to 2030 and gives Shriram an on-ramp to Japanese corporates seeking financing in India; branch network to expand ~150 this year.
- **Others:** Mahindra Finance's record quarter was powered by **tractors (+45%) and PV financing (+24%)**; Chola's vehicle-finance AUM reached ₹1,24,132 cr (+19% disbursements). **Hyundai Capital** (Sept 1) is entering dealer-level auto financing in India, and **HDB Financial Services** securitised its auto/CV/construction-equipment book (Horizon Trust, Provisional CRISIL AAA SO) — evidence of deep ABS demand for vehicle receivables.
- **For Muthoot context:** vehicle lending remains dominated by cash-flow underwriters (Shriram, Chola, Mahindra) with structurally higher stage-3 ratios than gold collateral lending; EV residual-value risk is the new monitorable across the segment.
