## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Tata Sons lines up IPO — Feb 2027 internal working target** (Sept 19 morning)
After the RBI rejected Tata Sons' bid to surrender its NBFC (Core Investment Company) registration on Sept 11–12, the salt-to-software holding company has started preparing for a mandatory stock-market listing. People familiar with the matter told ET that **February 2027** has emerged as an internal working target. Tata Sons had total assets of ~₹2.01 lakh crore as of March 31, 2026 — more than double the ₹1,00,000-crore threshold for Upper Layer classification. This is the single biggest regulatory-driven listing in Indian corporate history and will reshape India's ownership landscape. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/tata-sons-listing-nbfc), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/tata-sons-listing-rbi-cic-registration-nbfc-timeline-126091300142_1.html))

**2. Jio Credit–Bank of America ₹18,268-cr deal closing by December**
Jio Financial Services' non-bank lender Jio Credit is poised to receive **₹18,268 crore** from Bank of America (for a stake just under 50%, up to 49.9%) by **December**, pending RBI and CCI approvals. Board will be restructured: BofA gets 2 seats, JFS 2 (including MD & CEO), plus 4 independent directors. Deal values Jio Credit at ~$3.8 bn. BofA gets 26.5% initially via preferential allotment, potentially rising to 49.9% on warrant exercise. ([India Business Journal](https://indiabusinessjournal.com/full-story/2675/BofA-to-buy-49.9%-in-Jio-Credit-for-Rs-18,268-crore-to-gain-from-robust-loan-growth), [Newsbizkoot](https://newsbizkoot.com/finance-monetary-rbi-finncial-markets-prices-policy/2026/jio-financial-says-bofas-%e2%82%b918268-cr-to-come-by-dec-jio-credits-product-plans-strategy-to-be-redrawn/))

**3. SRG Housing Finance — NHB flags suspected fictitious loans (~₹300–400 cr)**
Udaipur-based affordable HFC **SRG Housing** saw its shares hit a 20% lower circuit two sessions running (Sept 10 and 11) after the National Housing Bank (NHB) reportedly classified the account as **Red Flagged (RFA)** and flagged it to RBI's CRILC. Allegations: loans shown as disbursed where customers couldn't be verified, evergreening, and NPA manipulation — roughly a third of its ~₹1,076-cr loan book. The company strongly denies all allegations and says a routine NHB inspection is ongoing with no adverse finding yet communicated. Stock fell from ₹195 to ₹156. Must watch the inspection outcome — relevant to the small-HFC trust narrative. ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/srg-housing-finance-share-price-falls-20-on-nhb-loan-report-company-denies-allegations-12026602), [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/industry/banks/story/national-housing-bank-red-flags-exposure-to-srbs-fictitious-housing-loans-worth-rs-400-crore-554496-2026-09-10))

**4. RBI cancels CoRs of 5 NBFCs; 8 surrender licences** (Sept 17)
Cancelled: Dar's Financial Services, Rolta Holding & Finance Corp, Vasudeo Securities, Parsoli Corporation, A C Choksi Financial Services. Surrendered: Anupam Mercantile, Grand Motor & Finance, Sky Limit International Finance, ASA International India Microfinance (exit from NBFI business), Shivam Securities, April Investment & Finance (amalgamation), Anagram Industries (now unregistered CIC), CDN Finance (unregistered Type-I NBFC). ## 📉 NPA & Asset Quality

- **Microfinance asset quality sharply better:** PAR 31–180 days fell to **1.6%** (June 2026) from 5.6% a year earlier; NBFC-MFI PAR 31–180 ~1.5%. But the headline recovery masks a **shrinking borrower base — ~1.4 crore borrowers and 2.9 crore loan accounts lost in one year**. Industry increasingly lends *larger tickets to existing, better-off customers* (>₹1-lakh loans +90% in value). A cleaner book, but with reduced inclusion. ([Substack/MicroLend](https://abhilashs385253.substack.com/p/microfinance-says-it-is-healing-so), [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/nbfc/microfinance-industry-returns-to-growth-after-seven-quarters-of-slowdown/133695206))
- **Banks retreating from direct microfinance:** bank MFI portfolios fell ~28.5% YoY (largely reclassification to retail + genuine de-risking); NBFC-MFIs now hold 44.3% of industry portfolio (+5.2% YoY), absorbing risk banks are stepping away from. NBFC-MFI debt funding from banks surged 91.4% YoY. ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/banks-shifting-microfinance-strategy-prefer-lending-to-entities-over-direct-borrowers-report-19981819.htm))
- **ARC / write-off-driven cleanup:** NBFC-MFI deliquency improvement has been partly aided by significant write-offs and portfolio sales to ARCs, per ICRA. ([ICRA](https://www.icra.in/Rating/DownloadResearchSummaryReport?id=6808))
- **SRG Housing** red-flag episode (see Urgent) highlights asset-integrity risk in smaller HFCs.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Infrastructure & Project Finance *(Saturday rotation)*

**Big week for infra finance — NaBFID dominated the agenda:**

1. **Infrastructure Conclave 2026 (Mumbai, Sept 18):** Keynotes from **SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey** and DEA JS **Laya Madduri**; panels on unlocking domestic/global capital, mobilising insurance capital into infra, capital recycling via securitisation/CDS/TRS, asset monetisation via InvITs & REITs, and new capital pools (private credit, pension, SWF, insurance). ([NaBFID PR](https://businessnewsforprofit.com/news/national-bank-for-financing-infrastructure-and-development-hosts-infrastructure-conclave-2026-to-deliberate-on-avenues-for-mobilising-long-term-infrastructure-capital/))

2. **NaBFID–BCG report:** India needs **₹168–172 lakh crore** infrastructure investment through FY31. Of that, only ~₹38–40 lakh cr is financed; ₹90–92 lakh cr in the announced pipeline; **₹78–80 lakh cr has not even entered the project pipeline**, and ₹21–22 lakh cr is stalled. Push is on for project bankability and capital recycling. ([Fortune India](https://www.fortuneindia.com/economy/india-needs-168-172-trillion-in-infrastructure-investment-by-fy31-but-78-80-trillion-is-yet-to-enter-project-pipeline-nabfid-bcg/159732))

3. **NaBFID data-centre push:** sanctioned loans of ₹30 bn+ (~$313 mn) each to at least 4 data centres (total >$1.2 bn) with 5-yr moratoriums/10-yr repayments, riding India's AI buildout. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/indian-project-financier-s-data-center-loans-top-1-2-billion))

4. **NaBFID funding plan (FY27):** targeting **~₹1,00,000 cr fundraise**, of which **30–40% (~₹40,000 cr) from overseas**, using RBI's concessional swap/ECB window open till December. Already raised $1 bn via ECB; bond issuance planned this month. Book target: **₹5 lakh cr by 2029-30** (sanctioned pipeline already ₹4 lakh cr; outstanding ~₹1.4 lakh cr). ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/industry/banks/story/nabfid-eyes-rs-1-lakh-crore-fundraise-to-tap-overseas-markets-for-up-to-40-556267-2026-09-17), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/interviews/have-a-pipeline-of-4-trillion-in-sanctioned-loans-says-nabfid-md-126091601058_1.html))

**Why it matters for NBFCs/you:**
- **Emerging sectors** (data centres, battery energy storage, urban infra, logistics, digital infra) are opening beyond roads/power for NBFC-IFCs and project financiers. ₹136 lakh cr estimated need over 2025-30.
- **InvIT AUM crossed ₹7 lakh cr** — capital recycling is unlocking room for NBFC participation.
- **Maharashtra** is setting up its own **state NBFC** (two-stage EoI/RFP, tendering committee formed Sept 7) to fund **₹25–35 lakh cr of 'Viksat Maharashtra 2047'** infra (metros, expressways, Vadhavan port, renewables, AI innovation cities). ([Yemeni... NewKerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/maha-govt-forms-tender-committee-set-up-nbfc-238.htm))
- RBI's **Project Finance Directions, 2025** and the IDF-NBFC large-exposure amendment (Aug 25) set the regulatory frame for project-finance NBFCs. Infrastructure is a structural-growth theme for FY27 onward.
