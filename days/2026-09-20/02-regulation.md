### Adjacent, not for MFL — [AMENDMENT] Bank KYC: FPI certified-copy facility (18 Sep)

- **What:** RBI substituted the definition of “Certified Copy” in the KYC Directions for **six bank classes**, so Foreign Portfolio Investors can use the same overseas certified-copy route already available to NRIs/PIOs.
- **Issuer:** RBI, Department of Regulation (AML).
- **Date:** **18 September 2026**, immediate effect. [EFFECTIVE:2026-09-18]
- **Reference:** RBI/2026-27/257 to 262 (Commercial Banks, SFBs, LABs, RRBs, UCBs, RCBs). Example: [Rural Co-operative Banks KYC Amendment](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13704&Mode=0) — DOR.AML.REC.223/14.01.005/2026-27.
- **In Plain English:** If an FPI opens an account at a bank, the bank may accept an original certified copy attested by an overseas SCB branch, correspondent bank, notary, magistrate, judge, or Indian embassy — instead of comparing Aadhaar/OVD to the original in India. This is a **banking KYC convenience**, not a mule-SOP, and **not** an NBFC KYC change.
- **Impact on NBFCs:** **None.** There is **no parallel NBFC KYC Amendment Directions, 2026** in the 18 Sep batch. The **11 Sep mule-SOP draft** (PR 1109) is still banks-only (BR Act s.35A); comments close **2 Oct**.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** Ignore for the gold / MSME / MHFL books. If compliance sees six KYC circulars on rbi.org.in dated 18 Sep, they are **bank** instruments. Watch only for an NBFC parallel of the mule-SOP after 2 Oct.

### The live regulatory clock (what is law vs what is still paper)

**What is it?**

RBI does not run one “NBFC rulebook” that flips on a single date. At any moment MFL is living in **four time-zones at once**:

1. **Already live** — you can be inspected on it tomorrow.
2. **Final, but not yet effective** — the text is published; the clock is running.
3. **Draft** — comments in; no legal force until the final Directions.
4. **Judicial overlay** — a court has told RBI to enforce *existing* text *now*, even if a later effective date is sitting in a circular.

Mixing those four is how finance teams either over-provision for drafts or under-prepare for go-lives.

**Why the four-zone split matters**

- A **draft** cannot be used as a P&L assumption. Revolving-credit “ban” is still a consultation. Kotak / FIDC colour this week is analysis, not a circular.
- A **final-but-not-live** item *can* be used as a P&L / process assumption. SNFA from 1 Oct and recovery-agent IIBF from 1 Jan are in that bucket.
- A **judicial overlay** punches a hole in “we will do it from 1 Jan.” The Supreme Court told RBI that existing fair-practices / possession guidelines have “existed only on paper,” and that waiting for 1 Jan 2027 is not an excuse for a 1 a.m. seizure happening *now*. Gold-auction notice and field-visit hours are the MFL analogue.
- **Already live** gold-loan rules (tiered LTV 85/80/75, 12-month bullet tenor, ongoing LTV, 7-day ornament return) are inspectable today. That is not this week’s news; it is the operating system of the book.

**Muthoot Fincorp Context**

MFL is **Middle Layer**, gold-loan heavy, Muthoot Pappachan Group — **not** Muthoot Finance (UL, separate group) and **not** Tata Sons (CIC / UL listing fight).

What actually moves this desk in the next 11 days:

1. **SNFA stock-take (1 Oct).** Gold jewellery pledged as *collateral* is **not** SNFA. SNFA is a **non-financial asset the lender has acquired** (typically immovable property taken in satisfaction of a debt — SARFAESI / settlement / court). Action: ask credit / legal for a one-pager — does MFL standalone or **MHFL** hold any such asset? If zero, document the nil and stop. If any, those assets go onto the SNFA register on 1 Oct: no income on pre-acquisition accrued interest, upkeep expensed as incurred, disposal clock 7 years, no sale-back to the defaulter. Legacy income reverse-through-P&L by 30 Sep 2027.
2. **Gold-auction / field conduct (already live + SC overlay).** Auction without the contractual notice, collection visits outside 08:00–19:00, or “taking” ornaments other than through the documented pledge-and-auction path is the Chola fact-pattern in gold-loan clothes. IIBF / recording can wait until 1 Jan as a *systems* project; **notice and hours cannot wait**.
3. **Do not bake revolving-credit or interest-rate drafts into FY27 guidance yet.** Interest Rate Directions are **High** for MFL *when final* (3-year spread lock on ML). They are not final. Revolving-credit is **Low** if the book is bullet/EMI gold and term MSME — but treasury should still list any OD / flexi / WC line so the Nov final does not surprise ALCO.
4. **GNPA/NNPA denominator** is still the 7-step gross carrying amount (gross AUM − ARC sold − DA/co-lending off-book + accrued interest − fee amortisation). MHFL uses on-book AUM and skips accrual + amortisation — still a consolidation restatement flag. Denominator is **not** net of loss allowance. Unchanged this week.
