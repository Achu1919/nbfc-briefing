## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **BofA to buy up to 49.9% of Jio Credit for ₹18,268 crore — NBFC mega-deal.** Bank of America signed a JV agreement with Jio Financial Services to acquire up to a 49.9% stake in Jio Credit (the digital-native NBFC, formerly Jio Finance) via preferential allotment of equity shares and warrants. Initial stake 26.5%, rising to 49.9% on warrant exercise; values Jio Credit at ~$3.8 billion. Board will be restructured (2 BofA + 2 JFS including MD&CEO + 4 independents). A JFS official said the funds are expected by **December 2026**, after RBI and CCI approvals, with Jio Credit's product/strategy being redrawn for rapid AUM growth. *(Reuters via IBJ, PRNewswire, newsbizkoot)*

2. **Shriram Finance board to consider buyback of debt securities TODAY (Mon, Sept 21).** The Banking & Finance Committee is meeting on/after Monday 21 Sept to consider a buyback of its debt securities. Amount and securities yet to be disclosed. Relevant for anyone holding Shriram NCDs. *(tradebrains)*

3. **Microfinance turnaround data out of Q1 but CGSMFI 2.0 utilisation lagging.** MFIN Micrometer Q1 FY27 shows industry portfolio ₹3.29 lakh crore with PAR (31-180 days) crashing from 5.3% to 1.5%, but the ₹20,000 crore CGSMFI 2.0 pool has seen only ~₹8,000–9,000 crore taken up — signalling smaller MFIs still struggle for affordable capital. *(MFIN/CNBC-TV18, ET)*

## 📉 NPA & Asset Quality

- **Microfinance PAR is falling fast**: industry PAR 31-180 days down to ~1.5% (NBFC-MFIs) from 5.3% a year ago; overall PAR 91-180 fell from 3.1% (Jun-25) to 0.8% (Jun-26). Banks reclassifying MF loans to retail (~28-30% of their MF book drop is accounting shift, not real exit). *(CNBC-TV18, substack)*
- **But**: total MF loan book shrank ~7.3% YoY, active loan accounts fell ~22% / 2.9 cr, borrowers -1.4 cr over the year — recovery is ratio-led while reach shrinks; concern that banks' retreat shifts risk onto smaller NBFC-MFIs. *(substack, MFIN)*
- **ARCIL** sees big opportunity in India's ~₹200 lakh crore credit market for buying stressed assets from banks & NBFCs. *(CNBC-TV18)*
- Gold dispatches growth kept Tata Capital gross NPA down to 1.9%.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Microfinance & SHG Lending (Monday)

- **Industry portfolio ₹3,28,708 crore** (Jun 30, 2026): +1.1% QoQ but **-6.9% YoY**; disbursements jumped **+8.9% YoY to ₹61,718 cr** (Q1). NBFC-MFIs dominate with **44.3% share** (portfolio +5.2% YoY). *(MFIN Micrometer 58th edition)*
- **Bank shift**: Banks' direct microfinance portfolio fell 28.5% YoY to ₹83,080 cr (reclassification to retail), but banks are the sector's largest funder — supplying **64.8% of NBFC-MFI borrowings** and 80.4% of fresh funding (₹21,407 cr; NBFC-MFI total funding +91.4% YoY). *(CNBC-TV18)*
- **Borrower base compression**: active micro-credit accounts down to 9.9 cr from 12.5 cr; average loan per account up ~28% to ₹37,584; loans >₹1 lakh grew ~90% in value — lending is concentrating in larger, repeat, known customers rather than new first-time borrowers. *(CNBC-TV18, substack)*
- **State concentration**: Bihar (₹53,163 cr), UP (₹39,661 cr), Tamil Nadu (₹38,718 cr) ≈ 40% of market; top-10 states = 83.3%. West Bengal portfolio -20.7% (highest overdue ~2x peers); Karnataka overdue (3-6m) fell from 12.5% to 1.3%. *(MFIN/substack)*
- **MFIN asks RBI** to revise the **₹3 lakh household income cap** (unchanged since 2022, eroded by inflation) and to end bank reclassification of MF loans to retail bureaus. *(365telugu/MFIN)*
- **CGSMFI 2.0**: ₹20,000 cr guarantee pool ~40-45% used (₹8,000–9,000 cr); SRO sought extension despite raised MFI borrowing ceiling — cheaper credit still not fully flowing to smaller MFIs. *(ET)*
- **Muthoot Microfin** raised ₹250 cr secured NCDs (9.25%, 24 months) on CRISIL AA-/Stable. *(sahi.com)*
- **IndusInd Bank (BFIL)** MD says microfinance stress phase over; growth expected to resume from Q2. *(wownews)*
