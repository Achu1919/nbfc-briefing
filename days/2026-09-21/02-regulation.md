## 🎓 Daily Teaching Session — SBR layers + the capital stack (Monday refresher)

Curriculum is in post-Day-30 refresher mode. Weekday cycle for Monday is **SBR / capital**. Sunday was the live regulatory clock (do not re-teach). Today’s job is the one capital question FP&A actually gets asked in a board pack: *which layer is MFL in, which ratios bind, and what would flip if the book crossed the Upper Layer line.*

### Scale-Based Regulation and the capital ratios that actually bind MFL

**What is it?**

Scale-Based Regulation is how RBI sizes the rulebook to the firm. It is **not** a credit-rating. It is a **layer**: Base / Middle / Upper / (empty) Top. Once RBI parks you in a layer, a different capital, governance and disclosure pack applies — even if your product mix does not change.

For a gold-loan ICC like MFL, two documents talk to each other:

1. **Which layer?** — *RBI (NBFCs – Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2025* (RBI/DOR/2025-26/339, DOR.FIN.REC.No.258/03.10.119/2025-26, 28 Nov 2025; updated 1 Jul 2026).
2. **How much capital in that layer?** — *RBI (NBFCs – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025* (RBI/DOR/2025-26/345, DOR.CAP.REC.264/21-01-002/2025-26, 28 Nov 2025; updated 1 Jul 2026), **paragraph 6**.

Muthoot Fincorp is **Middle Layer**. Muthoot Finance (the other group, listed, UL) is a **peer, not a cousin**. Never paste a UL CET1 or listing obligation into an MFL pack.

**The Technical Details**

**1. How layers are drawn — after 24 June 2026**

Previously: Upper Layer was a **parametric score** (size, interconnectedness, complexity, supervisory overlay). RBI published an annual list. The ₹10,000 crore figure people still quote was the *sample floor* for that scoring exercise, not a hard UL gate.

Now (from **24 June 2026**, immediate): the score is gone.

- **Source:** *RBI (NBFCs – Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation) Second Amendment Directions, 2026*, RBI/2026-27/163, DOR.FIN.REC.No.137/03.10.001/2026-27. [EFFECTIVE:2026-06-24]
- **Paragraph 24 (substituted):** “The Upper Layer shall consist of NBFCs having asset size of **₹1,00,000 crore and above** as per the latest audited balance sheet for the financial year.”
- RBI still **identifies the UL list annually** (para 12 as substituted). The *criterion* is now a single number.
- That ₹1 lakh-crore threshold is to be **reviewed every three years** (para 31 as substituted).
- Government-owned NBFCs can now sit in UL (ownership-neutral). Fully government-owned ULs get a listing / some disclosure carve-out; that is irrelevant to MFL.
- **Middle Layer** remains the default for NBFCs with assets **₹1,000 crore and above** that are **not** on the UL list. Activity-based names (HFC, MFI, Factor, IFC, CIC, IDF, ARC) are **at least ML** even if they are small.
- **Base Layer** is below ₹1,000 crore (and the Type I / no-public-funds / no-customer-interface pocket, which is a separate deregistration story — taught Friday).
- **Top Layer** is still empty. If RBI ever parks someone there, para 6(5) of the Capital MD says the extra capital charge is **communicated specifically** — there is no published Top-Layer CRAR.

**2. The capital stack that binds — paragraph 6, live text**

Read para 6 in order. Gold-loan is **first**, not a footnote.

| Who | Minimum | Source |
|-----|---------|--------|
| NBFC **primarily engaged in lending against gold jewellery** (≥50% of financial assets) | **Tier 1 12%** and **CRAR 15%** of aggregate RWA. Tier 2 ≤ 100% of Tier 1. | Capital MD para **6(1)** |
| NBFC-**ML and above** (general) | **Tier 1 10%** of RWA | para **6(2)** |
| NBFC-**ML and above** (general) | **CRAR 15%** of RWA. Tier 2 ≤ 100% of Tier 1. | para **6(3)** |
| NBFC-**UL only** | **CET1 9%** of RWA **in addition** | para **6(4)** |
| Top Layer | Higher charge, told at classification | para **6(5)** |

Definitions that matter for MFL:

- **‘NBFC primarily engaged in lending against gold jewellery’** = gold-jewellery loans are **50% or more of financial assets** (Capital MD para 4(8)). That is an **asset mix** test, not a layer test. It can bind a BL, ML or UL gold-loan shop.
- **CRAR** = (Tier 1 + Tier 2) / RWA.
- **Tier 1** is going-concern capital (CET1-type items + Additional Tier 1 where eligible). For gold-loan MFL the **binding floor is 12%, not 10%**.
- **CET1 9%** is an **Upper Layer add-on**. ML does not have a published CET1 minimum in para 6(4). Do not put “CET1 9%” on an MFL dashboard as a regulatory minimum.
- There is **no 30% CRAR** for NBFCs. Bank Basel buffers are a different book. If a slide says 30%, it is wrong.

**3. What “gold-loan 12%” actually interacts with**

Para 6(1) and 6(2) sit together. For MFL:

- Gold jewellery ≥ 50% of financial assets → **12% Tier 1** is the floor, even though MFL is ML (where the generic floor is 10%).
- CRAR floor is **15%** either way (6(1) and 6(3) agree).
- So the **only gold-specific capital add-on versus a generic ML ICC** is **+200 bps of Tier 1**. The CRAR number does not move.
- If the gold share of financial assets ever fell **below 50%**, the Tier 1 floor would drop back to the generic ML 10% — that is a mix-shift, not a layer change. Flag it in ICAAP; do not assume it.

**4. Layer change is a capital *and* governance event**

Crossing **₹1,00,000 crore audited assets** does not wait for a friendly email. RBI still publishes the annual UL list, but the criterion is now mechanical. UL brings, among other things:

- CET1 9% (para 6(4))
- Core Investment Company / listing-style obligations for identified ULs (listing within the prescribed window — **Muthoot Finance** lives here; **MFL does not**)
- Heavier governance (independent-director mix, mandatory Board risk committee, tighter ALM)
- Large-exposure / LEF-style concentration (UL chapter of the Concentration MD)

MFL’s FP&A question is therefore not “are we UL today?” (no). It is “how much headroom to ₹1 lakh crore on the **audited** balance-sheet, and what CET1 rebuild would a flip cost?”

**Why It Matters**

Capital is the binding constraint on AUM growth, dividend, and rating. For a gold-loan ML:

- RWA on gold is relatively kind versus unsecured PL, so **headline CRAR looks comfortable** even when **Tier 1** is the tighter of the two floors (12% vs 15%). Watch **both**, not just CRAR.
- A mix shift into MSME / unsecured / MHFL on-book raises RWA density. Same equity, lower CRAR. That is an FP&A story, not a compliance afterthought.
- ICAAP is **mandatory for ML and above** (Capital MD Chapter V). The Board pack should show: (a) current CRAR and Tier 1 versus 15% / 12%; (b) gold-share of financial assets versus the 50% test; (c) audited-asset distance to ₹1,00,000 crore.
- Dividend: the Dividend MD still gates pay-outs on CRAR and NPA. A 15% CRAR that is only 50 bps above the floor is not a dividend story.

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Parameter | BL | ML (MFL) | UL (e.g. Muthoot Finance — **not MFL**) |
|-----------|----|----------|------------------------------------------|
| Asset-size gate | < ₹1,000 cr | ₹1,000 cr+ and not on UL list | **₹1,00,000 cr+** audited assets [EFFECTIVE:2026-06-24] |
| CRAR | Leverage, not CRAR (para 6 CRAR is ML+) | **15%** | **15%** |
| Tier 1 | — | **10%** generic; **12%** if gold jewellery ≥50% of financial assets | Same gold overlay; plus **CET1 9%** |
| CET1 9% | No | **No** | **Yes** (para 6(4)) |
| Tier 2 cap | — | ≤ 100% of Tier 1 | ≤ 100% of Tier 1 |
| NOF (standing) | ₹2 cr pocket / Type I story | ₹10 cr | ₹30 cr |
| Listing | No | No (unless separately listed for NCS/equity) | Yes, as identified UL |
