### 1. IBBI tells IPs: PG interim moratorium is off — including pending s.95 cases
- **What:** IBBI circularises Bombay HC + Delhi HC: the s.96 / s.124 interim moratorium on personal guarantors to corporate debtors **ceased from 26 May 2026**, and that cessation covers **applications already pending** on that date.
- **Issuer:** IBBI (not RBI / SEBI)
- **Date:** Circular dated **21 Sep 2026**. Underlying law: IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026, ss.96(4) and 124(4), **[EFFECTIVE:2026-05-26]**
- **Reference:** IBBI/II/106/2026 dated 21 September 2026
- **Class:** [CLARIFICATION] — not a new NBFC rule. The statute has been live since 26 May; this is IBBI telling insolvency professionals to stop treating pending PG filings as a shield.
- **In Plain English:** From 26 May 2026, filing (or having already filed) a personal-guarantor insolvency application under s.95 no longer freezes civil suits, arbitration, or other recovery against that guarantor. Two High Courts read the new s.96(4) as **retroactive to pending cases**. IBBI has now put that reading in a circular so IPs cannot hide behind “the application is still pending.” The lead Bombay HC case is *Tata Capital Financial Services Ltd v. Neel Motors LLP* (24 Jul 2026) — an **NBFC** won the point.
- **Impact on NBFCs:** If you hold a personal guarantee from a promoter / director of a corporate borrower, you can resume or continue arbitration / commercial suits against that guarantor even while a s.95 petition sits at NCLT. Do **not** stay your own recovery just because the IP says “moratorium.” This does **not** change IRAC, NPA clocks, or provisioning. It is a **recovery-rights** overlay.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** Gold-jewellery book is individual, collateral-first — PGs to corporate debtors are **not** the engine. Relevance is **MSME / LAP / any corporate exposure at MFL or MHFL** where a promoter PG sits behind the facility. Legal should pull the live list of (a) s.95 applications against MFL/MHFL PGs and (b) stayed arbitration / SARFAESI / civil recovery, and un-stay where the only bar was s.96. Gold-auction process is untouched.
- **Source:** https://ibbi.gov.in/uploads/whatsnew/778e13b82df050c20c0c13dfa3e8ea16.pdf

### Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning — the 90-day machine, and the 1 Oct SNFA overlay

**What is it?**

IRAC is the rulebook that answers three FP&A questions every month:

1. **When does a loan stop being “standard”?** (classification)
2. **When do we stop accruing interest into P&L?** (income recognition)
3. **How much of profit do we ring-fence?** (provisioning)

It is **not** the same as Ind AS 109 ECL. ECL is accounting. IRAC is the **prudential** floor that still drives RBI returns, PCA triggers, and the GNPA/NNPA numbers the Board actually stares at. MFL reports both; they must reconcile.

**The Technical Details**

**Source of truth for NBFCs (including HFCs, with HFC MD prevailing where inconsistent):**
*Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025* — RBI/DOR/2025-26/356, DOR.STR.REC series, 28 Nov 2025, updated 1 Jul 2026. Applies to **all layers**. Restructuring sits in a sibling MD: *RBI (NBFC – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Directions, 2025* (RBI/DOR/2025-26/357).

**1. The 90-day rule — same for BL, ML, UL**

An account is **NPA** when interest or principal is overdue **more than 90 days**. Classification is at **account level**, via a **day-end** process (paras 18–19; origin: DOR.STR.REC.68/21.04.048/2021-22 dated 12 Nov 2021). An operational lag in flagging is itself non-adherence.

The SMA ladder that sits *before* NPA (same 12 Nov 2021 circular, now in the MD):

| Bucket | Overdue | What it means |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Standard (current) | 0 days | Performing |
| SMA-0 | 1–30 days | First miss. Ind AS: often SICR / Stage 2 conversation starts at 30 DPD |
| SMA-1 | 31–60 days | Stress is visible |
| SMA-2 | 61–90 days | Last stop before NPA |
| **NPA / Sub-standard** | **>90 days** | Income recognition stops; specific provision starts |

Once NPA, the clock keeps running into **Doubtful**:

| Class | Age in NPA | Secured provision (ML/UL) |
|-------|------------|---------------------------|
| Sub-standard | 0–12 months in NPA | 15% secured / **25% unsecured** |
| Doubtful-1 | 12–24 months | 25% of secured portion |
| Doubtful-2 | 24–36 months | 40% of secured portion |
| Doubtful-3 | >36 months | 70% of secured portion |
| Unsecured portion of any doubtful | — | **100%** |
| Loss | identified as uncollectible | **100%** (or write off) |

**Upgradation:** only when **entire arrears across ALL facilities of that borrower** are repaid. Paying one gold loan while another facility of the same borrower is still overdue does **not** upgrade the first. Borrower-level, not product-level.

**2. Income recognition**

On a **standard** account, interest is accrued. On an **NPA**, further accrual does **not** hit P&L — it goes to interest suspense (para 12.2). Cash received after NPA can be recognised, subject to the MD’s appropriation rules. This is why GNPA outstanding = principal + overdue interest + capitalised charges, **excluding** post-NPA suspense.

**3. Standard-asset provision — the quiet P&L line**

| Book | Rate | In NNPA? |
|------|------|----------|
| ML/UL — all loans | **0.40%** of outstanding | **No** (para 55 ML / 58 UL) |
| Gold loans with **LTV > 75%** | **1%** | No |
| BL | 0.25% | No |

Never net the 0.40% into NNPA. NNPA = GNPA − **specific** provisions against NPAs only.

**4. GNPA / NNPA — MFL’s 7-step bridge (standalone)**

No Master Direction writes a formula. The regulatory ratio is on the **loan book / gross carrying amount**, not on AUM.

MFL standalone (do not mix with MHFL):

1. Gross AUM
2. − ARC sold
3. − DA & co-lending off-book
4. + accrued interest
5. − fee amortisation (Ind AS 109 EIR)
6. = **“net loans” / gross carrying amount** ← **denominator**
7. GNPA% = NPA outstanding ÷ that denominator. **Do not** deduct loss allowance from the denominator.

MHFL uses on-book AUM directly and skips accrual + amortisation — flagged for restatement on consolidation / audited tie-out.

Co-lending: partner share is never in the denominator (Co-Lending Arrangements Directions, 2025, para 28). Borrower-level SMA/NPA classification is **binding across all REs** — if the bank tags NPA, MFL tags NPA on its retained share.

**5. The 1 Oct overlay — SNFA (taught 15 Sep; here as the live countdown, not a re-teach)**

*RBI (NBFC IRAC) Third Amendment Directions, 2026* — **RBI/2026-27/197, DOR.STR.REC.162/21-04-048/2026-27, 16 Jul 2026**. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-10-01]**

New paras 40C–40D, Chapter II (all NBFCs):

- **40C.** Accrued but unrealised interest/charges on the **extinguished exposure** from periods *before* you acquired a Specified Non-Financial Asset **shall not** be recognised as income on acquisition. If you already booked it on SNFA stock outstanding as on **30 Sep 2026**, reverse through P&L **by 30 Sep 2027** to the extent still unrealised.
- **40D.** Cash from an SNFA = **non-interest / other income** in the year realised. Upkeep cost = expense in the year incurred.

**What SNFA is, in one line:** a non-financial asset (typically immovable property) taken onto the books in extinguishment of a loan — the SARFAESI / settlement leftover, not the gold packet in the vault.

**Gold jewellery pledged as collateral is not SNFA.** You never “acquire” the ornament as an asset of the company while the loan is live; you hold a pledge. Auction proceeds are recovery against the loan, not SNFA income.

**Why It Matters**

- GNPA/NNPA is the number rating agencies, lenders (bank lines, NCDs), and the Board use. Getting the **denominator** wrong is how a pack disagrees with the audited notes.
- The 90-day rule is **layer-blind**. Crossing into UL does not buy you a longer NPA clock.
- Standard-asset 0.40% is a **P&L** cost on the entire standard book. On a ₹X gold book, that is 40 bps of outstanding every year, before ECL.
- Gold LTV >75% steps standard provision to **1%**. LTV discipline is a provisioning lever, not just a conduct rule.
- SNFA from 1 Oct is a **P&L classification** issue (interest vs other income, and a 12-month reversal window for legacy accrual). It does not reclassify gold NPAs.

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Item | Number | Applies |
|------|--------|---------|
| NPA trigger | **>90 days** overdue | All layers |
| SMA-1 / SMA-2 | 31–60 / 61–90 | All |
| Standard provision (ML/UL) | **0.40%** | MFL |
| Gold LTV >75% standard | **1%** | Gold book |
| Sub-standard secured / unsecured | **15% / 25%** | ML/UL |
| Doubtful-1 / 2 / 3 secured | **25% / 40% / 70%** | ML/UL |
| Unsecured doubtful / Loss | **100%** | ML/UL |
| SNFA income rule | **1 Oct 2026** | All NBFCs |
| SNFA legacy reversal | **30 Sep 2027** | Stock as on 30 Sep 2026 |
| Upgradation | Full arrears, **all** facilities of the borrower | All |

**Recent Changes**

- **Previously:** no special IRAC paragraph for assets taken in extinguishment of a loan. Practice varied — some books left accrued interest sitting as “interest income” after the loan was swapped for property.
- **Now (from 1 Oct 2026):** paras 40C–40D. No interest income on acquisition; cash = other income when received; legacy unrealised interest on 30 Sep 2026 stock must be reversed by 30 Sep 2027.
- **Unchanged:** 90-day NPA, SMA ladder, ML 0.40% standard, gold >75% LTV at 1%, borrower-level upgrade.

**Muthoot Fincorp Context**

- Core gold book: pledged jewellery, LTV cap 75%. If LTV is inside the cap, standard provision is **0.40%**, not 1%. Slip above 75% and the extra 60 bps hits P&L on that slice.
- Gold NPA is still **90 days**, even on a bullet loan. A 12-month bullet that is 91 days past the due date is NPA — the “bullet” does not buy you a longer clock.
- Secured (gold) sub-standard = **15%**. That is why gold NPA is capital-light compared with unsecured personal loans (25% sub-standard, 100% once doubtful).

For each: (a) any unrealised accrued interest still in P&L? (b) is income being booked as interest? Gold packets are out of scope. MSME + MHFL are in scope.
