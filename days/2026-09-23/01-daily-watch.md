## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **SRG Housing Finance under NHB fraud lens — RFA flagged on CRILC.** The National Housing Bank has red-flagged ~₹300–400 crore of suspected fictitious loan accounts at the Udaipur-based affordable housing lender — roughly **one-third of its reported loan book** (AUM ₹1,076 crore as of June, +35% YoY). Per ET's investigation report (Sept 9): loans shown as disbursed where borrowers couldn't be verified, no corresponding assets, alleged **evergreening and NPA-record manipulation**, and funds allegedly routed to promoter-linked entities. NHB classified the exposure as a Red Flagged Account and reported it on RBI's CRILC (required within 7 days of classification; fraud/clean verdict due within 180 days). The company **denies all allegations**, says it received no NHB communication, and says a regulatory inspection is underway. Its Q1 numbers looked clean on the surface — PAT ₹8.47 cr (+25% YoY), GNPA 1.73%, CAR 39.21% — which is exactly why this story matters: reported asset quality can't be trusted until the NHB probe concludes. Shares crashed ~20% on the initial report (Sept 9–10). Sequel: Aviom (board superseded Jan 2025, now in insolvency) and Star Housing precedent. **Watch for Muthoot Fincorp relevance: this is the second small-HFC governance blow-up this year — NHB scrutiny of smaller lenders is clearly intensifying.** (ET, Sept 9; livehomes.in update Sept 22)

2. **ICRA upgrades Aadhar Housing Finance to [ICRA]AA+ (Stable)** — effective **September 22, 2026**, from AA with Positive outlook. Covers ₹2,231.63 cr NCDs, ₹1,350 cr long-term bank facilities, ₹60 cr sub-debt; A1+ reaffirmed on ₹600 cr CPs; ₹504.40 cr NCD ratings withdrawn on redemption. A clean credit-positive for one of India's largest affordable-housing financiers. (InvestyWise/company filing, Sept 22)

3. **Rate-hike risk is building — JM Financial flags faster funding-cost rise for NBFCs** (ET BFSI, Sept 22). Bank loans to NBFCs/HFCs grew ~27% in FY26 and 32% YoY in Q1 FY27 (vs just 6% in FY25); NBFCs' share of incremental bank credit jumped from ~5% to 14–15%. Yields are up 20–90 bps since June 2026 and 1-yr MCLR ~30 bps since March, with the market pricing ~50 bps of hikes. A 50 bps hike would hit RoA by −20 bps to +10 bps and EPS −8% to +7%. **Gold lenders (fixed-rate books, incl. Muthoot-model NBFCs), vehicle financiers and MFIs are the most exposed; prime HFCs (LIC HF, PNB HF, BHFL) would actually benefit.** Also relevant: 10–60% of outstanding NCDs come up for repricing in FY27/FY28.

4. **Moneyview's ₹1,092-cr IPO: anchor book opens TODAY (Sept 23), public issue Sept 24–28, listing ~Oct 1.** Price band ₹32–34; ₹750 cr fresh + ₹342 cr OFS. FY26 revenue ₹3,351 cr, PAT ₹242 cr; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,065 cr, PAT ₹174 cr. AUM ₹22,520 cr (June 30), disbursals +31% YoY (₹23,099 cr FY26), ~80% of customers from Tier 2/3 cities, 75% of book via LSP partners. The DLG question is the key risk — company cites RBI's 5% DLG cap and AI underwriting (~1 lakh data variables/borrower). ₹325 cr of proceeds go to lending ops (incl. DLG deployment), ₹250 cr to shore up its NBFC subsidiary. First big consumer-fintech-NBFC IPO in a while — barometer for the digital lending space.

5. **RBI cancellations + deregistration deadline.** RBI cancelled CoRs of **5 NBFCs** under Section 45-IA(6) — Dar's Financial Services, Rolta Holding and Finance Corporation, Vasudeo Securities, Parsoli Corporation, and A.C. Choksi Financial Services (now Deus Financial Capital) — and removed **8 more NBFCs** following voluntary CoR surrender. Separately, the voluntary deregistration window under the new NBFC Registration/Exemptions framework (PRAVAAH portal) **closes September 30, 2026** — 7 days away (legal500; Akand Partners digest, Sept 21).

6. **Supreme Court orders Cholamandalam to pay ~₹15 lakh** to a truck owner whose vehicle was seized by recovery agents at midnight and sold without the notice required under its own loan agreement and RBI guidelines (ET, Sept 22). HC dismissal on delay set aside; **RBI directed to ensure its recovery guidelines are actually enforced.** Timely reminder as festive-season collections ramp up — recovery-code compliance is now a litigation risk, not just a compliance checkbox.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Housing Finance (Wednesday rotation)

**State of play:** Home-loan rates advertised from ~7% (PSBs lead the lower end; HFCs follow). Housing is the single largest retail credit block (₹44.4 lakh crore vs gold at ₹18.6 lakh crore), and our earlier gold-loan data shows housing still #1.

1. **NHB scrutiny has teeth now.** The SRG Housing episode (see URGENT #1) follows Aviom and Star Housing. NHB is red-flagging, using CRILC, and small HFCs with double-digit growth + thin governance are in the crosshairs. **Takeaway for anyone lending to/small-HFC counter-parties: verify accounting quality, not just reported GNPAs.**
2. **Long-term funding squeeze for smaller HFCs.** NHB can fund only ~35% of an HFC's incremental portfolio; bank money is short/medium-tenor; bond access is gated by rating (AA+ and above fine, per India Ratings' Jinay Gala). The RMBS route (RMBS Development Company Ltd) has been used **only twice**. Motilal Oswal Home Finance MD Sukesh Bhowal wants insurers/pension funds + tax incentives in affordable-housing funding. Expect more capital-markets innovation (RMBS, DFI lines) for affordable HFCs.
3. **Prime HFCs = rate-hike beneficiaries.** JM Financial: LIC HF, PNB HF, BHFL (70–100% floating-rate books) could see RoA gains from repricing if RBI hikes; NCD-heavy/higher-refinancing names (Tata Capital HFC, LTF, Aavas, HFFC, Fusion) more sensitive. RBI's draft norms are also squeezing pricing flexibility for prime HFCs (ET BFSI) — watch the final rules.
4. **Ratings health mixed.** Upgrades flowing (Aadhar Housing→AA+ stable; PNB Housing Buy-rating), CRISIL reaffirmed GIC HF (AA+/Stable) though GNPA rose to 4.5% after daily-delinquency recognition — accounting-rule changes are inflating reported NPAs across the industry (same effect at Muthoot Housing, 2.6%).
5. **Growth leaders:** Bajaj Housing FY26 AUM ₹1.41 lakh cr (+22%+), GNPA 0.27%, AAA/Stable; Can Fin Homes FY26 PAT ₹1,085.8 cr, RoMA 2.5%; ICICI Home Finance (ICRA AAA/Stable, bank lines raised to ₹22,000 cr) all reinforcing the "scale + parent support wins" narrative.
