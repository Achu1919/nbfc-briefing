# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Wednesday, 23 September 2026 (Published 24 Sep)

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

- **Rate-hike playbook lands — and gold is in the line of fire.** JM Financial's thematic report *"The Rate Hike Playbook for NBFC/HFCs"* (Sep 23) warns that a potential RBI repo hike will hit NBFCs very unevenly. **Direct relevance to Muthoot Fincorp: JM Financial explicitly names "MFI/vehicle/Gold financiers" as the cohort "most exposed"** to a hike, because a large share of their loan books is fixed-rate while their borrowing is bank-loan-heavy and fast-repricing. The report estimates a **50-bps repo hike** swings NBFC/HFC return-on-assets by **−20 bps to +10 bps** and FY28 EPS by **−8% to +7%**. Yields on G-secs, corporate bonds and NBFC borrowings are already up **20–90 bps since June 2026**, and bank funding to NBFCs/HFCs jumped **~32% YoY in Q1 FY27** (vs just 6% in FY25) — so a hike would feed into cost of funds across a far larger share of the book than before. Housing financiers are the biggest beneficiaries. **JM's top NBFC picks:** Aditya Birla Capital, Piramal Finance, PNB Housing Finance, Aadhar Housing Finance, Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services, Cholamandalam Investment, CreditAccess Grameen, Five Star Business Finance and Fedfina. [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/which-nbfcs-will-gain-the-most-from-rate-hike-cycle-jm-financial-lists-top-picks/articleshow/134386955.cms) | [ANI/JM](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/expected-rise-interest-rates-may-hit-nbfc-profits-121.htm)

- **U.S. Fed kick-started the global tightening cue.** The Fed hiked its benchmark rate **25 bps last week — its first hike since 2023 — to 3.75–4%**, and officials expect one more hike this year; the 10-yr Treasury yield has crossed **5%**. This is the macro backdrop driving India's rate-hike expectations. Indian NBFC stocks have already fallen **up to 9% over the past month** on this anticipation. [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/which-nbfcs-will-gain-the-most-from-rate-hike-cycle-jm-financial-lists-top-picks/articleshow/134386955.cms)

- **Jefferies: loan demand healthy, asset quality resilient — but rate risk is the watch-word.** Based on talks with **nine NBFCs at the Jefferies India Forum 2026** (Sep 23), demand is strong across vehicle, consumer, MSME and festive-season lending, with "strong Q2 growth and asset quality trends"; credit costs could "surprise positively." **Shriram Finance guided AUM growth of ~17% in FY27 and 18–20% in FY28–29**, and reiterated credit-cost guidance of 2%; **Aditya Birla Capital** guided 25% annualised growth over FY26–29 with credit cost of 1–1.1%. NIMs expected range-bound in FY27, cushioned by loan repricing and mix shifts. [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-see-healthy-loan-demand-resilient-asset-quality-rate-hikes-may-lift-funding-costs-jefferies/134429899)
