### Recovery of loan dues and engagement of recovery agencies — the 1 Jan 2027 machine, and why you cannot wait for it

**What is it?**

RBI split “how you lend” from “how you collect.” Product rules (LTV, EMI, IRAC) sit in Credit Facilities / IRAC Directions. **How staff and outsourced collectors behave** sits in the **Responsible Business Conduct (RBC) Directions**. Until August 2026 that was a short “recovery agents” subsection. RBI then replaced it with a full code covering **own employees and outsourced agencies**, including **taking possession of security**. For a gold NBFC, “possession of security” is the auction packet. For MHFL, it is the SARFAESI / hypothecated asset.

Two clocks, not one:

1. **RBI code** — *RBI (NBFCs – Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026*, **RBI/2026-27/230, DOR.MCS.REC.No.199/01-01-039/2026-27 dated 6 Aug 2026**, `[EFFECTIVE:2026-01-01]`.
2. **Supreme Court overlay** — 16 Sep 2026 Chola repossession judgment. **Already binding.** Waiting for the January circular to “go live” is the wrong read.

**The Technical Details**

**Parent MD:** *RBI (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025* — RBI/DOR/2025-26/362, DOR.MCS.REC.No.281/01-01-039/2025-26, 28 Nov 2025, updated 1 Jul 2026. Applies to **all layers** of NBFC-D / ICC / Factor / MFI / IFC / IDF / **HFC**. Carve-outs: CIC, AA, SPD, NOFHC, MGC, and NBFCs with **no customer interface**. MFL (ML, gold, customer interface) and MHFL (HFC) are **in**.

The 6 Aug amendment **deletes** old paras 26–27, 89–97 and 98–100 (the old “Responsibilities of Recovery Agents” section) and inserts **Section J (paras 100A onwards)**.

**1. Who is a recovery agency / agent — nomenclature does not save you**

- **Recovery agency (para 6(11A)):** any entity or individual **other than the NBFC’s own employees** engaged under an outsourcing arrangement to assist in recovery of dues from a borrower in default, **including taking possession of a security** — whatever you call the contract.
- **Recovery agent (para 6(11B)):** the person at the **customer interface**.
- If you hire an **individual** directly, **both** agency and agent rules apply to that person.
- **Own employees** who do recovery / possession are inside Section J (para 100C). Branch gold staff who handle overdue packets are not “outside” because they are on payroll.

**2. Board policy is the first artefact (100D–100G)**

The Board-approved collection / recovery policy must cover, at minimum:

| Must sit in the policy | Why FP&A / ops care |
|------------------------|---------------------|
| Triggers for starting recovery | Stops “every SMA-0 gets a visit” |
| Graded escalation matrix | Documented steps before possession / auction |
| Code of conduct for staff **and** agents | Same standard, two populations |
| Demise of borrower | Gold packets of deceased pledgors |
| Pre-escalation engagement + resolution options | Distress cases cannot jump straight to auction |
| Agency due diligence, performance, audit, penalties | Outsourcing MD cross-ref (id=12941) |
| **Compensation** to borrower / guarantor for non-compliant recovery (100F) | P&L contingency — this is how Chola-style awards get paid |
| Tech-based recovery (100G / 100S) | Device lock / app restrictions, if any — gold book mostly N/A |

**3. Engagement rules that cost money (100H–100N)**

- Due diligence = **Outsourcing Directions, 2025**, plus **antecedent verification** at hire and on a **policy-defined cycle**.
- **IIBF Debt Recovery Agent certificate** is mandatory. Agents already on the books get **one year from 1 Jan 2027** (i.e. **by 1 Jan 2028**) to obtain it. New hires: certificate **before** customer interface.
- Website must list every empanelled / engaged agency: type, address, period, purpose (recovery vs possession). Update within **7 calendar days** of any change; **promptly** on termination.
- **One-day prior intimation** to borrower / guarantor before the **first in-person visit**. Change of agency, or termination: **immediate** notice so the borrower does not keep dealing with a fired agent.

**4. Conduct at the door (live from 1 Jan 2027; SC overlay live now)**

The code (own staff + agents) is the operational heart. The established reading of Section J, already on this desk:

| Constraint | Rule |
|------------|------|
| Calling / visit hours | **08:00–19:00** |
| Call recording | Retain **6 months** |
| Privacy | Disclose borrower data to agents only as needed to collect (100O); penalise misuse |
| Harassment / public humiliation / intimidation | Prohibited (this is also what the SC punished in Chola) |
| Taking possession | Only per statute + this code; **not** a freelance field decision |

**Previously:** a thin “recovery agents of the NBFC” subsection (paras 98–100) plus FPC. **Now (from 1 Jan 2027):** a full Section J that treats **employees and agencies as one conduct universe**, adds IIBF, website disclosure, 1-day visit notice, compensation, and an explicit possession-of-security perimeter.

**5. The Supreme Court overlay — do not calendar this to January**

The 16 Sep 2026 judgment against Cholamandalam (forcible repossession / recovery conduct) is **already law**. RBI has **not** issued a companion circular. That does not freeze enforcement. A gold auction done without proper notice, or a field recovery that looks like Chola, can attract **court-ordered compensation today**, on top of whatever RBI writes in January. Para 100B of the amendment says Section J is **without prejudice** to statutory enforcement rights (SARFAESI, pledge, etc.) — it does **not** license extra-statutory possession.

**Why It Matters**

Conduct failures do not hit CRAR the way NPA does. They hit **franchise, Ombudsman, Board minutes, and cash compensation**. RBI’s CoR cancellations of 17 Sep were mostly coercive-recovery stories, not CRAR breaches. For FP&A: (a) IIBF + recording + website list are **opex and vendor-capacity** items for Q3/Q4 FY27; (b) 100F compensation is a **contingent P&L** line that should be in the recovery-policy paper the Board will pass before 1 Jan; (c) gold-auction **notice discipline** is the MFL-specific control — LTV was yesterday’s class; **how you sell the packet** is today’s.
