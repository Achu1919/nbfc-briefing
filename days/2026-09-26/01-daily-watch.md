# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Friday, 25 September 2026

*Edition for 26 September 2026 (Saturday). Focus: Infrastructure & project finance.*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **Carlyle + Aditya Puri take control of Nido Home Finance (Edelweiss) — ₹2,000+ crore control deal consummated Friday (Sep 25).** A Carlyle affiliate (CA Sardo Investments) and Salisbury Investments (Aditya Puri & family's vehicle) closed their purchase of a **45% stake in Nido from Edelweiss Financial Services for ₹580.68 crore**, plus a **primary infusion of ₹1,446.14 crore** (2.59 cr equity shares + 5.18 cr warrants). Buyers now hold **58.26%** of Nido, rising to **72.7%** after warrant conversion within 18 months. Nido has quit the Edelweiss fold. This is a defining control transaction in affordable housing finance — new owners get clean control, Edelweiss cuts debt and stays at ~38.9% (25.46% fully diluted). *Why it matters:* signals continued M&A appetite in the HFC space and validates affordable-housing asset quality.

2. **IRDAI insurance-distribution overhaul triggers a historic sell-off in financial/distribution names — PB Fintech (Policybazaar) crashed 36% Thursday, kept sliding ~5% Friday.** Not an NBFC, but the market shock hit lenders and distributors with loan/insurance crossover (credit-life, bancassurance). IRDAI's consultation paper caps commissions (health new business 15–20% for entities vs 20% for agents; renewals 5–10%; term first-year 5–20% depending on tenor; motor OD heavily cut). Brokerages slashed targets: HSBC cut PB Fintech to ₹1,150 (from ₹2,100), Motilal ₹1,150 (from ₹1,820), BofA ₹1,410, Jefferies ₹1,540. ~₹31,400 crore of market cap wiped in one day. Watch any NBFC with material distribution income / credit-life business — *same regulatory-draught theme as the RBI revolving-credit clampdown affecting various NBFC segments.*

3. **JM Financial: rate-hike/bond-yield cycle is splitting the sector — HFCs gain, vehicle & MFI lenders face headwinds (this week's key strategy call).** With bank loans' share of NBFC liabilities rising, higher policy rates and soaring bond yields feed into cost of funds faster and across a broader book. JM flags **LIC Housing Finance and PNB Housing Finance as NIM winners**; vehicle financiers and microfinance lenders carry the downside. NOTE: this means higher funding costs pressure gold/precious-metal and CV/MFI-heavy books (relevant given Muthoot Microfin's margin sensitivity).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Infrastructure & Project Finance (Saturday)

- **PFC–REC merger (biggest PSU-NBFC consolidation):** boards approved the scheme; **REC merges into PFC** at **88:100**. Combined loan book **>₹11 lakh crore** — REC ~₹5.82 lakh cr + PFC ~₹11.51 lakh cr, with intercompany overlap; to be India's largest NBFC and a dominant power/infrastructure financier. Surviving entity must stay a government company; needs statutory/government/creditor/shareholder approval. *Bearish view (CFA/analysts):* deepens **single-sector (power) concentration**, single-borrower/sector limits bind faster, and IMF has flagged stability risks in power-heavy state NBFCs. *Bullish view:* scale, capital optimisation, lower duplication, stronger capacity to finance renewables/transmission/energy-transition and infrastructure. This is the marquee infra-finance event.
- **NaBFID scaling up:** targeting **~₹1 lakh crore fundraising in FY27**, **30–40% from overseas** (riding RBI's concessional OFCB/ECB swap window open till Dec 2026); loan-book target **₹5 lakh crore by FY29–30**. Hosted Infrastructure Conclave 2026 (launched **NaBFID–BCG report**): India needs **₹168–172 trillion** infra investment through FY31, of which **₹78–80 trillion** is not yet in announced pipelines; only 34–36% of demand sits in proven bankable sectors; residual annual gap ₹2–3 trillion; three routes flagged — **partial credit enhancement (PCB/institutional bonds), InvITs for capital recycling, AIFs + private credit**.
- **RECPDCL SPV handovers (Sep 23):** **Barmer HVDC** transmission SPV (Rajasthan REZ Ph-IV Part-5, 6 GW solar, LCC) transferred to **POWERGRID** (annual transmission charges ₹3,244.33 cr); **Sakoli 400/220/132kV AIS** (Maharashtra) to **Ashoka Buildcon** (~₹126.5 cr/yr over 35 yrs) — shows the TBCB transmission pipeline feeding project-finance demand.
- **Project-finance provisioning regime (RBI, eff. Oct 2025):** construction-phase provisioning cut to **1%** (CRE 1.25%, CRE-residential 1%, operational projects 0.4%, CRE-RH 0.75%); existing projects exempt — a structural tailwind for PFC/REC/IRFC margins. PFC/REC shares had jumped ~6% on the release.
- **IRFC / DVC:** explored financial support for upcoming DVC projects (IRFC CMD visit, Sep).
- **Takeaway for NBFC watchers:** infra/PFC financing is consolidating toward large state-backed and DFI-led balance sheets; private/institutional capital (insurance, pensions, private credit) is being courted for the ₹168–172 trn gap. The PFC–REC tie-up, NaBFID's ₹5 lakh cr goal, and the RBI project-provisioning relief together define the infra-finance landscape as FY27 begins. *(Nifty NBFC/infra exposure: REC, PFC, IRFC all sit in NBFC-UL.)*
