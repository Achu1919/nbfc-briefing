### Gold-loan capital stack: CRAR 15% / Tier 1 12% / no CET1 9% (ML)

**What is it?**

MFL is a **Middle Layer** gold-loan NBFC. That is two overlays sitting on the same number, not one number.

1. **Layer overlay (SBR):** every NBFC-ML must hold **CRAR 15%** and **Tier 1 10%** of risk-weighted assets, on an ongoing basis.
2. **Activity overlay (gold):** an NBFC *primarily engaged in lending against gold jewellery* — gold jewellery loans ≥ **50% of financial assets** — must hold **Tier 1 12%** and **CRAR 15%**. Tier 2 cannot exceed 100% of Tier 1.

The gold overlay is **stricter on Tier 1**. It is **not** a 15% vs 15% story. The extra 2 percentage points of Tier 1 is the live constraint for a gold book. Upper Layer adds a third overlay — **CET1 9%** — which does **not** apply to MFL.

**The Technical Details**

**SBR layers (current, not the 2021 scoring model)**

UL test substituted by **RBI/2026-27/163**, DOR.FIN.REC.No.137/03.10.001/2026-27, **24 Jun 2026**. `[EFFECTIVE:2026-06-24]`

| Layer | Who is in it | Capital overlay |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Base (BL)** | ND-NBFCs with assets **< ₹1,000 cr**; plus P2P, AA, NOFHC, Type I CoR | Leverage ≤ **7×** owned funds. **Exception:** gold-jewellery BL still takes para 6(1) CRAR/T1. |
| **Middle (ML)** | All **NBFC-D**; ND-NBFCs with assets **≥ ₹1,000 cr** and **not** in the annual UL list; SPD and IDF-NBFC always ML; CIC / HFC / IFC are ML or UL, never BL | CRAR **15%**, T1 **10%**. Gold overlay if ≥50% gold jewellery. |
| **Upper (UL)** | Identified **annually** by RBI. Test is now **audited assets ≥ ₹1,00,000 cr** as at the latest FY balance sheet. Scoring metric **repealed**. Threshold reviewed every **3 years**. | CRAR 15% + T1 10% **plus CET1 9%**. Gold overlay still 12% T1 if the book qualifies. |
| **Top (TL)** | Ideally empty. RBI can lift a UL name into TL if systemic risk jumps. | Higher capital, communicated case-by-case. |

Previously (until 24 Jun 2026): UL used a parametric + qualitative score, and the top-10 by size sat in UL automatically. Now (from 24 Jun 2026): one number — **₹1 lakh crore audited assets**. Muthoot Finance is the UL gold-loan *peer*. MFL is **not** that company and is **not** UL.

**Capital minima (the actual paragraphs)**

Source: *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025*, RBI/DOR/2025-26/345, DOR.CAP.REC.264/21-01-002/2025-26, 28 Nov 2025, updated **1 Jul 2026**. Chapter II, para 6.

| Para | Rule | Who |
|------|------|-----|
| **6(1)** | T1 **12%** + CRAR **15%**. T2 ≤ 100% of T1. | NBFC **primarily engaged in lending against gold jewellery** (gold jewellery loans ≥ **50% of financial assets** — definition, para 4(8)). Layer-agnostic. |
| **6(2)** | T1 **10%** of RWA. | NBFC-ML **and above**. |
| **6(3)** | CRAR **15%** of RWA. T2 ≤ 100% of T1. | NBFC-ML **and above**. |
| **6(4)** | CET1 **9%** of RWA. | **NBFC-UL only.** |
| **6(5)** | Higher capital charge, specified at classification. | Top Layer. |
| **17** | Leverage (outside liabilities / owned fund) **≤ 7**. | **NBFC-BL only.** Paras 6(1), 6(2) and 17 do **not** apply to NBFC-MFI. |

**How the two T1 floors interact for MFL:** para 6(2) says ML T1 ≥ 10%. Para 6(1) says gold-primary T1 ≥ 12%. MFL takes the **higher** of the two — **12%**. CRAR is 15% under both 6(1) and 6(3), so CRAR does not move. CET1 9% is **UL-only**; do not put it in an ML ICAAP as a regulatory minimum.

**What counts as capital (short version, ML / gold BL)**

- **Tier 1** for an NBFC-BL-gold and an NBFC-ML is owned-fund style T1 (para 10): paid-up equity, compulsorily convertible preference, free reserves (quarterly profits only after limited review/audit, haircut for average dividend of last 3 years), share premium, capital reserves from asset sales — minus accumulated losses, intangibles, deferred revenue expenditure. ROU on a **tangible** leased asset is **not** deducted (para 9 note; also CET1 para 8(1)(iii)).
- **CET1** (para 7–8) is the UL building block: equity, premium, statutory reserves, disclosed free reserves, retained earnings, optional revaluation reserves at **55% discount** if own-use property conditions are met. Deductions: goodwill/intangibles, DTA, group investments/advances above 10% of owned fund, impairment reserve, own shares. **Impairment reserve is never CET1 and never T2 (para 8(4) and 16(8)).**
- **Current-year profit** can come into CET1 / owned fund **quarterly** only if audited or limited-reviewed, reduced by `0.25 × D × t` where D = average dividend of last 3 years. Current-year **loss** is deducted in full. That is the Q2 pack rule.

**Risk weights that actually hit a gold book**

Do **not** use the old “35–50% gold LTV” shortcut — it is **not** in the 2025 Capital Adequacy Directions.

Para 18(1) table:

- Cash / bank balances / approved G-secs: **0%**
- Other **secured** loans considered good: **100%** (sr. 3(e))
- Consumer credit *retail*, **excluding** housing, education, vehicle, **loans against gold jewellery**, and MFI/SHG: **125%** (sr. 3(e)(i))
- Credit-card receivables: **125%**

Gold jewellery loans are carved **out** of the 125% consumer-credit bucket. They sit in the general secured bucket unless a more specific row applies. **Plan RWA at 100% of gold AUM** unless credit risk has a documented, MD-cited exception. Unsecured / general consumer is the expensive 125% book — that is MSME clean and personal-loan, not gold.

ECLGS 5.0 got a narrow 0% RW on 75% of the guaranteed portion (Third Amendment, **RBI/2026-27/136**, 16 Jun 2026). Irrelevant to gold.

**Why It Matters**

CRAR is the binding growth governor. For a gold NBFC, **Tier 1 12%** binds before CRAR 15% does, because T2 is capped at 100% of T1 — you cannot “fill” a 15% CRAR with cheap T2 if T1 is only 10%. A 12% T1 floor plus T2 ≤ T1 implies you can get to 15% CRAR with 3 points of T2, but you **cannot** run T1 at the generic ML 10% and make up the rest with subordinated debt.

ICAAP (Chapter V of the same MD) is **mandatory for ML and above**. That is the document where concentration (gold price, geography, LTV), ALM gaps, and operational risk get a capital add-on **above** the 12/15 floors. Do not treat 12/15 as the ICAAP answer.
