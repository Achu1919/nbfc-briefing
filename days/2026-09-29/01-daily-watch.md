# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Tuesday, 29 September 2026

*Covering events of Monday, 28 September 2026, and overnight updates.*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **Markets crashed hard on Sept 28** — the Sensex plunged **1,124 points (1.52%)** to close at **72,771.72**, while the Nifty fell **360 points (1.56%)** to **22,780.25**, slipping below the 22,800 level to its **lowest point since April**. Bank and financial stocks were among the worst hit, with HDFC Bank, Reliance and ICICI Bank the key drags. *(Reuters/BusinessLine/India TV)*
2. **FIIs recorded their biggest September outflow** — foreign investors net sold **₹5,353 crore** on the day (largest single-session outflow since Aug 31's ₹7,986 cr), while DIIs bought ₹5,189 crore. FIIs have now been net sellers in **14 of 19 sessions this month**, taking September's outflow to roughly **₹23,885 crore**. *(Moneycontrol)*
3. **MUFG to buy 20% of Shriram Finance for ₹39,618 crore** — the largest cross-border investment ever in India's financial sector (announced 25 Sept, pending shareholder approval). Follows Shriram's upgrade to AAA by CRISIL/ICRA/India Ratings in April after MUFG's strategic equity investment. *(India Business Journal)*

## 📈 Market & Stock Summary

- **Catalysts for the crash:** escalating **Iran-US conflict** spiking **crude oil prices**, US Treasury yields surging (10-yr hit **5.24% — a 19-year high**), and continued **FII outflows**. Every intraday recovery was sold into. *(ET Now, CNBC TV18)*
- **Wealth erosion:** investors lost roughly **₹6–8.9 lakh crore** in market value on the day. *(TOI, 5paisa)*
- **PSU bank stocks were under particular pressure** on concerns that persistent inflation could limit RBI easing and even raise the odds of a **rate hike at the October policy meeting**. *(Moneycontrol)*
- **IFCI and NIACL fell up to 4%** as NSE's stock (4.4% held via SHCIL) slipped below its IPO price on a weak debut amid the broader sell-off. *(Moneycontrol liveblog)*
- **NBFC movers on the day:** Cholamandalam fell roughly ₹28.5 to ~₹1,634 (-1.7%); Bajaj Finance -1.51%, Shriram Finance -2.62%, Jio Financial Services -2.78%. Chola is now ~11% lower over the past month. *(Bajaj Finserv, Livemint)*
- **A three-day nationwide bank strike (28–30 Sept) was deferred** after IBA and the United Forum of Bank Unions reached an understanding to continue talks over the five-day banking week — removing a near-term operational risk for PSU banks and month-end transactions. *(Ventura daily)*
- **US markets also fell overnight** (Nasdaq -~1%) as Treasury yields surged and the Iran conflict rumbled on — President Trump rejected an Iranian proposal to end the war, with more talks expected this week. *(TheStreet, WSJ)*

## 📉 NPA & Asset Quality

- **RBI's Specified Non-Financial Asset (SNFA) norms kick in from 1 Oct 2026** — NBFCs must set disposal limits (max 7 years) for immovable assets acquired against claims, with legacy SNFAs to be brought into compliance by 30 Sept 2027. Sets clearer accounting for stressed-asset sale.
- **Purple Finance's ₹15.5 cr NPA-portfolio acquisition** (under RBI's credit-risk-transfer master direction) shows continued consolidation/trading of stressed micro-loan assets.
- Microfinance sector stress is easing but **Sa-Dhan still expects pressure to persist for another 2–3 quarters** and supports continued lending guardrails as the industry recovers. *(Sa-Dhan, Apr 2026)*

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — **Gold Loan Sector** (Tuesday rotation)

Gold loans are the **fastest-growing segment of India's retail credit** and are reshaping how NBFCs build their books:

- **Triple-digit growth:** RBI sectoral credit data shows gold loans expanded **~105% year-on-year in the 12 months to May 2026**, with NBFCs leading the charge; gold-loan growth for FY26 is put around **84% YoY** — even as housing loan growth cooled. *(Multibagg/ET, Maliktimes)*
- **NBFCs gaining share:** lenders' gold-loan sourcing value jumped sharply in FY26, with NBFCs taking a bigger slice of an expanding market as borrowers favoured collateral-backed credit. *(NewsD)*
- **Branches:** the sector plans ~**3,000 new gold-focused branches over the next 12 months** — described as the biggest annual branch addition in recent times. *(Times Now)*
- **Pricing & demand tailwind:** retail gold prices have run to roughly **₹1.5–1.54 lakh per 10g (24K)** in the current week, lifting loanable values; higher gold prices plus resilient credit demand underpin growth.
- **Regulatory tailwind (context):** last year's RBI **final gold-loan guidelines** (LTV caps, hallmarking, transparency) were seen as a positive for organised lenders — Morgan Stanley called Muthoot and Manappuram key gainers. *(Moneycontrol)*
- **Profitability:** CRISIL projects robust **4.25–4.5% RoA** for gold-loan NBFCs through FY26–FY27 on high demand, low credit costs and strong loan growth. *(ET BFSI)*
- **Why it matters for you (Muthoot Fincorp):** the co-lender/gold-book expansion and the Kanakadurga gold-book acquisition (Godrej) both signal intensifying competition, while the ₹1.5L+/10g gold price environment directly supports LTV headroom and growth. Keep an eye on the 1 Oct SNFA norms and the upcoming Q2 results season.
