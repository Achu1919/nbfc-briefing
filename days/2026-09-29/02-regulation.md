### Q2 IRAC close: denominator, ECL bridge, and the 1 Oct SNFA switch

**What is it?**

Two clocks hit this week, and they are not the same clock.

1. **Tomorrow (30 Sep 2026)** — Q2 FY27 ends. GNPA, NNPA, SMA, IRAC provisions and the Ind AS 109 ECL number all freeze on the **day-end classification** of 30 Sep. That is also the **stock-take date** in IRAC para 40C for Specified Non-Financial Assets.
2. **Thursday (1 Oct 2026)** — *Reserve Bank of India (NBFC – IRAC) Third Amendment Directions, 2026* goes live. New SNFA *acquisitions* from that date cannot take pre-takeover accrued interest into P&L. Legacy stock gets a year (to 30 Sep 2027) to reverse what is still unrealised.

IRAC answers three FP&A questions. ECL answers a fourth. Mixing them is how Q2 notes fail the auditor’s tie-out.

**The Technical Details**

**Source of truth:** *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025* — RBI/DOR/2025-26/356, DOR.STR.REC series, 28 Nov 2025 (updated 2026). **Applies to all layers.** HFCs: HFC MD prevails where inconsistent. Restructuring sits in the sibling MD *RBI (NBFC – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Directions, 2025* (RBI/DOR/2025-26/357).

**Third Amendment (the one that goes live Thursday):** RBI/2026-27/197, DOR.STR.REC.162/21-04-048/2026-27 dated **16 Jul 2026**. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-10-01]**. Inserts paras **40C and 40D**.

**1. Classification does not freeze on the Board pack date — it freezes at day-end 30 Sep**

NPA = overdue **>90 days**, account-level, **day-end process** (paras 18–19; origin DOR.STR.REC.68/21.04.048/2021-22 dated 12 Nov 2021). An operational lag in flagging is itself non-adherence. If LMS and the trial balance disagree at 30 Sep close, the LMS day-end DPD wins for classification; the TB wins for the rupee outstanding. Reconcile both *today*.

Upgradation is **borrower-level**: entire arrears across **all** facilities of that borrower. A gold-loan EMI caught up while an MSME facility of the same PAN is still overdue does **not** upgrade the gold loan.

**2. The Q2 denominator — MFL standalone 7-step (do not mix with MHFL)**

No Master Direction writes a GNPA% formula. The regulatory ratio is on **gross carrying amount** (loan book), not AUM.

| Step | What | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| 1 | Gross AUM | Starting point for investor decks |
| 2 | − ARC sold | Derecognised. Not in the book. |
| 3 | − DA & co-lending off-book | Partner share was never MFL’s asset (CLA 2025 para 28) |
| 4 | + uncollected accrued interest | On-book, in gross carrying amount |
| 5 | − unamortised net upfront fees | Ind AS 109 EIR netting |
| 6 | = **gross carrying amount** | **Denominator** |
| 7 | GNPA% = NPA outstanding ÷ that number | **Do not** deduct loss allowance from the denominator |

NNPA = GNPA − **specific** provisions against NPAs. The 0.40% standard-asset provision (ML para 55) is **not** netted. Gold loans with LTV >75% carry 1% standard provision — still not in NNPA.

**MHFL trap:** the housing subsidiary uses on-book AUM directly and skips steps 4 and 5. That will not tie to audited FS / RBI returns. Flag for consolidation restatement before the Q2 pack goes to the Board.

**Co-lending:** if the partner bank tags the borrower NPA, MFL tags NPA on the retained share. Classification is binding across REs (CLA 2025 para 28). Partner share never enters the denominator.

**Technical write-offs:** off-book for audited GNPA, still NPA for borrower tracking (12 Nov 2021 circular). Don’t drop them from AUM on a different date from the books.

**3. IRAC provision vs Ind AS 109 ECL — the Q2 note the auditor will ask for**

| | IRAC (prudential) | Ind AS 109 ECL (accounting) |
|--|-------------------|------------------------------|
| What it is | Floor for RBI returns, PCA, GNPA/NNPA | P&L loss allowance in the FS |
| Trigger | DPD buckets + secured/unsecured | 12-month vs lifetime PD, SICR, Stage 1/2/3 |
| Standard book | 0.40% (ML/UL); 1% if gold LTV >75% | Stage 1 (12-m ECL) / Stage 2 (lifetime, still performing) |
| NPA book | 15/25/40/70/100% slabs | Stage 3 lifetime ECL |
| Income | Stop accrual on NPA (para 12.2 → suspense) | Effective-interest on amortised cost, credit-impaired |
| Disclosure | FS MD DOR.ACC.REC.No.46/21.04.018/2021-22, Appendix II-A **IRAC ↔ ECL bridge** | Same note, other direction |

The Board stares at GNPA/NNPA. The P&L stares at ECL. If ECL < IRAC specific provision, you still hold the IRAC floor for regulatory returns; the FS shows ECL and the bridge explains the gap. **Never let the Q2 deck quote AUM-based GNPA as if it were the RBI ratio.**

**4. SNFA — what actually changes on 1 Oct (paras 40C / 40D)**

A Specified Non-Financial Asset is what you **take over against a claim** — typically immovable property via SARFAESI / decree — after the loan is extinguished. **Gold jewellery pledged as collateral is not an SNFA.** You do not “acquire” the gold; you hold it as security and auction it under the gold-loan chapter of the Credit Facilities MD.

From **1 Oct 2026**:

- **40C (new acquisitions):** any accrued but unrealised interest / charges on the *extinguished* loan, relating to periods *before* you took the SNFA, **shall not be recognised as income** when you book the SNFA.
- **40C (legacy stock as at 30 Sep 2026):** if that pre-takeover interest is already sitting in P&L against an SNFA still on the books at 30 Sep 2026, reverse it through P&L **by 30 Sep 2027**, to the extent it is still unrealised on the reversal date. You do **not** have to reverse it on 1 Oct. You have a year. But you **do** have to know the 30 Sep stock, because that is the population the year-long clock applies to.
- **40D:** rent / sale proceeds / any income from an SNFA = **‘non-interest / other income’** in the year it is **realised**. Upkeep costs in the year incurred. No more sneaking SNFA yield into interest income.

**Why 30 Sep is the magic date:** para 40C’s legacy clause is written against SNFA **outstanding as on 30 Sep 2026**. If finance does not extract that inventory tomorrow, the 30 Sep 2027 reversal clock has no population.

**Recent Changes**

Previously: no special IRAC rule for income on assets taken over against a claim — practice varied, and some books folded pre-takeover accrued interest into the SNFA carrying amount. Now (from **1 Oct 2026**): that interest is **not** income on acquisition (40C), and SNFA yield is **other income when cash** (40D). Calamity-restructuring IRAC amendments (Apr 29, **eff 1 Jul 2026**) are already live — 5% extra specific provision, standard-status retain/upgrade on Chapter VI-A plans. Unrelated to SNFA; don’t mix them in the Q2 note.
