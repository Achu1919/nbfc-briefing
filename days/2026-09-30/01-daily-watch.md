## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **IRDAI proposes sharp caps on NBFC insurance commissions (BIG — affects lender PBT).** IRDAI has floated draft rules capping commissions on credit-linked life insurance at **2% for single-premium** and **2.5% in the first year for multi-year** products — far below current payouts that run up to **57%** (first-year NBFC commissions average 42–67%). Insurance commissions made up **3–25% of profit-before-tax for select NBFCs in FY26**, per Kotak Institutional Equities. Most hit: lenders heavy in credit-protection products. Exposed names cited by analysts: **L&T Finance (insurance ≈26% of FY26 PBT)**, **Poonawalla Fincorp**, **Cholamandalam**, **M&M Financial**. The proposal also **bans compulsory insurance bundling with loans**, makes portfolios insurance borne by the lender, mandates all-inclusive commissions and cost audits. NBFC-channel life premium nearly tripled from ₹36 bn (FY23) → ₹103 bn (FY25); 93% was single-premium group credit life. This is the single biggest profit-lever story for NBFCs this week — watch NBFC Guru for regulatory depth. *(Sources below, BFSI section.)*
2. **RBI pressure on Tata Sons to list; Tata Trusts proposes restructuring to dodge NBFC tag.** RBI rejected Tata Sons' March-2024 application to surrender its **Upper-Layer NBFC** registration in September 2026 (post ₹21,813 cr debt repayment). Its 3-yr listing deadline lapsed in Sept 2025. Tata Trusts (66% holder) now proposes merging operating cos **Tata Electronics Systems Solutions** and **Tata Consulting Engineers** into Tata Sons to push its financial-asset ratio below RBI's 50% principal-business threshold and shed NBFC classification — an RBI official called such restructuring "an effort of avoidance" and wants a listing "at the earliest." On Sept 17 the TSPL board voted **4–1** to proceed with listing steps and extend N. Chandrasekaran's term; Noel Tata (Trusts) voted against. RBI is awaiting Tata Sons' formal response + timeline. *(SCOPE FLAG: regulatory detail → NBFC Guru's report.)*
3. **JPMorgan double top-pick: PNB Housing (HFCs) & IIFL Finance (gold).** After management meetings, JPMorgan kept **PNB Housing Finance** its top HFC pick and **IIFL Finance** its preferred gold lender. Key negative finding: the **FCNR(B) liquidity flood is NOT** raising bank mortgage-price competition NOR cutting NBFC cost of funds — the money hasn't changed the competitive landscape. PNB Housing NIM could gain ~10 bps (+3–5% EPS) per 25 bp policy-rate hike; IIFL has limited pass-through on gold-loan rates. NBFCs keep gaining share in housing and gold; asset quality "pristine" except micro-LAP. Street: 13/15 Buy on PNB Housing, 7/8 Buy on IIFL. PNB Housing traded +1.65% at ₹1,102.90 Tue (+12% YTD); IIFL −1.18% at ₹591.10 (−5% YTD).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Housing Finance (Wed rotation)

- **The ₹10–15 lakh segment is the specialist battleground.** Systematix's 42-branch channel check (Maharashtra, Karnataka, TN, Telangana, Rajasthan) found **60–65% of AHFC borrowers sit in the ₹10–15 lakh ticket band** — a segment specialist lenders defend by doing income verification on the ground rather than by documents. The affordable (<₹25 lakh) home-loan market is worth ~**₹10.9 lakh crore** but has grown <6%/yr since FY19 vs ~13% overall — because PSU banks dominate the segment and have pulled back. Specialist AHFCs grew ~23%+/yr, ~4x the market.
- **Fierce new competition:** Piramal Housing, Cholamandalam, Tata Capital, Mahindra Finance, Tyger, Repco, Star Housing and Finova are all crowding the space; Big NBFCs chase higher ticket/customers with stronger credit profiles. Mature branches (>₹50–60 cr AUM or >5 yrs) are stagnating — expansion into new geographies is the growth lever.
- **Aadhar Housing = the sector bellwether.** ₹31,364 cr book (Jun-26), tripled since FY19; **RoA ≥4% for 12 straight quarters**; ~43% of loans to self-employed/no-payslip borrowers; 628 branches, 12,600 "Aadhar Mitras"; borrowing cost ~7.3% (lowest in 2+ yrs) after the ICRA AA+ / CARE AA+ upgrades; FY26 profit ₹1,100+ cr (+21%). Management guides to **₹50,000 cr AUM in 3 years**. PMAY-U 2.0 subsidy (up to ₹1.8 lakh over 5 yrs) reduces early pay-off/churn.
- **Private-capital validation:** Carlyle/Aditya Puri's ₹2,027 cr Nido deal + JICA's $150 mn to IIFL Home Finance + Bharat Housing Network's green bond raise confirm strong investor appetite in affordable housing finance.
- **Valuation/macro context:** JPMorgan sees NBFCs gaining housing share; FCNR liquidity not cutting funding costs; NIM/EPS most geared to rate hikes for PNB Housing-type HFCs.
