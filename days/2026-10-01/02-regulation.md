### 1. SNFA / IRAC 3rd Amendment — live from today (not a new circular)

- **What:** The July amendment on Specified Non-Financial Assets starts applying to new take-overs from **today**.
- **Issuer:** RBI, Department of Regulation
- **Date:** Issued **16 July 2026**. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-10-01]**
- **Reference:** *RBI (NBFC – IRAC) Third Amendment Directions, 2026* — `RBI/2026-27/197` `DOR.STR.REC.162/21-04-048/2026-27` (inserts paras **40C–40D**). Definition / acquisition / valuation / disposal sit in the sibling *RBI (NBFC – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026* — `RBI/2026-27/189` (Chapter VII-A, para 15A).
- **Classification:** [AMENDMENT] — go-live of a rule already on the books. Not overnight news.
- **In Plain English:** An SNFA is an **immovable / non-banking asset taken over in satisfaction of a claim** (SARFAESI, bilateral settlement, court, etc.). From today, **accrued but unrealised interest and charges on the extinguished loan cannot be booked as income** on a new SNFA (para 40C). The **30 Sep 2026** book is the stock-take date. Anything still sitting as unrealised pre-SNFA interest on that stock must be reversed through P&L by **30 Sep 2027**.
- **Impact on NBFCs:** New SARFAESI / takeover accounting from 1 Oct must stop capitalising pre-takeover interest. Valuation, holding-period and disposal rules in Chapter VII-A apply to new SNFAs. This is a **books** issue, not a gold-auction issue.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** **Gold jewellery in the strong-room is collateral, not an SNFA.** Do not dump the gold book into the SNFA inventory. Do dump **MHFL and any MSME SARFAESI take-overs** as at last night’s 30 Sep stock. If MHFL has a taken-over flat, stop recognising pre-takeover interest on any **new** take-over from today; diary the 30 Sep 2027 reversal for leftover unrealised interest on the 30 Sep stock.
- **Source:** [RBI Notification Id=13575](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13575&Mode=0) (IRAC 3rd Amd, 16 Jul). Tuesday’s teaching covered the mechanics — do not re-open IRAC today.

### Recovery conduct, recovery agents, and the SC overlay

**What is it?**

“Recovery” is not a back-office afterthought. For an NBFC with a customer interface it is a **regulated activity**: who you hire, what they may say, when they may call, how you prove it, and what you must give the borrower back after you sell the security. RBI put the operational rulebook in the *Responsible Business Conduct* Directions. The Supreme Court, on 16 Sep, added a constitutional overlay that **does not wait for 1 Jan 2027**.

Two clocks, one book:

1. **RBI RBC 3rd Amendment (6 Aug 2026)** — recovery-agent engagement, hours, certification, recording. **[EFFECTIVE:2027-01-01]**
2. **Supreme Court repossession judgment (16 Sep 2026)** — no new RBI circular. **Live now.** Waiting for January is the wrong legal advice.

**The Technical Details**

**Source of law (current):**

- *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025*, as amended by the **Third Amendment Directions, 2026** dated **6 August 2026** (recovery / recovery-agent chapter; Notification [Id=13672](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx)). Applies to NBFCs **with a customer interface**. Carve-outs typically include CICs, NBFC-AAs, NOFHCs, Mortgage Guarantee Companies, Standalone Primary Dealers, and NBFCs with no customer interface.
- Parallel HFC alignment was issued the same day so MHFL sits in the same conduct world.
- **Second Amendment (15 June 2026)** — advertising, DSA/DMA, consent, mis-selling, dark patterns — **also [EFFECTIVE:2027-01-01]**. Same January stack; different chapter. Do not mix the two, but do not staff them as two unrelated projects.
- Gold-auction **surplus** and **notice** sit in the gold-loan chapter of the *Credit Facilities* Directions (yesterday’s topic). Conduct failure there is what **KLM Axiva** paid for.

**Who the recovery rules bind**

| Person | Bound? |
|--------|--------|
| MFL employees doing follow-up | Yes |
| Empanelled recovery agents / agencies | Yes — MFL remains responsible |
| LSPs / DSAs who “influence” repayment | Treat as in-scope until Legal says otherwise; RBC 2nd Amd already pulls LSPs into the DSA/DMA definition for *sale* |
| Branch gold-auction vendor | Auction process + surplus = Credit Facilities; *calls and visits* = RBC recovery |

You cannot outsource the liability. Due diligence, a Board-approved recovery policy, a published list of empanelled agencies, training, and a documented code of conduct are the lender’s job.

**The operating rules that will be inspected**

| Item | Rule | Why it bites |
|------|------|----------------|
| Contact hours | **08:00–19:00** only, unless the borrower asks otherwise | Night calls are an easy DoS finding |
| IIBF certification | Recovery agents must hold the prescribed IIBF certificate | Uncertified vendors = stop-work on 1 Jan |
| Call recording | Record recovery calls; retain **6 months** | No tape = you cannot defend a complaint |
| Board policy | Board-approved recovery / agent-engagement policy before go-live | Not a management circular |
| Fair treatment | No harassment, abusive language, public shaming, or employer/neighbour contact as a pressure tactic | SC overlay makes this a **today** issue |
| Grievance | Dedicated recovery-complaint channel | Ombudsman + DoS both read this |
| Device locking | Financed-device lock/unlock only under the specified procedure | Irrelevant to gold; relevant if any consumer-durable / digital book exists |
| Gold auction surplus | Surplus after costs + outstanding **belongs to the borrower** | **KLM Axiva ₹2.70 lakh, PR 1181, 24 Sep 2026** |

**Previously vs now**

Previously: recovery conduct was scattered — Fair Practices Code, outsourcing guidelines, a few DoS observations, and (for gold) auction-notice practice. Now (from **1 Jan 2027**): a single RBC chapter with certification, hours, recording, and Board ownership. **Separately, from 16 Sep 2026**, the Supreme Court has already treated coercive repossession / recovery as a live rights issue. RBI has **not** issued a follow-on circular. That silence is not a stay.

**The SC overlay — what “enforce now” means**

Do not wait for DoR to rewrite the RBC chapter. Until RBI speaks, treat the judgment as:

- Recovery that looks like **self-help repossession without due process** is unsafe, even if the loan agreement has a seizure clause.
- **Gold** is different from a car: the metal is already in your strong-room. The live gold issues are **auction notice, reserve, surplus remittance, and how you speak to the borrower before the auction** — not kicking down a door.
- **MHFL / MSME** SARFAESI take-overs are the book that looks most like the Chola fact-pattern. Those files should already be on Legal’s list.

**The rest of the 1 Jan 2027 stack (do not skip)**

RBC **Second** Amendment (15 Jun 2026) lands the same morning:

- Board policy on advertising / marketing / sale of own **and** third-party products.
- DSA/DMA list on the website, updated within **7 calendar days** of any change.
- Explicit consent; default choice = **No**.
- Codified **mis-selling** (unsuitable product, incomplete information, no consent, compulsory bundling) with **full refund** if established.
- No dark patterns; CCPA 2023 guidelines apply.
- Customer-feedback within 30 days of sale, by a **non-sales** team.

If MFL sells insurance / other third-party products in branches, that is a January item for Compliance + the product partner, not for FP&A provisioning. Flag it so it does not surprise the Board in December.

Under the RBC 3rd Amd, when is that call allowed? **Only if the borrower has asked for contact outside 08:00–19:00.** “They are hard to reach” is not consent.
2. The Supreme Court judgment is dated 16 Sep 2026. The recovery-agent directions are effective 1 Jan 2027. Can MHFL keep using its old SARFAESI field process until January? **No.** The judgment is live now; RBI’s silence is not a transition period.
3. A branch auctions pledged jewellery, recovers the outstanding plus costs, and parks the ₹18,000 extra in “miscellaneous income” pending the borrower collecting it. What is wrong? **The surplus is the borrower’s.** Keeping it is the KLM Axiva fact-pattern (PR 1181). It is not income. Remit / hold to the borrower’s order and document the notice.
