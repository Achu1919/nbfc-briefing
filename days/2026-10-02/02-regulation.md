### Digital Lending + Co-Lending Arrangements (current MD text)

**What is it?**

Two different machines that often get mixed up.

- **Digital lending** is *how* you originate: a **remote and automated** process (app / web DLA) for acquisition, underwriting, sanction, disbursement, recovery. It lives in **Chapter III** of the Credit Facilities MD.
- **Co-lending (CLA)** is *who* funds: two regulated entities jointly fund a loan in a pre-agreed share, with revenue and risk sharing. It lives in **Part B** of the Transfer and Distribution of Credit Risk MD.

A gold loan given across the counter, with jewellery in the strong-room, is **not** digital lending. The same gold loan originated on an app, with disbursement to the borrower’s bank account, **is**. CLA can sit on top of either.

**The Technical Details**

**1. Digital lending — Credit Facilities MD, Chapter III**
Source: *RBI (NBFC – Credit Facilities) Directions, 2025*, `RBI/DOR/2025-26/347` `DOR.CRE.REC.No.266/07-01-008/2025-26`, **updated 15 Jul 2026**. [EFFECTIVE: on issuance, Nov 28 2025; Ch. III is current.] Applies to **all layers** of ICC / MFI / Factor / IFC / IDF / HFC / NBFC-D. Type I is out. P2P only gets para 21(2) (no DLG).

- **LSP contract is mandatory.** Enhanced due diligence on tech, privacy, borrower conduct, past record. Periodic review. Outsourcing MD still applies. **The NBFC remains fully liable** for the LSP’s acts (para 6(7)). You cannot outsource the licence.
- **Multi-lender LSPs (para 7):** the DLA must show **all matching offers** plus unmatched lender names; ranking only on a **publicly pre-disclosed metric**; no dark patterns.
- **KFS** is the RBC Directions KFS, not a home-grown sheet. Digitally signed KFS / sanction / T&Cs / privacy policy must auto-flow to the **registered, verified** email/SMS on execution (para 9(3)).
- **Disbursement (para 10):** always to the **borrower’s bank account**. Never to the LSP. Exceptions: statutory mandate, **CLA fund-flow between lenders**, specific end-use paid to the end-beneficiary’s bank account. Servicing / repayment likewise **direct to the NBFC**, no pass-through / pool account. LSP fees are paid by the **NBFC**, never collected from the borrower by the LSP. Cash recovery on delinquent loans is allowed but must hit the borrower’s account **same day**.
- **Cooling-off (para 11):** Board policy, **floor of one day** (not three). Exit = principal + proportionate APR, no penalty. A reasonable one-time processing fee may be retained if disclosed in the KFS. After cooling-off, ordinary prepayment rules apply.
- **Data (paras 13–14):** need-based, explicit consent, audit trail. **No** file/media/contacts/call-log/telephony access. One-time camera / mic / location for KYC only. LSP stores only **minimal** operational data. **No biometrics** unless a statute allows it. **All data in India.** If processed abroad, delete offshore and bring back **within 24 hours**.
- **CIMS DLA reporting (para 18):** every DLA — own or LSP — on CIMS, CCO/Board-designate certified. RBI **publishes the list as filed**; it does not validate it. Listing is **not** an RBI endorsement.
- **DLG (paras 19–24):** only with an LSP (or another lender acting as LSP) that is a **Companies Act company**. Cover only as cash with the NBFC, **lien-marked SCB FD**, or **bank guarantee**. Cap: **5% of amount disbursed** out of that frozen “DLG set”. Implicit guarantees count. Recovery does **not** reinstate the cap. P2P: no DLG. Credit-guarantee-scheme loans: no DLG.

**2. Co-lending — Transfer & Distribution MD, Part B**
Source: *RBI (NBFC – Transfer and Distribution of Credit Risk) Directions, 2025*, `RBI/DOR/2025-26/352` `DOR.STR.REC.271/21.04.048/2025-26`, **updated 1 Jul 2026**. Part B **[EFFECTIVE:2026-01-01]** (or earlier if the Board opted in). Pre-6 Aug 2025 CLAs stay on old rules until they roll off.

- **Who:** CLA with SCBs (**excluding SFB / LAB / RRB**), AIFIs, and other NBFCs/HFCs. Not multiple banking, consortium, or syndication.
- **Minimum retention (para 102):** each NBFC in a CLA **keeps at least 10%** of the **individual loan** on its books. The old **80:20 (bank:NBFC)** ceiling is **repealed**. Shares are whatever the ex-ante agreement says, subject to that 10% floor.
- **Blended rate (para 108):** borrower pays the **weighted-average** of each RE’s contracted rate. Any extra fee goes into **APR / KFS**. Unrealised CLA profit is deducted from **CET1 / NOF** until maturity (para 107).
- **15-day clock (paras 112–115):** partner’s share must hit **both books within 15 calendar days** of disbursement. Miss it, and the whole loan **stays with the originator** and can move only under Part A (loan transfer), not as CLA.
- **Escrow (para 117):** all disbursements and repayments through a **bank escrow**. Each RE keeps its **own borrower account** for its share (para 116).
- **CLA-DLG (para 123):** originating NBFC may give the partner **up to 5%** DLG, same mechanics as Chapter III.
- **KYC:** partner may rely on the originator’s Customer Identification Process.
- **CIC:** each RE reports **its share**.

**Why It Matters**

Digital lending is where RBI has been writing penalties (apps, LSPs, fund-flow, dark patterns). CLA is where the 2021 80:20 muscle-memory will fail an inspection: a supervisor opening a 2026 CLA file and seeing “bank 80 / NBFC 20 because that is the rule” is looking at a **repealed** rule. The live tests are 10% retention, 15-day booking, escrow, blended APR in the KFS, and CET1 add-back of unrealised profit.

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Item | Rule | Source |
|------|------|--------|
| Digital-lending cooling-off | **≥ 1 day** (Board may set higher) | CF MD para 11 |
| DLG cap (digital or CLA) | **5%** of disbursed / outstanding (as specified) | CF MD para 24; T&D para 123 |
| Data localisation | India; offshore processing **≤ 24 hours** then delete | CF MD para 14(4) |
| CLA minimum NBFC share | **10% of each loan** | T&D para 102 |
| CLA booking clock | **15 calendar days** or the loan stays with originator | T&D paras 113–115 |
| Old 80:20 | **Repealed** for new CLAs from 1 Jan 2026 | T&D Part B |
| Unrealised CLA profit | Deduct from **CET1 / NOF** till maturity | T&D para 107 |
| CIC | Digital: **all** DLA loans, any tenor. CLA: **each RE’s share** | CF MD para 17; T&D para 122 |
| Layer | Both MDs: **all layers** of in-scope NBFCs (MFL = **ML**) | CF MD para 3; T&D para 3 |

**Recent Changes**

- **Previously (Nov 2021 co-lending circular):** bank minimum 80%, NBFC maximum 20%, “CLM-1 / CLM-2” folklore. **Now (Part B from 1 Jan 2026):** 10% floor on the NBFC, flexible split, blended rate, 15-day clock, escrow.
- **Previously (2022 digital-lending circular):** cooling-off often described as 3 days in industry notes. **Now (CF MD para 11):** floor is **one day**; three days is a **policy choice**, not the law.
- CF MD last touched **15 Jul 2026** (Second Amendment — not digital-lending). T&D MD last touched **1 Jul 2026** (Type I footnote). **Nothing overnight.**

**Muthoot Fincorp Context**

- **Branch gold loan ≠ digital lending.** Physical pledge + branch disbursement sits in **Chapter IV (gold/silver)**, not Chapter III. Do not force-fit KFS-on-app / cooling-off / CIMS DLA rules onto the counter product.
- **Gold-loan app / assisted-digital / LSP sourcing:** that *is* Chapter III. Disbursement still to the **borrower’s bank account** (para 10 — no exception for gold). Cooling-off of at least one day must be in the credit policy and the KFS. If an LSP is in the chain, the LSP **cannot** touch principal, interest, or recovery proceeds (except same-day cash collection on delinquent accounts, fully posted).
- **CIMS:** every live DLA, own or LSP, must be on the portal and on the website. RBI will publish whatever the CCO certified.
- **CLA:** useful for MSME / MFI / MHFL, less natural for jewellery (collateral custody stays with MFL). If a bank CLA is live, check: (i) MFL share ≥ 10% per loan, (ii) partner booked within 15 days, (iii) blended rate in the KFS, (iv) unrealised profit stripped from CRAR, (v) escrow, (vi) each side reporting its CIC share. Do **not** quote 80:20 in a Board note.
- **DLG:** gold is collateralised; DLG is a fintech/unsecured tool. If MFL ever gives or takes DLG, the 5% frozen-set arithmetic in para 24 is the workpaper, not a spreadsheet plug.
- **Interest Rate Directions (draft, Apr 2027):** if finalised, the ML 3-year spread lock and MFI APR cap will sit **on top of** blended CLA rates. Flag any CLA that would be re-priced under that draft.

2. MFL originates an MSME loan and a bank is meant to take 70%. On day 16 the bank’s share is still not on the bank’s books. What is the loan now, and which MD governs any subsequent transfer?
3. An LSP wants 8% first-loss on a digital personal-loan pool, funded by a corporate guarantee from the LSP’s parent. Can MFL take it? What three forms of DLG are actually allowed, and what is the cap?

*(Answers: (1) No — not remote/automated. Chapter IV gold + RBC KFS/FPC. (2) Whole loan stays with MFL; further transfer only under Part A (loan transfer), not as CLA. (3) No — cap 5%, provider must be a company LSP, and only cash / lien-marked SCB FD / bank guarantee. Parent corporate guarantee is not a permitted form.)*
