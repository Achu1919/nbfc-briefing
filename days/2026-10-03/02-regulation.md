### LODR Regulation 52 — quarterly results for listed NCDs

**What is it?**

If MFL (or any group issuer) has **listed non-convertible debentures**, SEBI — not RBI — owns the *public* numbers. RBI cares that GNPA, CRAR and provisions are right in the books. SEBI cares that those same numbers, plus a prescribed set of ratios, hit the stock exchange within a hard number of days, with a limited-review report attached, and then appear in the newspaper.

Think of two parallel clocks on the same 30 Sep freeze:

1. **RBI / IRAC clock** — already stopped. GNPA/NNPA/SMA/ECL are frozen. Do not reopen.
2. **SEBI / LODR 52 clock** — still running. Board approves, auditor limited-reviews, exchange filing, newspaper. Due **45 days** from quarter-end = **14 November 2026**.

This is **Chapter V** of the LODR Regulations (Regulations **49–62**), not Chapter IV (equity, Reg 33). Muthoot Finance (the other group) is equity-listed; **do not copy their Reg 33 pack**. MFL’s listed-debt pack is Reg 52.

**The Technical Details**

**Primary sources (current versions, not folklore):**

- SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, **Chapter V**.
- **Master Circular** `SEBI/HO/DDHS/DDHS-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/0000000103` dated **11 July 2025** — listing obligations for NCS / SDIs / CP. Formats for Reg 52 and 54 live in **Chapter I**; LRR/audit formats in **Chapter II** (Banks/NBFCs have their *own* annexes: II-B and II-D); delay-SOP fines in **Chapter IX / Annex IX-A**. - The **28 Sep 2026 Debenture Trustee Master Circular** is a restatement for **DTs**. It does not rewrite issuer Reg 52. Do not wait for the DT to “clear” your results.

**The filing machine (Reg 52)**

| Piece | What | When |
|-------|------|------|
| **52(1)** | Quarterly standalone financial results to the exchange(s) **and** on the website | **45 days** from quarter-end → **14 Nov 2026** for Q2 FY27 |
| **52(2)(a)** | Unaudited results must carry a **limited review** (SRE 2410) | Same pack |
| **52(2)(d)** | Annual audited standalone **and** consolidated | **60 days** from FY-end (not this quarter) |
| **52(2)(f)** | Statement of **assets & liabilities** and **cash flows** | **Half-yearly** — Q2 *is* H1, so this is in *this* pack |
| **52(4)** | Prescribed line items / ratios with the results | Same pack. Missing them is a separate fine. |
| **52(7) / (7A)** | Utilisation of issue proceeds / material deviation | If any listed-NCD proceeds are still being deployed |
| **52(8)** | Newspaper publication of the results extract | After the exchange filing (Annex I-A format) |
| **54(2)/(3)** | Extent and nature of **security created and maintained** on secured NCDs | In the financials |
| **50(1)** | Prior intimation of the **board meeting** that will consider the results | Before the meeting — missing it is ₹5,000 per instance |
| **57** | Interest / principal payment obligations | Running ISIN calendar, not the results pack |

**NBFCs are not “other companies”.** Chapter II of the 11 Jul 2025 Master Circular gives Banks/NBFCs **separate** limited-review and audit formats (Annex II-B, II-D, II-F). Using a manufacturing-company LRR template is a process miss. The auditor is opining that the statement discloses what **Reg 52** requires, “including the manner in which it is to be disclosed.”

**Cash flows:** 52(2)(f) requires the statement of cash flows under the **indirect method** (Ind AS 7 / AS-3). H1 pack = P&L + A&L + CFS. Q1 and Q3 are P&L-only plus 52(4) line items.

**If the pack is late:** disclose *detailed reasons* to the exchange within **one working day** of the due date — or within one working day of the *decision* to delay, if that decision is taken earlier (Master Circular Chapter I, para 6). That disclosure does **not** stop the SOP fine.

**Layer-wise applicability:** LODR Chapter V is a **listing** test, not an SBR test. BL / ML / UL does not change the 45-day clock. MFL is **ML** for RBI (CRAR 15% / Tier I 10%, gold-loan Tier I 12% if jewellery assets ≥ 50%). Those RBI numbers *feed* the 52(4) sector-specific ratios. They do not replace the SEBI filing.

**Why It Matters**

- Exchange SOP fines accrue **per day until rectified**, on top of any SEBI enforcement. They are credited to the exchange’s Investor Protection Fund.
- A late or messy 52 pack is visible to NCD investors, rating agencies and the DT. That is a funding-cost issue, not a secretarial nicety.
- Q2 is also **H1**. A&L and CFS go in. GNPA/NNPA in the notes must tie to the 30 Sep freeze (gross carrying amount, 7-step). MHFL consolidation still needs the restatement flag you already have — do not let the H1 CFS paper over the subsidiary’s different GNPA method.
- Security cover (Reg 54) on gold-backed NCDs is a **collateral** disclosure. It is not an SNFA inventory. Gold jewellery in the strong-room stays collateral.
