### MFL pocket card — the numbers that must not drift

**What is it?**

A one-page map of the *current* SBR / capital / gold-loan / IRAC rules that FP&A quotes in every board pack. The trap is quoting last year’s layer definition or last year’s uniform 75% LTV. Both have moved in 2026.

**The Technical Details**

**1. Layer — MFL is Middle Layer, not Upper Layer**

Source: *RBI (NBFC – Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2025*, as amended by the **Second Amendment Directions, 2026** — `RBI/2026-27/163`, `DOR.FIN.REC.No.137/03.10.001/2026-27` dated **24 Jun 2026** `[EFFECTIVE:2026-06-24]`.

Previously: UL was identified by a **scoring methodology**; the top-10 by asset size were always UL.

Now (from 24 Jun 2026): **scoring is repealed**. Paragraph 24 now reads: the Upper Layer consists of NBFCs with **asset size of ₹1,00,000 crore and above** as per the **latest audited balance sheet**. RBI publishes the UL list **annually**. Threshold reviewed every **three years**.

| Layer | Who is in it | MFL? |
|-------|--------------|------|
| **Base (BL)** | Assets **< ₹1,000 cr** (and specified activity types) | No |
| **Middle (ML)** | Assets **₹1,000 cr+** and **not** on the UL list; also all NBFC-D, HFC, CIC, IFC, IDF, SPD by activity | **Yes — MFL** |
| **Upper (UL)** | Audited assets **₹1,00,000 cr+**, named annually | **No.** **Muthoot Finance** is the UL gold-loan *peer*, a different company |
| **Top** | Empty unless RBI moves a UL name up | Empty |

Once named UL, enhanced rules apply for **at least five years** even if the entity later falls below the cut. That is why Muthoot Finance staying UL does not pull MFL with it.

**2. Capital — gold overlay sits on top of ML, not instead of it**

Source: *RBI (NBFC – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025* (`RBI/DOR/2025-26/345`, `DOR.CAP.REC.264/21-01-002/2025-26`), **updated 1 Jul 2026**. Paragraph 6:

| Who | Floor | Cite |
|-----|-------|------|
| NBFC **primarily engaged in lending against gold jewellery** (≥50% of financial assets) | **Tier 1 12%** and **CRAR 15%** of RWA. T2 ≤ 100% of T1 | Para 6(1) |
| NBFC-ML and above (general) | **Tier 1 10%**, **CRAR 15%**. T2 ≤ 100% of T1 | Paras 6(2)–6(3) |
| NBFC-UL **additionally** | **CET1 9%** of RWA | Para 6(4) |

For MFL the binding pair is **CRAR 15% + Tier 1 12%** (gold overlay), **not** the general ML 10% Tier 1, and **not** UL CET1 9%. Do not quote 30% CRAR — that figure is wrong for NBFCs.

Current-year profit may enter CET1 **only** if audited or limited-reviewed, reduced by average dividend of the last three years (para 7(viii)). ROU assets on tangible leases are **not** deducted from CET1.

**3. Gold LTV — tiered, continuous, bullet uses maturity amount** `[EFFECTIVE:2026-04-01]`

| Total consumption gold loan per borrower | Max LTV |
|------------------------------------------|---------|
| ≤ ₹2.5 lakh | **85%** |
| > ₹2.5 lakh and ≤ ₹5 lakh | **80%** |
| > ₹5 lakh | **75%** |

LTV is maintained **throughout tenure**, not only at disbursal. For **bullet** loans, LTV is on **principal + interest payable at maturity**. Bullet itself is still only for **≤ ₹2.5 lakh**, tenure **≤ 12 months**. Renewals / top-ups: standard accounts only; bullets only **after accrued interest is paid**; no renewal if LTV would breach on the maturity date.

Risk weights (unchanged): LTV ≤50% → **35%**; 50–75% → **50%**. Standard provision **0.40%**; gold LTV **>75%** standard provision **1%**.

**4. IRAC — 90-day, book-based, gold is not SNFA**

Source: *RBI (NBFC – IRAC) Directions, 2025* (`RBI/DOR/2025-26/356`). NPA = overdue **> 90 days**, day-end process. Upgrade only when **all** facilities of the borrower are regular.

MFL standalone GNPA/NNPA denominator = **gross carrying amount**, not AUM and not net of loss allowance. Seven-step bridge: gross AUM → −ARC sold → −DA & co-lending off-book → +accrued interest → −fee amortisation. MHFL uses on-book AUM directly — flag for consolidation restatement.

SNFA (Stressed Assets MD, live **1 Oct 2026**): **gold jewellery is not SNFA**. MHFL / MSME SARFAESI take-overs from 1 Oct cannot book pre-takeover interest. Legacy SNFA stock as of 30 Sep 2026 must be reversed by **30 Sep 2027**.

**Why It Matters**

Wrong layer → you overstate governance (UL 50% independents, CET1 9%, listing) or understate gold capital (T1 12%). Wrong LTV formula → a bullet book that looked 75% at disbursal can be over-limit the day gold dips or interest accrues. Wrong GNPA denominator → the LODR 52 pack that is already on the **14 Nov** clock will not tie to audited notes.

**Quiz yourself**

1. An NBFC has audited assets of ₹95,000 cr. Is it automatically UL? What extra capital would apply if it *were* named UL?
2. A ₹2 lakh gold bullet is disbursed at 84% LTV on principal. Interest will accrue to 8% of principal by maturity. Is the loan within LTV at disbursal? At maturity? Can you renew it if gold is unchanged?
3. Why can MFL’s GNPA% on AUM look “fine” and still fail the LODR 52 / audited-note tie-out?

*Answers (fold after you try): (1) No — UL is ₹1,00,000 cr audited, annual list. If named UL: extra **CET1 9%** on top of 15/10, and gold overlay still 12% T1 if ≥50% gold. (2) Disbursal 84% is inside the 85% slab; maturity LTV = 2L×1.08 / gold value ≈ 90.7% of original gold value — **breach**. Renewal blocked if LTV would breach on maturity date, and accrued interest must be paid first. (3) AUM skips accrual and fee amortisation and mixes off-book; the legal denominator is gross carrying amount before loss allowance.*
