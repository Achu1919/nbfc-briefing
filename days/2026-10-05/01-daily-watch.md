## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. RBI MPC decision this week (Mon 5–Wed 7 Oct) — a rate HIKE is now the base case.** Repo rate has been frozen at 5.25% for four consecutive meetings after 125 bps of cuts in 2025. But economists now lean hawkish: **8 of 10 respondents in a Business Standard poll expect a 25 bps hike to 5.50%** at the Oct meeting, and a **Union Bank of India report sees the repo rate rising toward 6% by end-FY27**, citing persistent inflation, elevated crude and high global bond yields. The decision lands **Wednesday 7 Oct**. For NBFCs this is the single biggest swing factor of the week — it directly lifts cost of borrowings and gold-loan yields, pressures NIMs, and slows loan demand (esp. housing). **Watch the MPC statement before locking any funding/pricing decisions today and tomorrow.** *Full policy analysis is in NBFC Guru's report.*

**2. Gold-loan boom is the defining sector story — and directly your business.** RBI data shows **bank loans against gold jewellery jumped 88.1% YoY and NBFC gold loans +68.5% YoY in July 2026**, versus just 21.4% for total NBFC retail loans and 16.2% for bank personal loans. The formal gold-loan book hit **₹5.6 lakh crore by Aug 2026 (+83.2% YoY)**. Domestic gold prices nearly doubled from ~₹74,152/10g (Sep 2024) to ~₹1,51,687/10g (Sep 2026). ICRA expects **NBFC gold-loan AUM growth to moderate to ~17–19% in FY27** (from an estimated 60–65% in FY26) on tighter RBI lending norms and gold-price stabilisation — a watch-out for Muthoot's near-term growth trajectory. *Deep dive on the gold/sector economics in the Sector section of last Tuesday's edition; today's rotation is microfinance.*

**3. Bajaj Finance Q2 FY27 business update (filed Sat 3 Oct) + a ₹17,500 cr capital raise.** Bajaj Finance reported **AUM of ₹584,750 cr (+26.5% YoY)** for the Sept quarter, a customer franchise of **128.85 mn** (+16.5%; +4.42 mn added in the quarter), **new loans booked of 13.45 mn (+11%)** and a **deposits book of ~₹69,750 cr**. Separately (board meeting 1 Oct), it plans a **₹17,500 cr raise — ₹11,700 cr QIP plus ₹5,800 cr of convertible warrants to promoter Bajaj Finserv**. The stock has been weak into the update (down ~9% over five sessions, ~12% over a month) on dilution concerns. This is the strongest read-through for the sector's Q2 momentum.

## 💻 Digital & Fintech

- **UPI MDR framework takes effect 15 Oct.** For lenders receiving UPI loan repayments: a **manual one-off UPI repayment above ₹2,000 in the debt-collection category attracts a flat ₹5 charge**; **EMIs via UPI Autopay/recurring mandates stay MDR-free**. Banks and NBFCs (esp. those with high manual-repayment share) are most exposed — the ~₹5 charge is an ecosystem fee (₹3.50 interchange, ₹1.50 PSP, ₹1 app, +GST) not passed to borrowers. UPI volumes/value dipped in late September ahead of the change.
- **Digital NBFC personal loans (FACE/CRIF report):** sanctions of **₹64,656 cr in Q1 FY27 (+50% YoY)**; outstanding portfolio **₹1.54 lakh cr (+28%)**; **90–180 DPD down to 1.4%** from 2.5% a year ago; avg ticket **₹18,802**; **58% of sanction value to under-35s**, ~**40% to Tier-3+ cities**. Lenders reaching deeper into thin-credit-file and younger borrowers while keeping delinquencies low.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Monday rotation: Microfinance & SHG Lending

**The sector has turned the corner — recovery is now broad-based, but fragile and lopsided.**

- **Cycle turn:** After a ~17% YoY contraction in portfolio from Mar 2025 to Mar 2026 (a post-COVID stress cycle), the industry saw its **first QoQ growth (3%) in Dec 2025–Mar 2026**, and **Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026) AUM rose ~3.3% QoQ to ~₹3.27 lakh crore**. Newer vintages (loans originated in 2025) are performing far better than legacy stress.
- **Delinquency collapsed:** 30+ DPD **6.64% → 2.35%** in a year (Mar 2025→2026); ICRA-90+ DPD **3.3% (Jun 2026)**. NBFCs show the lowest delinquency of any lender class. Credit costs seen easing to **2.4–2.8% in FY27 (ICRA)** from a 7.1% FY25 peak.
- **NBFC-MFIs are now the engine:** they account for **~44–50% of industry AUM, active loans and disbursements** (up from ~39% in Mar 2024), taking share as banks trimmed exposure. NBFC-MFI AUM expected to grow **~15% in FY27 (CareEdge)**; GNPA to ~2.7% by Mar 2027.
- **Watch-outs:** (~1) **Drought caution in Karnataka & Maharashtra** (together >16% of the ~₹3.28 lakh cr book); MFIs advised to pull back sanctioning. (~2) **Borrower base is still shrinking** — RBI's June FSR noted ~22.7 lakh fewer borrowers QoQ, and average ticket sizes are rising (loans >₹75k now ~41% of value vs 26% a year ago) — i.e. the recovery is quality-led, serving fewer, better borrowers. (~3) **Funding skew:** large MFIs grabbed ~98% of FY26 incremental borrowings; small/medium institutions struggle for debt. The **CGSMFI 2.0 credit-guarantee scheme** (₹20,000 cr, launched Mar 2026) has seen limited traction — only ~₹1,500 cr sanctioned/₹900 cr disbursed so far.
- **SHG angle:** **Andhra Pradesh leads all states in SHG bank linkage** — **₹15,229 cr disbursed and ₹82,640 cr outstanding** (as at 15 Sep 2026, DAY-NRLM), achieving 78% of its YTD target. Collateral-free lending limit for SHGs was **raised ₹10 lakh → ₹20 lakh**; interest-rate bands standardised down to **7–11.70%**; Aadhaar-biometric checks introduced to curb fake memberships. A notable NBFC-SHG employment angle: SERP/minister flagged rural women-led distribution as a growing formalisation island.
- **Bottom line for today:** microfinance is back in growth mode but on a leaner, higher-ticket, better-quality borrower base. For a gold-focused lender like Muthoot, the MFI leg of the book participates in this up-cycle with much lower exposure than peers — a defensive posture that's paying off as non-gold MFI lending normalises.
