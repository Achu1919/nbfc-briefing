### Gold-loan RWA — LTV is a capital event, not just a product limit

**What is it?**

CRAR is a fraction: **eligible capital ÷ risk-weighted assets**. The gold overlay (Tier 1 **12%**, CRAR **15%**) is the *numerator* rule. The *denominator* is RWA. For a gold book, RWA is not “loans × 100%”. It is loans × a **risk weight that jumps when LTV crosses 50%**.

That is why the **1 Apr 2026** rule — LTV must be maintained **throughout tenure**, and bullet LTV is on **principal + interest at maturity** — is a capital-planning rule, not only a branch product rule. A gold-price dip that pushes a loan from 48% LTV to 56% LTV does three things at once:

1. Product: still inside the ticket slab (85 / 80 / 75%), so the branch may think nothing happened.
2. Capital: risk weight **35% → 50%**, so RWA on that rupee rises **43%**.
3. P&L: standard provision stays **0.40%** until LTV **>75%**, when it becomes **1%**.

FP&A that only watches the 15 / 12 floors on last quarter’s RWA will miss the intra-quarter gold-price path.

**The Technical Details**

Source — capital: *RBI (NBFC – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025* (`RBI/DOR/2025-26/345`, `DOR.CAP.REC.264/21-01-002/2025-26`), updated **1 Jul 2026**. Gold overlay in paragraph 6: NBFC primarily engaged in lending against gold jewellery (≥50% of financial assets) → **Tier 1 12%** and **CRAR 15%** of RWA; T2 ≤ 100% of T1. General ML is 15 / 10. UL adds **CET1 9%** — not MFL.

Source — LTV / continuous monitoring: *RBI (NBFC – Credit Facilities) Directions, 2025* (`RBI/DOR/2025-26/347`), gold-collateral chapter, `[EFFECTIVE:2026-04-01]`.

Gold jewellery loan risk weights (on-balance-sheet, standard):

| Gold LTV (current, not disbursal) | Risk weight | What it does to RWA |
|-----------------------------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **≤ 50%** | **35%** | ₹100 of loan → ₹35 of RWA |
| **> 50% and ≤ 75%** | **50%** | ₹100 of loan → ₹50 of RWA |
| **> 75%** | Not a permitted *new* LTV. If a live loan breaches, it is a **conduct + provisioning** event (standard provision **1%**), not a third RW bucket you plan into. | Do not originate here |

Unsecured / most other retail is **not** 35–50%. Gold’s RW concession is the whole point of the 12% Tier 1 overlay: RBI gave you a lighter denominator and took a heavier numerator.

**Worked example** (illustrative ₹, not MFL’s actual pack)

Take a **₹1,000 crore** standard gold-jewellery book, **Tier 1 ₹50 crore**, **Tier 2 ₹15 crore** (T2 is inside the 100%-of-T1 cap). Eligible capital = **₹65 crore**. Gold overlay floors: CRAR **15%**, Tier 1 **12%**.

| State of the book | RWA | CRAR (65 / RWA) | Tier 1 (50 / RWA) | Pass 15 / 12? |
|-------------------|-----|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| All loans LTV **≤50%** (RW 35%) | **₹350 cr** | **18.57%** | **14.29%** | Yes — looks comfortable |
| Same book, all loans LTV **50–75%** (RW 50%) | **₹500 cr** | **13.00%** | **10.00%** | **No — both floors break** |

Nothing changed in the P&L, the borrower count, or the rupee of loans. Only LTV crossed 50%. Capital that printed 18.6 / 14.3 is suddenly 13.0 / 10.0.

How much extra capital the 50% bucket demands on this ₹1,000 cr book:

| Floor | At RW 35% (RWA 350) | At RW 50% (RWA 500) | Extra needed if the book migrates |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Tier 1 12% | ₹42.0 cr | ₹60.0 cr | **+₹18.0 cr of T1** |
| CRAR 15% | ₹52.5 cr | ₹75.0 cr | **+₹22.5 cr of T1+T2** |

A 20% gold-price fall (collateral value × 0.80) moves LTV as **old LTV ÷ 0.80**:

| LTV before the dip | LTV after −20% gold | Bucket |
|--------------------|---------------------|--------|
| 45% | **56.25%** | 35% RW → **50% RW** |
| 70% | **87.5%** | Already 50% RW, and now **over the 75% cap** (conduct + 1% standard provision on that ticket) |

Bullet loans are worse: LTV is tested on **principal + interest payable at maturity**. Accrued interest is a silent LTV creep even if gold is flat. That is yesterday’s quiz #2, now seen from the CRAR side.

**Why It Matters**

Board packs that show “CRAR 18%, well above 15%” without an LTV-bucket split are not a capital plan. They are a snapshot. After **1 Apr 2026**, LTV is continuous. After **24 Jun 2026**, UL is a hard **₹1,00,000 crore audited-asset** cut (`RBI/2026-27/163`, `DOR.FIN.REC.No.137/03.10.001/2026-27`) — scoring gone. Two different capital conversations:

1. **Stay ML** — keep audited assets below ₹1L cr. That is a *size* watch, not a CRAR watch. Muthoot Finance being UL does not pull MFL.
2. **Hold 15 / 12 inside ML** — that is an *RWA* watch. Gold price and bullet accrual move RWA without moving AUM.
