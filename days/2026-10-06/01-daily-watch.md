## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — GOLD LOAN SECTOR *(Tuesday rotation)*

**Gold lending is the hottest growth pocket in Indian credit right now — and it is getting more crowded and more competitive.**

- **Demand signals:** Gold financiers are forecast to post the **strongest credit growth in the Q2 season — improved disbursements and higher gold-loan demand, with stable yields and asset quality** (Q2 FY27 sector preview). Gold prices remain elevated, and rising gold-loan demand is being driven by both consumption credit and a shift toward the more secure, high-LTV retail segment.
- **Why it matters — the majors are piling in:** Bajaj Finance explicitly named **gold loans** a key growth engine and will deploy part of its ₹17,500 cr raise there; **L&T Finance's gold book more than tripled to ₹3,230 cr** in Q2; **Shriram Finance's gold book grew 46% YoY in Q1 and it aims to roughly double gold's share of its loan book to ~5% in three years**; **Capri Global's gold AUM is forecast to grow ~103% YoY in Q2**, driven by branch-network expansion.
- **The pressured players:** for the traditional pure-play gold NBFCs, **competition is squeezing margins, asset quality and valuations**. Analysts note one fast-growing gold lender added ~97% gold-loan growth yet still trades ~33% cheaper than Muthoot on valuation — a sign the market is pricing in competitive pressure. Banks now advertise gold loans around 9–10%, while NBFC advertising rates (9–9.9%) translate to **effective 18–24%** for most schemes — a wide gap that keeps shifting low-risk borrowers to banks and pushing NBFC portability risk.
- **Regulatory overlay:** RBI's loan-to-value (LTV) framework remains the binding constraint on gold-loan yields and book growth, and is a key reason relative valuations have compressed.
- **Muthoot angle:** With banks advertising single-digit headline rates and majors (Bajaj, L&T, Shriram, Capri) scaling aggressively, the gold-loan pie is growing but the fight for margin is intensifying — expect continued emphasis on doorstep service, speed and rural branch reach to defend share, plus FY27 growth guidance to hinge on LTV headroom and gold-price stability.
