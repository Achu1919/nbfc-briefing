### 1. [AMENDMENT] NABVENTURES FUND-II added to the exempted-AIF list

- **What:** RBI restamped *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Undertaking of Financial Services) Directions, 2025* as **“Updated as on October 05, 2026”**. Annex I (exempted AIFs under para 12) now has a third fund: **NABVENTURES FUND-II**, inserted **w.e.f. 5 Oct 2026**.
- **Issuer:** RBI, Department of Regulation
- **Date:** **[EFFECTIVE:2026-10-05]**
- **Reference:** `RBI/DOR/2025-26/343`, `DOR.AUT.REC.No.262/24-01-041/2025-26` (28 Nov 2025 MD, restamped). **No new October circular number** on the [circular index](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx) — October is still headed by `RBI/2026-27/272`–`280` (all **1 Oct**, banks / ADs / currency). Footnote 9 of the PDF: *“Inserted w.e.f October 05, 2026”*. PDF: [343MD](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/343MD54448C9FA65A429BB5AFADBB162904F1.PDF). Previous versions stopped at **1 Jul 2026**.
- **In Plain English:** NBFCs that invest in AIFs face hard limits: **10%** of a scheme’s corpus (solo), **20%** of a scheme’s corpus (all REs together), **100% provision** if you put **>5%** into a scheme that has downstream debt/investment in your own borrower, and **full capital deduction** for subordinated units (paras 8–11). Para 12 lets RBI, with GoI, **carve named funds out of those AIF rules** (you still need a Board investment policy — para 7 stays). Annex I was SWAMIH Fund-I + AgriSURE. **NABVENTURES FUND-II** (NABARD’s venture vehicle) is now the third carve-out.
- **Impact on NBFCs:** Only NBFCs that actually subscribe to **NABVENTURES FUND-II**. Everyone else: no change to gold LTV, IRAC, CRAR, recovery, or digital lending.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** **Ignore for the gold book and for Q2 GNPA.** Flag once to treasury / investments: if MFL or a group entity has (or plans) a line in NABVENTURES FUND-II, the 10/20/5% AIF overlay does **not** apply to that scheme from 5 Oct. If there is no AIF book, close the item.

### The H1 GNPA pack — frozen day-end, 7-step denominator, gold is still a loan

**What is it?**

The number the Board, NCD holders, and bank-line lenders will read in the **14 Nov LODR 52** pack is **GNPA% / NNPA%**. Those are **prudential** ratios on the **loan book**, not investor-deck AUM, and not Ind AS 109 ECL. SNFA going live on **1 Oct** changed the *income* treatment of **property taken in extinguishment**. It did **not** change when a gold loan is NPA, how you provision it, or what sits in the denominator.

**The Technical Details**

**Source:** *RBI (NBFC – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025* — `RBI/DOR/2025-26/356`, DOR.STR.REC series, 28 Nov 2025, updated **1 Jul 2026** (SNFA restamp **1 Oct 2026**). Applies to **all layers**. HFC MD prevails for MHFL where inconsistent. Restructuring is a sibling MD: `RBI/DOR/2025-26/357`.

**1. Classification is a day-end process, not a month-end journal**

NPA = interest or principal overdue **>90 days**, **account level**, **day-end** (paras 18–19; origin `DOR.STR.REC.68/21.04.048/2021-22`, 12 Nov 2021). An operational lag is itself a breach. Q2 freeze means the **30 Sep close-of-business** LMS flag is the number. Do not “true up” in October for a 28 Sep SMA-2 that the branch regularised on 2 Oct — that is Q3.

SMA ladder (same circular, now in the MD) — only as a reminder, not a re-teach:

| Bucket | Overdue |
|--------|---------|
| SMA-0 | 1–30 |
| SMA-1 | 31–60 |
| SMA-2 | 61–90 |
| **NPA** | **>90** |

**2. Upgradation is borrower-level. Gold auction does not auto-clean the customer.**

Upgrade **only** when **entire arrears across ALL facilities of that borrower** are repaid. Paying gold loan A while gold loan B (or an MSME line) of the same PAN is still overdue does **not** upgrade A.

Gold auction: you are recovering a **pledged loan**, not acquiring an SNFA. Surplus belongs to the borrower (KLM Axiva ₹2.70L, 24 Sep, PR 1181 — the live enforcement example). Shortfall remains NPA until paid or written off. **Technical write-off** takes the amount off the **audited GNPA stock** but the borrower stays NPA for tracking and for the “all facilities” test (12 Nov 2021; FS MD disclosure).

**3. Denominator = gross carrying amount. Never AUM. Never net of ECL.**

No MD writes a formula. Regulatory GNPA% is on the **loan book**.

MFL standalone 7-step (do **not** mix with MHFL):

1. Gross AUM
2. − ARC sold
3. − DA & co-lending off-book
4. + accrued interest
5. − fee amortisation (Ind AS 109 EIR)
6. = **gross carrying amount** ← **denominator**
7. GNPA% = NPA outstanding ÷ that denominator. **Do not** deduct loss allowance from the denominator.

NNPA = GNPA − **specific** provisions against NPAs. Standard-asset **0.40%** (para 55 ML) is **not** netted. Gold LTV **>75%** steps that standard line to **1%** — a P&L item, still not in NNPA.

MHFL uses on-book AUM and skips accrual + amortisation — **restate on consolidation**. Co-lending partner share never enters the denominator (*Co-Lending Arrangements Directions, 2025*, para 28); borrower-level SMA/NPA is **binding across all REs**.

NPA outstanding in the numerator = principal + overdue interest + capitalised charges. Post-NPA accrual sits in **interest suspense** (para 12.2) and stays out.

**4. SNFA is live — and gold jewellery is still not SNFA**

*IRAC Third Amendment Directions, 2026* — `RBI/2026-27/197`, `DOR.STR.REC.162/21-04-048/2026-27`, 16 Jul 2026. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-10-01]**

Paras **40C–40D**: do not recognise pre-acquisition unrealised interest on an extinguished exposure as income when you take a Specified Non-Financial Asset; reverse any such amount still unrealised on **30 Sep 2026** stock **by 30 Sep 2027**; cash from an SNFA = **other income** in the year realised.

**SNFA = typically immovable property taken onto the books in extinguishment of a loan** (SARFAESI / settlement leftover). The ornament in the vault is a **pledge**. You never “acquired” it as a company asset while the loan is live. Auction proceeds = recovery against the loan, not 40D other income.

For the H1 pack: if MFL / MHFL holds any SARFAESI property, that is the SNFA population. Gold NPAs stay on the IRAC clock.

**Why It Matters**

- A pack that prints GNPA on **AUM** will not tie to the audited notes or to RBI returns. That is how Q2 reviews get reopened.
- ECL (Ind AS 109) can move with PD/LGD without a single extra DPD. IRAC does not. Both go in the notes; **IRAC is what the Board calls “GNPA”**.
- Standard 0.40% is a **full-book** P&L charge. On a ₹X standard gold book that is 40 bps a year **before** ECL and **before** specific NPA provision.
- SNFA live ≠ gold NPA holiday. The 90-day rule is **layer-blind**. Crossing into UL later would not buy a longer clock either — and MFL is ML.

1. A borrower has two gold loans. Loan A is 95 DPD. Loan B is current. On 2 Oct the borrower pays A in full. Q2 closed 30 Sep. What is A on the **H1** pack? What is B on **30 Sep**?

3. MHFL takes a house in SARFAESI on 3 Oct. MFL auctions a gold packet on 3 Oct and there is a surplus. Which of these is SNFA? What income line does each hit?

*Answers (fold after you try): (1) A was NPA at 30 Sep day-end → it is in **Q2 GNPA**. The 2 Oct payoff is **Q3** — it does not reopen September. B was current at 30 Sep, but **upgrade/classification is borrower-level**: if A was NPA at close, B is not a clean “standard” story for that borrower until **all** arrears are cleared. Do not upgrade B in September because A was paid in October. (2) AUM-based GNPA will not tie to audited notes or RBI returns. Denominator = step 6, **gross carrying amount** after ARC / DA / co-lending off, plus accrual, minus EIR fees. Never net ECL. (3) The **house** is SNFA (40D other income when cash is realised; upkeep is expense). The **gold auction** is loan recovery; surplus is the borrower’s (KLM Axiva). Gold is not SNFA.*
