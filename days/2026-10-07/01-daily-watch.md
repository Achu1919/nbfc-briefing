## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **⏰ RBI MPC outcome due TODAY (10:00 AM IST) — first rate hike since Feb 2023 expected.** The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee ends its meeting Wednesday 7 Oct, with a near-unanimous consensus for a **25 bps repo hike to 5.50%** (Reuters poll, Sept 28; ET BFSI). Breadth: sources say a **flat 25 bps hike** is expected, though a few analysts (Nomura) initially saw 50 bps; the bond market is pricing **25 bps + a temporary 50 bps CRR hike**. This is the single biggest swing factor for NBFC margins and funding costs today. Markets have largely front-run it — Jefferies argues NBFC valuations "already reflect higher rates," while Nomura warns funding costs could shave **1%–12% off FY27–28 EPS** for covered NBFCs. *Watch for: policy stance (neutral vs hawkish), growth/inflation guidance from Governor Sanjay Malhotra.*
2. **💰 BofA to acquire 49.9% of Jio Credit for ₹18,268 crore (~$3.8 bn valuation) — Oct 6.** Bank of America will buy into Jio Financial Services' NBFC subsidiary **Jio Credit** via preferential allotment of equity (₹6,613 cr) + warrants (₹11,655 cr). Initial 26.5% stake, rising to 49.9% on warrant exercise. One of the largest foreign investments in an Indian NBFC, extending the run of FII deals (MUFG–Shriram, Emirates NBD–RBL). Jio Financial shares rose ~2.6% on the news.
3. **🏦 Bajaj Finance launches up to ₹17,500 cr capital raise (QIP ₹11,700 cr + warrants ₹5,800 cr).** Board approved Oct 2; Kotak, JP Morgan, Morgan Stanley mandated; deal targeted for **November** (2H). Funds to fuel the FY27–29 ~23% AUM growth path. Last equity raise was 2023 (₹10,000 cr).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Housing Finance (Wednesday)

- **Growth:** ICRA projects HFC **AUM growth ~14–16% in FY27** (after ~15% in FY26), driven by the affordable/Non-Housing Loan (NHL) segment; earnings healthy with **RoMA of 1.8–2.0%**, supported by stable opex and range-bound credit costs. For Q2FY27 specifically, ET BFSI sees **HFC/AHFC AUM ~13% YoY** on ~24% disbursement growth.
- **Rate-cycle advantage (the key call):** With a **floating-rate asset + fixed-rate liability** mix, HFCs are the **biggest winners from the expected rate hike** — they repass rate rises to borrowers within a quarter (Jefferies, Nomura, Nuvama all flag this). **Bajaj Housing, LIC Housing Finance** named top beneficiaries (Kotak); **Nomura has a BUY on Aadhar Housing**. Jefferies specifically: valuation multiples compress *before* a hike but recover as rates peak → HFCs set to gain.
- **But:** affordable-housing financiers face **rising credit costs / tougher screening** (Financial Express) — a watch risk for the lower-income segment.
- **ICRA separates the tiers:** mainstream HFCs ~14–16% AUM growth; **AHFCs historically faster (~22% FY25, LAP-led)** though under competitive pressure.
- **Key names to track this results season:** Bajaj Housing (+25% AUM), Aadhar Housing, Home First (both broker BUYs), LIC Housing, Aditya Birla Housing Finance (raised ₹2,750 cr from Advent in Feb-26), PNB Housing, Can Fin Homes.
