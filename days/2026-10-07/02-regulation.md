### Gold-loan LTV, bullet & renewals (the ops overlay on a 69% book)

**What is it?**
RBI lets NBFCs lend against **gold/silver jewellery, ornaments and coins** — not against **primary gold/silver (bullion)** or gold ETFs/MFs. For **consumption** gold loans, LTV is no longer a flat 75%. It is **tiered by ticket**, must be held **every day of the tenor**, and for **bullet** loans is measured on **principal + interest due at maturity**, not on the amount you disbursed.

**The Technical Details**
**Source:** *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Credit Facilities) Directions, 2025* (updated as on **15 Jul 2026**), **Chapter IV**, paras 32–50. `RBI/DOR/2025-26/347`, `DOR.CRE.REC.No.266/07-01-008/2025-26` (28 Nov 2025). [EFFECTIVE:2026-04-01] for the LTV table / bullet / renewal rewrite (footnote 3: para 31 deleted w.e.f. 1 Apr 2026). Conduct/collateral management sits in the **Responsible Business Conduct** MD (`RBI/DOR/2025-26/362`), not in Credit Facilities.

**Applies to:** All layers of NBFC-ICC / NBFC-D / NBFC-MFI / HFC etc. that actually do this product (para 3). MFL = **ML**. Muthoot Finance = **UL peer** — same Chapter IV product rules; different SBR overlay (CET1 9%, listing).

**Why it matters now:** A 69% YoY gold print with **ongoing LTV** means gold-price down-days are no longer “wait till maturity”. A breach on any day is a policy event (para 32: credit policy must say **what you do on LTV breach**). Bullet loans that look 70% LTV at disbursal can be **over the cap at maturity** once accrued interest is loaded.

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Rule | Number | Source |
|------|--------|--------|
| Consumption LTV ≤ ₹2.5 lakh | **85%** | Para 43 |
| Consumption LTV > ₹2.5 lakh & ≤ ₹5 lakh | **80%** | Para 43 |
| Consumption LTV > ₹5 lakh | **75%** | Para 43 |
| LTV held | **Ongoing, every day** | Para 44 |
| Bullet LTV base | **Total amount repayable at maturity** (P+I), not disbursed principal | Para 43 explanation |
| Detailed credit assessment | Mandatory if total gold/silver loan to a borrower **> ₹2.5 lakh** (bullet: threshold = amount payable at maturity) | Para 33 |
| Bullet tenor (consumption) | **Max 12 months** (renewable under para 34) | Para 38 |
| Renewal / top-up | Only if **standard** + **within LTV**; bullet renewal only **after accrued interest is paid** | Para 34 |
| Gold ornaments cap (all loans to a borrower) | **1 kg** | Para 39(1) |
| Gold coins cap | **50 grams** | Para 39(2) |
| Silver ornaments / coins | **10 kg / 500 g** | Para 39 |
| Valuation | **Lower of** 30-day average closing **or** previous-day closing, IBJA or SEBI commodity exchange, **that purity**; intrinsic metal only — no stones | Paras 40–42 |
| Re-pledge of customers’ gold | **Prohibited** | Para 37 |
| Loan to buy gold / gold ETFs | **Prohibited** | Para 35 |
| Disbursal | **Generally to borrower’s bank account** | Para 46 |
| BS disclosure | Gold and silver loans as **% of total assets**, separately | Para 50 |
| Capital overlay (do not re-teach) | If gold jewellery assets **≥ 50%** of assets: **CRAR 15% + Tier I 12%** | Capital Adequacy MD; Monday’s class |

**Recent Changes**
Previously (pre-1 Apr 2026): uniform **75% LTV**, typically tested at sanction. Now (from **1 Apr 2026**): **85/80/75** by ticket, **ongoing**, bullet LTV on **maturity payable**. Renewals cannot paper over an LTV breach or an NPA. That is why a 69% volume print is not free: every extra rupee of bullet interest eats LTV headroom.

**Muthoot Fincorp Context**
- Core product is **small-ticket gold**, often **bullet ≤ ₹2.5 lakh** → 85% LTV is available, but **maturity payable** is the LTV numerator. Price the interest so 85% still holds at month 12.
- **> ₹2.5 lakh** is no longer “just another gold loan”: you need **repayment-capacity assessment** (para 33) and you drop to **80% then 75%**.
- Auction surplus / notice failures are already an **enforcement live wire** (KLM Axiva ₹2.70L, 24 Sep). Growth does not relax RBC.
- SNFA (live 1 Oct) does **not** apply to pledged jewellery. Do not mix SNFA provisioning into the gold NPA pack.

### Microfinance — household income ₹3 lakh and the 50% FOIR cap

**What is it?**
A **microfinance loan** is a **collateral-free** loan to a household with annual income **≤ ₹3,00,000**. Husband + wife + unmarried children = one household. Lien on a deposit or hypothecation of any security **kills** the “microfinance” tag.

**The Technical Details**
**Source:** Same Credit Facilities MD, **Chapter V**, paras 51–62. Activity-based: applies to **any NBFC** doing this product, not only NBFC-MFIs. NBFC-MFI *registration* is a separate MD (`RBI/DOR/2025-26/371`).

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Rule | Number | Source |
|------|--------|--------|
| Household income ceiling | **₹3,00,000 p.a.** | Para 51 |
| Household | Husband, wife, unmarried children | Para 51 |
| FOIR / repayment cap | **Max 50%** of monthly household income | Para 55 |
| What sits inside the 50% | **All** household loans — microfinance **and** collateralised (so a gold loan EMI/interest counts) | Para 56 |
| Future income from the financed activity | **Cannot** be counted to squeeze under 50% | Para 55 explanation |
| If already > 50% | Existing loans **run off**; **no new loan** until back under 50% | Para 57 |
| CIC | Household income **must** be reported; explain divergences | Para 54 |
| Pricing | Board policy + **ceiling** on interest and all other charges; not usurious; RBI will look | Paras 60–61 |
| Repayment periodicity | Board policy — must allow flexibility the borrower needs | Para 59 |

**Muthoot Fincorp Context**
If MFL / a group MFI originator puts a collateral-free loan into a household that already has a gold loan, the **gold outflow is inside the 50%**. You cannot pretend the gold book and the MFI book are two different households. For FP&A: MFI is a **conduct + FOIR** constraint, not a CRAR 12% product (unless the entity is an NBFC-MFI with its own qualifying-assets test).

**Why it matters**
The Aug 2026 print does **not** break out microfinance. Retail’s 22% is gold + housing + vehicle + durables. MFI stress in 2024–25 taught RBI that **FOIR 50% + CIC household income** is the brake. Do not grow an MFI/MSME unsecured line off the same household that is already at 85% gold LTV without adding the gold servicing into the 50%.

**Quiz yourself:**
1. A ₹2.4 lakh bullet gold loan is disbursed at 82% LTV on principal. Accrued interest to maturity takes LTV to 86% of today’s gold value. Is the loan compliant on day 1? On day 180 if gold is unchanged?
2. Can you renew that bullet at month 12 if the borrower has not paid the accrued interest, but the account is still standard and gold price has risen so LTV on principal is 70%?
3. A household with ₹20,000 monthly income has a gold-loan interest outflow of ₹8,000 and wants a ₹4,000-per-month microfinance EMI. Allowed?

*(Answers: (1) Day 1 already fails para 43 — bullet LTV uses maturity payable, 86% > 85%. Day 180 still fails para 44. (2) No — para 34 requires accrued interest paid before bullet renewal. (3) No — 8+4=12k is 60% > 50% FOIR, para 55–56.)*
