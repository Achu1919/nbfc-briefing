# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Wednesday, 07 October 2026 (published 8 Oct)

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **RBI MPC raises repo rate 25 bps to 5.50% — first hike since Feb 2023, stance flips from 'neutral' to 'calibrated tightening.'** The 6-member MPC voted unanimously on 7 Oct to raise the repo to 5.50% (from 5.25%); SDF is 5.25%, MSF/Bank Rate 5.75%. Reverses four 2025 cuts totalling 125 bps and the lengthy pause. Triggered by broadening inflation (crude above $100/bbl on West Asia supply fears + El Niño/wheat impact), FY27 CPI projected 5.2%, GDP 7.1%. Near-term rate cuts are off the table; Governor Sanjay Malhotra signalled the next move can only be a hike or a pause — several economists now expect a further hike in December. **Why it matters for NBFCs:** funding costs are repricing faster than loan yields, pressuring net interest margins across the sector. *(business-standard.com, etnownews.com, thehindu.com, corplawupdates.in)*

2. **NBFC stocks slid hard through this cycle, and today (8 Oct) the market crashed.** On 8 Oct the Sensex fell ~1,172 pts (−1.61%) to ~71,466 and Nifty ~386 pts (−1.71%) to ~22,217, with ~₹10 lakh crore of investor wealth wiped, led by financials — RBI tightening compounded by persistent FII selling, Brent >$100 and Middle-East supply fears (2,645 declines vs 693 advances). Bajaj Finance traded −1.29% at ₹951.40 intraday (8 Oct). Broader risk-off, not NBFC-specific. *(business-standard.com, indiatvnews, ndtv, trendlyne)*

3. **RBI Governor: the proposed NBFC revolving-credit ban is a "clarification," not a new rule — but a final decision is still pending.** In the post-policy presser, Sanjay Malhotra said NBFCs were never regulatorily approved to offer revolving credit and have been directed against the practice in past inspections; the issue only arises because fintechs are packaging term loans as revolving facilities. RBI is studying stakeholder feedback (NBFCs are seeking exemptions for secured lending and supply-chain finance; ~₹2 lakh crore of products could be disrupted, hitting MSME/individual access) and evaluating systemic-risk implications before issuing final directions. Tata Capital CEO Rajiv Sabharwal said the product is <5% of the lender's book and it is engaging with RBI. **Watch closely — a blanket ban would reshape NBFC working-capital-style products.** *(livemint, economictimes, ETBFSI, flashfinance)*

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Thursday: MSME & Commercial Lending

- **SME Growth Fund:** Union Cabinet approved a **₹10,000 cr commitment** to an AIF providing patient equity to high-potential SMEs (manufacturing, services, tech, strategic value chains) — addresses the long-term risk-capital gap in MSME scaling. Direct beneficiary of the same credit-demand NBFC commercial lenders serve.
- **ECLGS 5.0 (working-capital guarantee):** 6,73,979 guarantees covering **₹2,50,024 cr** issued as of 20 Aug-26 (MSMEs = 97.3% by number, 80.8% by value); NBFC loans under it capped at 13% p.a. Operational to 31-Mar-27.
- **New ₹100 cr collateral-free guarantee (per FM Sitharaman, 8 Oct, Bengaluru MSME outreach):** a fresh credit-guarantee scheme set to launch via Cabinet soon; each PSU bank to build its own credit-assessment model; guarantee covers the first ₹100 cr of borrowing — no collateral, no third-party guarantor. Positive catalyst for MSME term-loan availability (a segment banks historically under-served vs working capital).
- **RBI bulletin data:** SCB credit to MSME stood at **₹36.79 lakh cr (Dec-25), ~15% CAGR** over five years; PSL recognition for NBFC on-lending + co-lending, the Account Aggregator (enabled **₹3.5 lakh cr** of lending in FY25–26), TReDS and ULI are the structural channels expanding MSME credit.
- **NBFC commercial lending on the rise (FIDC-CIBIL):** NBFC share of credit-active commercial entities grew 10%→18% between Jun-2021 and Jun-2026; NBFC-served entities grew 2.2x vs 1.2x for the overall market.
- **However — the revolving-credit clarification hangs over MSME-focused NBFCs:** MSME-body FISME warned a ban could choke MSME working-capital funding; NBFCs are lobbying for exemptions on secured lending and supply-chain finance. Chola's SME AUM +41% (FY26), SBFC's secured MSME focus (outlook upgraded), and IKF's pivot to lower-ticket MSME loans all illustrate the segment's strategic importance.
