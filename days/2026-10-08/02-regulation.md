### 1. [AMENDMENT] SEBI — merchant banker optional on ₹10,000-face private-placement debt for clean, high-rated, regulated issuers

- **What:** Listed issuers that are already regulated (RBI / SEBI / IRDAI / PFRDA) may skip appointing a merchant banker on private-placement debt / NCRPS at ₹10,000 face value, if every eligibility condition is met.
- **Issuer:** SEBI, Department of Debt and Hybrid Securities
- **Date:** Issued **7 Oct 2026**. **Immediate effect.** [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-07]
- **Reference:** Circular **HO/17/11/24(7)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23122/2026**. Amends para 1.3, Chapter V of the **NCS Master Circular dated 15 Oct 2025**. PDF: [1791374560264.pdf](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1791374560264.pdf). Page: [sebi.gov.in … _105080.html](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/exemption-from-the-requirement-of-mandatory-merchant-banker-appointment-for-debt-issued-through-private-placement-by-certain-listed-issuers_105080.html).
- **In Plain English:** Previously, any issuer wanting the ₹10,000 face-value private-placement route **had** to appoint at least one merchant banker, with public-issue-grade duties. Now there is a **fork**: keep the MB, **or** drop the MB if you tick **all five**:
  1. Registered / regulated by SEBI, RBI, IRDAI or PFRDA.
  2. Listed on any segment of a recognised stock exchange for **≥ 1 year**, with **no pending SEBI / exchange fines or penalties** under LODR. The exchange must confirm this at in-principle approval.
  3. **No default** in the last **three FYs + the current FY** on redemption / repayment / interest / dividend on debt, NCRPS, SDIs, CPs, deposits or loans. Statutory-auditor certificate to the exchange.
  4. The paper is **unsubordinated / senior** and **secured** by a first or pari-passu charge on identifiable assets (CPSEs / PSUs / statutory bodies may be unsecured).
  5. Rated **AA- or above** on the date of placement. Multiple ratings → **lowest** rating.
- **Impact on NBFCs:** This is a **cost and timeline** relief for listed NBFCs that already issue NCDs on private placement and sit at **AA- or better**. It is **not** a waiver of LODR, Debenture Trustee, security creation, or KFS/offer-document content. Fail any one of the five (pending LODR fine, a loan-book default, unsecured retail NCD, rating below AA-) and the MB is back.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** MFL is an **RBI-regulated ML NBFC with listed NCDs** (LODR Reg 52 clock is live for Q2). If the next private placement is **secured senior, AA- or better, no pending LODR fines, no default in FY24–FY27**, treasury / CS can drop the MB on the ₹10,000 face route and save issue cost. **Do not** assume the exemption if any unsecured / subordinated / structured tranche is in the same issue. Muthoot Finance (UL peer) is a separate issuer.

### 2. [NEW_REG] SEBI — colour-coded Credit Risk-o-Meter on every listed / to-be-listed debt offer

- **What:** A mandatory six-level colour meter, mapped to the existing CRA rating, must appear in offer documents, abridged prospectus, PPM, issuer / OBPP advertisements, and OBPP web & mobile screens.
- **Issuer:** SEBI, DDHS
- **Date:** Issued **7 Oct 2026**. Live **45 days** later = **21 Nov 2026**. [EFFECTIVE:2026-11-21]
- **Reference:** Circular **HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1**. Inserts **Chapter II-C** in the NCS Master Circular (15 Oct 2025) and clause 14 in Annexure-XXIB (OBPP minimum disclosures). PDF: [1791375324647.pdf](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1791375324647.pdf). Page: [sebi.gov.in … _105081.html](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/introduction-of-credit-risk-o-meter-as-an-additional-disclosure-mechanism-for-debt-securities_105081.html).
- **In Plain English:** SEBI is not inventing a new rating. It is forcing the **existing** rating onto a traffic-light so a retail buyer on a bond platform cannot miss it. Multiple CRAs → meter shows the **lowest**. Unsecured instruments must say **“unsecured” in bold red**. “Issuer not cooperating” gets its own arrow. The prescribed disclaimer says the meter is **credit risk only** — not market / liquidity risk, and not investment advice. Perpetual / AT1 paper gets a harsher disclaimer (risk of total loss).
- **Mapping (long-term / short-term):**

| Meter | Rating | Colour |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Lowest credit risk | AAA / A1+ | Irish Green `#08A04B` |
| Very low | AA+ to AA- / A1 | Chartreuse `#7FFF00` |
| Low | A+ to A- / A2 | Neon Yellow `#FFFF33` |
| Moderate | BBB+ to BBB- / A3 | Caramel `#C68E17` |
| Moderate risk of default | BB+ to BB- / A4+ | Dark Orange `#FF8C00` |
| High to very high | B and below, C, D / A4, D | Red `#F70D1A` |

- **Impact on NBFCs:** Every **listed or proposed-to-be-listed** NCS, CP, SDI, SR, structured debt / MLD — public **or** private placement — needs the meter in the pack. OBPPs must auto-update within **24 hours** of an NSDL/CDSL rating change; **no manual override**; audit trail required. A downgrade is now a **visual** event on every bond-platform listing page, not just a CRA press release.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** Q2 / H1 LODR 52 pack is **14 Nov** — **before** this meter goes live. The **next** NCD / CP issuance after **21 Nov** must carry the meter in PPM, ads and any OBPP listing. CS + merchant banker (if any) + rating agency should lock the template in the next 6 weeks. A gold-loan NBFC at AA- sits in the **“Very low” / chartreuse** bucket; a notch cut to A+ drops it to yellow. That is a **funding-cost and retail-NCD** story, not a CRAR story.

### 3. [AMENDMENT] SEBI — more ISINs allowed to mature in a financial year (private placement)

- **What:** Ceiling on ISINs maturing in any FY for private-placement debt is raised, with a volume-based extra bucket and two carve-outs.
- **Issuer:** SEBI, DDHS
- **Date:** Issued **7 Oct 2026**. **Immediate effect.** [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-07]
- **Reference:** Circular **HO/17/11/24(8)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23125/2026**. Replaces paras 1.1–1.3 of **Chapter VIII**, NCS Master Circular (15 Oct 2025). PDF: [1791374056536.pdf](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1791374056536.pdf). Page: [sebi.gov.in … _105076.html](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/review-of-provisions-related-to-international-securities-identification-number-isin-for-debt-securities-issued-on-private-placement-basis_105076.html).
- **In Plain English:** Previously: Chapter VIII capped how many private-placement ISINs could mature in one FY (the circular is an **increase**; it does not restate the old integer). Now:
  - **17 ISINs** may mature in any FY.
  - Of those, **12** for plain vanilla (secured **or** unsecured). If outstanding amount maturing in that FY crosses **₹15,000 crore**, **+1 ISIN per extra ₹3,000 crore**.
  - **5** for structured / MLD / FRB / ZCB / **Tier II**. Legacy FRB / ZCB / Tier II ISINs already outstanding are **grandfathered**; if a FY already has ≥5 such maturing, **no new ISIN** in this bucket until the stock falls below 5.
  - If the issuer issues **only** structured / MLD / FRB / ZCB / Tier II, the cap is **12**.
  - Extra **6 ISINs** for authorised **§54EC** issuers.
  - **Excluded from the count:** GoI-serviced / EBR bonds, and **ESG debt** issued under ILNCS Regulations 2021.
- **Impact on NBFCs:** Direct **ALM / liability-programme** relief. Frequent NCD issuers who were clustering tap issues onto old ISINs to stay inside the cap can now cut more distinct tenors. ESG labels become even more useful because they sit **outside** the count.
- **Muthoot Fincorp Note:** Treasury should re-run the FY27 maturity-ISIN map against the new 12 + 5 split **this week**. If MFL was compressing taps onto existing ISINs, this is the window to open clean tenors for H2. Tier II, if any, sits in the **5** bucket — do not burn it on a ZCB / FRB curiosity.

### Responsible Business Conduct — Board policies, FPC, KFS (the conduct MD)

**What is it?**
RBI’s “how you treat the customer” rulebook for NBFCs, consolidated in November 2025. It is **not** CRAR, not IRAC, not LTV. It is the Fair Practices Code, the Key Facts Statement, penal charges, gold-loan *conduct*, MFI conduct, DSAs and recovery agents — all sitting under an explicit **Board** duty.

**The Technical Details**
**Source:** *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025* (updated as on **1 Jul 2026**). `RBI/DOR/2025-26/362`, `DOR.MCS.REC.No.281/01-01-039/2025-26` (28 Nov 2025). [EFFECTIVE:2025-11-28] unless a paragraph says otherwise. KFS for new retail / MSME term loans: [EFFECTIVE:2024-10-01]. Gold-conduct Annex II deleted w.e.f. **1 Apr 2026** (product LTV now lives in **Credit Facilities** MD — taught yesterday; do not mix the two files). Recovery-agent rewrite is the **Third Amendment Directions, 2026** (6 Aug 2026), live **1 Jan 2027**.

**Applies to:** All layers of NBFC-D / ICC / Factor / MFI / IFC / IDF / HFC **with customer interface**. **Not** CICs, AAs, SPDs, NOFHCs, MGCs, or NBFCs with no customer interface. MFL = **ML, customer-facing ICC** → **full MD**. Muthoot Finance = UL peer, same conduct MD, heavier SBR governance overlay.

**Chapter II — Role of Board (paras 7–8).** The Board must have, and periodically review, written policies covering at least:

| # | Board-approved policy | Why it exists |
|---|----------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Fair Practices Code (preferably vernacular) | The customer-facing contract of fairness |
| 2 | Grievance redressal (appeal at next higher level) | Ombudsman / RBI complaints start here |
| 3 | Interest / processing / penal / other charges — internal principles | Stops “we just charged what the system spat” |
| 4 | Lending against gold and silver collateral | Conduct + auction surplus, **not** the LTV table |
| 5 | FPC for microfinance borrowers | FOIR / household-income overlay |
| 6 | Floating-rate personal-loan reset, including switch to fixed | EMI shock |
| 7 | Engagement of recovery agents | 6 Aug Amd → 1 Jan 2027 |
| 8 | Code of Conduct for DSA / DMA / recovery agents | Staff + outsourced |

The Board must **periodically review** FPC compliance and grievance-redress functioning; a **consolidated report** goes to the Board at regular intervals. This is the paper RBI’s DoS asks for in an inspection.

**Chapter III.A — Fair Practices Code (paras 9–28), in plain English:**

- All borrower communication in **vernacular / language understood**.
- Application form must let the borrower **compare** with another lender; acknowledge every application; preferably quote a disposal timeline.
- Sanction in writing: amount, **annualised** rate, method of application, **penalties in bold**. Hand over the **loan agreement + every enclosure** at sanction / disbursement. Not doing so is called out as an unfair practice.
- Changes in rate / charges **prospective only**; notice in vernacular. Recall / accelerate only as the agreement allows.
- On full repayment, **release all securities**, subject to a disclosed lien; if you set off, give notice with particulars.
- No interference in the borrower’s affairs except as contracted.
- Transfer-of-account request: yes/no within **21 days**.
- Recovery: **no undue harassment, no odd hours, no muscle**. Staff must be trained. (The 6 Aug Amd + SC 16 Sep overlay make this justiciable, not hortatory.)
- Interest-rate **model** (cost of funds + margin + risk premium) adopted by the Board; published on the website / newspaper; **annualised**.
- Vehicle repossession: built-in clause covering notice period, when notice can be waived, possession procedure, **final chance before sale**, repossession-back, and auction procedure.
- No discrimination against physically / visually challenged applicants; disability-rights module in staff training.

**Chapter III.B — Key Facts Statement (para 29).** For **all new retail and MSME term loans** sanctioned on or after **1 Oct 2024** (fresh loans to existing customers included; credit-card receivables exempt):

- Standardised **Annex I** KFS, language the borrower understands, explained, **acknowledgement** that they understood.
- Unique proposal number. Validity: **≥ 3 working days** if tenor ≥ 7 days; **1 working day** if tenor < 7 days. If the borrower accepts inside that window, **the NBFC is bound**.
- APR computation sheet + amortisation schedule. APR = interest **plus all charges levied by the NBFC**.
- Part 2 of the KFS must flag: recovery-agent clause, grievance officer (response within **1 working day** if a generic email is used), whether the loan may be transferred / securitised, co-lending blend, and for digital loans the **cooling-off** period and the LSP authorised to recover.

**Why It Matters**
Conduct is where RBI has been **fining**, not theorising. **KLM Axiva ₹2.70 lakh (24 Sep, PR 1181)** was gold-auction **surplus** — a Chapter III.G / gold-conduct failure, not an LTV failure. **Hero Fincorp ₹10 lakh (24 Sep, PR 1180)** was excess interest — an FPC / charges-model failure. The 16 Sep SC repossession judgment is already live, months before the 1 Jan 2027 recovery-agent Amd. A Board that cannot produce the eight policies, the last FPC-compliance review, and a KFS archive will not survive a DoS inspection on a 69% gold book.

The SEBI pack from this morning is the **liability-side twin**: you owe borrowers a KFS; you now also owe **debt investors** a colour meter and a clean ISIN / MB process. Same idea — do not hide the economics.
