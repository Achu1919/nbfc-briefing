### SEBI LODR for listed-debt NBFCs — Reg 52, NCS pack, and why IBC sits next to it

**What is it?**
If an NBFC has **listed non-convertible securities** (NCDs, NCRPS), it is a “listed entity” for those securities under the **SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015**. Equity listing is **not** required. MFL’s LODR duty is the **debt** duty: quarterly results, security-cover, rating changes, default disclosure — plus the **NCS Master Circular (15 Oct 2025)** that governs how you **issue** the next NCD.

IBC sits next to it because the same listed NCD holders are **financial creditors** in a CIRP, and RBI has a special on-switch for large NBFCs.

**The Technical Details**

**A. LODR — the clock FP&A actually owns**

**Source:** SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, as amended. For **high-value debt listed entities** the working chapter is **Reg 52** (financial results) + **Reg 51 / 51A** (material events / default) + **Reg 54** (security cover) + **Reg 50** (intimation of board meeting). [EFFECTIVE: ongoing]

| Filing | Deadline | What goes in |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Q1 / Q2 / Q3 results** | **45 days** from quarter-end | Unaudited, with limited-review. Standalone **and** consolidated if you have subsidiaries (MHFL). |
| **Annual results** | **60 days** from FY-end | Audited. |
| **Security cover** | With the results (Reg 54) | Asset cover vs outstanding listed NCDs, typically a practising CA / CS certificate. |
| **Board-meeting intimation** | At least **2 working days** before (Reg 50), subject to the current LODR text | “We will consider results / dividend / issue.” |
| **Default / rating change** | **Prompt** (Reg 51 schedule) | Missed coupon, downgrade, invocation of guarantee. |

**Indian FY:** 1 Apr – 31 Mar. Q2 FY27 ended **30 Sep 2026**. 45 days → **14 Nov 2026**. That is **35 days** from this morning.

**What “results” means for an NBFC:** not just P&L. The exchange pack typically carries GNPA / NNPA, CRAR, liquidity, and the **Reg 52(4)** line items (debt-equity, DSCR, ISCR, outstanding redeemable preference shares, capital redemption reserve, net worth, NPA ratios). The **denominator** for MFL GNPA/NNPA is **gross carrying amount** (the 7-step standalone walk: gross AUM − ARC sold − DA & co-lending off-book + accrued interest − fee amortisation). MHFL uses on-book AUM and skips accrual + amortisation — flag that **before** the consolidated tie-out, not on 14 Nov afternoon.

**Previously:** listed-debt NBFCs already filed Reg 52. **Now (from 7 Oct 2026):** the **issuance** side of the same debt programme changed (see B). The **results** clock did not.

**B. NCS Master Circular — this week’s three bolts**

**Source:** SEBI Master Circular for NCS / SDI / SR / municipal debt / CP, **15 Oct 2025**, as amended by three circulars dated **7 Oct 2026**. Applies to **issuers of listed / to-be-listed debt**, stock exchanges, depositories, merchant bankers, OBPPs.

1. **Merchant banker optional on ₹10,000-face private placement** — Circular **HO/17/11/24(7)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23122/2026**. [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-07] **Immediate.** Replaces para 1.3, Chapter V. You may skip the MB **only if all five** hold: (i) regulated by SEBI / RBI / IRDAI / PFRDA; (ii) listed on any segment **≥ 1 year**, **no pending** SEBI / exchange LODR fines (exchange confirms at in-principle); (iii) **no default** in last **3 FYs + current FY** on debt / NCRPS / SDI / CP / deposits / **loans** (statutory-auditor certificate); (iv) paper is **unsubordinated / senior and secured** by first or pari-passu charge on identifiable assets (CPSE / PSU / statutory-body carve-out for unsecured); (v) rated **AA- or above** on the placement date (multiple ratings → **lowest**). Fail any one → MB is back, with **public-issue-grade** duties.

2. **ISIN cap raised** — Circular **HO/17/11/24(8)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23125/2026**. [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-07] **Immediate.** Replaces paras 1.1–1.3, Chapter VIII. **17 ISINs** may mature in any FY: **12** plain vanilla (secured or unsecured) + **5** structured / MLD / FRB / ZCB / **Tier II**. Volume kicker on the 12: once outstanding maturing in that FY crosses **₹15,000 cr**, **+1 ISIN per extra ₹3,000 cr**. If you issue **only** structured / MLD / FRB / ZCB / Tier II, the cap is **12**. Extra **6** for authorised **§54EC** issuers. **Out of the count:** GoI-serviced / EBR, and **ESG debt** under ILNCS 2021. Legacy FRB / ZCB / Tier II ISINs are **grandfathered**; if a FY already has ≥5 such maturing, **no new ISIN** in that bucket until the stock falls below 5.

3. **Credit Risk-o-Meter** — Circular **HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1**. [EFFECTIVE:2026-11-21] (**45 days** from 7 Oct). Inserts **Chapter II-C** and clause 14 in Annexure-XXIB. Mandatory on offer document, abridged prospectus, **PPM**, issuer / OBPP ads, OBPP web & mobile. Six colour levels mapped to the **existing** CRA rating (not a new rating). Multiple CRAs → meter shows the **lowest**. Unsecured = the word **“unsecured” in bold red**. INC (“issuer not cooperating”) gets its own arrow. Disclaimer: credit risk only — not market / liquidity risk, not advice. Perpetual / AT1 gets a harsher “total loss” disclaimer. OBPPs must auto-update within **24 hours** of NSDL/CDSL rating change; **no manual override**; audit trail.

**C. IBC — the Saturday twin, in one page**

**Source:** Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016; IBBI regulations. Last IBBI legal instrument on the site: **24 Sep 2026** (Liquidation Process 5th Amendment + delayed-form fee). No new IBC circular this morning.

Two different hats:

| Hat | What MFL is | Rule |
|-----|-------------|------|
| **Financial creditor** of a defaulting borrower | Everyday gold / MSME / housing book | File **s7** at NCLT if default ≥ the IBC threshold (currently **₹1 crore** for corporate debtors, unless a later notification says otherwise). Personal guarantors: **II/106 (21 Sep 2026)** is the current IBBI line on **cessation of interim moratorium** — legal should not treat PG recovery as stayed. |
| **Corporate debtor** (if MFL itself were in stress) | Theoretical for a going-concern ML | RBI can cause CIRP of a **systemically important** NBFC under the RBI Act / IBC overlay used for large NBFCs (the “Section 227 / FSP” track). This is **not** a DIY filing. Thresholds and the process are **RBI-initiated**. Do not analogise to a ₹1 crore s7 against a trading company. |

CIRP timeline (ordinary corporate debtor): moratorium Day 1 → IRP in 14 days → CoC → resolution plan in **180 days** (extendable to **270**). Waterfall: IRP costs → workmen (24 months) → employees (12 months) → **secured financial creditors** → government → unsecured financial → trade → equity. Listed NCD holders vote in the CoC **as financial creditors**; LODR disclosure continues during CIRP.

**Why It Matters**
- **14 Nov** is a **finance** deadline, not a CS-only deadline. A late / restated GNPA number is an LODR miss **and** a DoS conversation.
- The 7 Oct NCS pack is **liability-side conduct**. You already owe borrowers a KFS (Thursday’s teaching). You now owe **NCD buyers** a colour meter and a clean ISIN / MB process. Same idea: do not hide the economics.
- A **downgrade** after 21 Nov is a **visual** event on every bond-platform page within 24 hours. That is a funding-cost story, not a CRAR story.
- IBC is how large **borrower** defaults get recovered. Gold loans rarely go to NCLT (the jewellery is in the branch). MSME / housing / personal-guarantee files do.
