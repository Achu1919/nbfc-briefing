- **Governor's read (7 Oct):** Sanjay Malhotra said high liquidity is **no concern for NBFC asset quality**, and bank credit growth should stay strong.

## 💻 Digital & Fintech

- **The headline data point of the week — FIDC–TransUnion CIBIL "Bharat Nirman: NBFC Forming the Foundation of Credit Dispersion":** NBFCs now originate **47% of all new-to-credit (NTC) consumer loans**; of live NTC consumers as of Jun-2026, **74% have used ONLY NBFC credit** (10% non-NBFC only, 16% both). Loans up to **₹2 lakh are 82% of consumer-credit origination volumes**, where NBFCs do **47% vs banks' 17%**. Semi-urban and rural markets are **58% of NBFC loan volumes by count** (banks 60%). Below-prime borrowers are **35% of NBFCs' credit-active base**. NBFCs' credit-active consumer base has grown **~7x in a decade, ~2.5x the industry pace**. *(Reported 5 Oct; Mint analysis 11 Oct.)*
- **AA rails:** RBI's NBFC-Account Aggregator interoperability + deposit accounts in CAS (implement by 31 Dec 2026) is the biggest structural plumbing change since the digital-lending guidelines — it directly benefits the branch-heavy lenders who want a single financial view of a customer.
- **UPI economics:** NPCI has classified **NBFC loan repayments under the flat-fee category — a flat ₹5 per repayment** (vs ad-valorem MDR), a relief retained from earlier in this cycle; RBI Governor Malhotra said on 7 Oct that UPI MDR is "a small fee" that won't materially affect volumes.
- **AI in lending — the sector's own report card (ETBFSI NBFC Leaders' Retreat 2026, 8–10 Oct):**
  - **15–30 minute initial credit decisions are live** at Namdev Finvest for EV and solar finance; secured MSME still takes 4–5 days because of legal/technical/collateral checks.
  - **But full autonomy is nowhere near:** "Whether we are able to do complete decisioning… it is still a no. It is a co-pilot kind of thing" — Jyothirlatha B, CTO, Godrej Capital. Clix/InCred/Lendingkart/Oxyzo named **model drift, organised fraud, mule accounts, multiple borrowing and DPDP data governance** as the real risk frontier; "Compliance at the end is a brake; compliance at the beginning is a steering wheel."
  - **Economics:** smaller NBFCs need **~3–3.5% RoA** to attract capital vs **~2%** for large parent-backed lenders (UGRO Capital's Anuj Pandey) — which is exactly why AI-driven cost-to-serve matters more at the smaller end. Consolidation is expected for those who can't manage credit costs.
  - **Distribution:** FlexiLoans ~**60% of loans in Tier-II to Tier-IV towns**; Belstar does **~60% of collections digitally**; SEBI's G Ram Mohan Rao: NBFCs are ~**22% of credit** vs banks 78%, credit/GDP ~**68%** vs 150–200% in developed markets — headroom, but "every paisa lent must go into economic activity, not to repay debt", and tokenised loan assets are only as good as the underlying credit.
- **Sector credit data (HDFC Securities, 09 Oct):** NBFC credit outstanding **₹61 trn, +16% YoY in Aug-26**; retail is 44% of NBFC credit at **+22% YoY** — housing +12%, vehicle +15%, and **gold loans +69% YoY** (industry-wide gold loan growth was +62% YoY in Jun-26).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Microfinance & SHG lending

**Bottom line: the sector's outlook has been formally upgraded for the first time in this cycle.**

1. **ICRA has revised the NBFC-MFI sector outlook to STABLE from Negative** (half-yearly update dated 30 Sep, public 5 Oct), citing improving asset quality and an expected recovery in growth and profitability. Its numbers:
   - **NBFC-MFI AUM ₹1.44 lakh cr in June 2026, up ~5% YoY**
   - **FY27 AUM growth forecast: 20–25%**, driven by improving collections, disbursements and portfolio diversification
   - **Q1FY27 disbursements: +22% YoY for NBFC-MFIs vs +9% for the overall industry** — the specialist lenders are growing more than twice as fast as the blended market, helped by higher ticket sizes
   - **Key monitorables: suboptimal rainfall, moderating rural wage growth, and uneven funding access across entities** (the last point matters most now that AAA NBFC bond yields are up 50–60 bps since June — small MFIs fund at a much steeper spread, and IBL Finance's 11.05% NCD coupon is the proof)
2. **Credit costs are normalising hard:** from a **7.1% peak in FY25 to a projected 2.4–2.8% in FY27**. That single line explains most of the profit recovery across the space.
3. **Market structure:** NBFC-MFIs now command **nearly half** of the industry's active loans, portfolio outstanding and disbursement value (SIDBI/Equifax Microfinance Pulse, Sep-2026). MFIN's Micrometer showed **banks' microfinance portfolio falling 28.5% YoY** — largely a **reclassification** of loans into retail portfolios — while NBFC-MFIs expanded. MFIs and SFBs stepped up Q2 lending on improved asset quality (FE, 5 Oct).
4. **The weather caveat is real and it is the whole story for the next two quarters.** Lenders are deliberately going slow in **drought-affected Karnataka and Maharashtra** (and watching Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Gujarat). Portfolio at risk for borrowers with multiple loans remains above the portfolio average, even as the count of multi-loan borrowers falls. Weak Rabi prospects (low reservoir levels, El Niño risk) could hit rural cash flows into H1FY28 — which is why Shriram Finance is tracking Karnataka/MP/Chhattisgarh rainfall before committing to its H2 growth path.
5. **Why this matters at Muthoot Fincorp:** CARE's AA assignment explicitly attributes the **improvement in Muthoot Fincorp's consolidated profitability to the recovery in its microfinance business** (Muthoot Microfin). So the MFI outlook upgrade is a direct, positive input into the group's consolidated earnings trajectory — and simultaneously the reason CARE says "improving, but still moderate" asset quality. The geography overlap (south India concentration plus MFI exposure) means a third consecutive weak monsoon would hit both the gold book's rural borrowers and the MFI book at the same time. That is the correlated-risk scenario worth stress-testing internally.
6. **Conduct/compliance load on small-ticket lending:** Belstar Microfinance's CEO flagged at the ETBFSI retreat that rising compliance requirements create disproportionate operational strain where rules don't fully reflect small-ticket lending characteristics — a live theme given the new recovery-agent conduct framework from 1 Jan 2027.
