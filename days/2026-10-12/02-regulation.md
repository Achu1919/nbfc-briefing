### CRAR for an ML gold-loan ICC — the number in the 14 Nov LODR 52 pack

**What is it?**
CRAR is not “how much equity we have.” It is **eligible capital ÷ risk-weighted assets**, tested **on an ongoing basis**, not only at year-end. For MFL it is the number rating agencies, NCD investors and DoS all read off the same page. NOF is a different test (registration floor). CET1 9% is a **UL** test. Muthoot Finance lives there. You do not.

**The Technical Details**

**B. What counts as capital — do not mix the buckets**

**CET1 (para 7)** — paid-up equity, share premium, capital reserves, statutory reserves (s45-IC), disclosed free reserves, retained earnings. Revaluation reserves **may** sit in CET1 at a **55% discount** if the property is own-use, freely saleable, revalued by two independent valuers at least every three years, and the auditor has not qualified it; otherwise they stay T2. Current-year profit may be included **quarterly only if audited or limited-reviewed**, then haircut by **0.25 × average dividend of last three years × quarter number** (`EPt = NPt − 0.25 × D × t`). Current-year **losses** are deducted in full.

**Deductions from CET1 (para 8), the ones FP&A actually hits:**
- Goodwill and intangibles (net of associated DTL).
- **ROU asset is not deducted** if the underlying lease is a **tangible** asset (Ind AS 116). Do not haircut branch leases out of CET1.
- DTAs on accumulated losses, in full; other DTAs net of DTL.

**Tier 1 for ML / gold-BL (para 10)** = CET1-style owned-fund items plus perpetual / IPDI within the cap. **Tier 2** = subordinated debt (original maturity ≥ 5 years, amortised in the last five), general provisions on standard assets (capped), hybrid debt, revaluation (if not already in CET1). **T2 cannot exceed T1.** That is why a large sub-debt issuance does not “solve” a T1 12% miss.

**NOF is not CRAR.** NOF is the registration / going-concern floor (ML **₹10 cr**; UL **₹30 cr**; BL **₹2 cr**). It subtracts investments in group NBFCs and certain related-party advances. You can be comfortably above ₹10 cr NOF and still fail CRAR if RWA grew faster than eligible capital. Report both. Do not substitute one for the other in the 14 Nov pack.

**C. The denominator — RWA, and why gold is the cheap book**

On-balance-sheet RWA = carrying amount × risk weight (para 18). Netting only where a provision for depreciation / bad debts has already been made. Cash-margin with set-off may be netted.

| Exposure | Risk weight | Why it sits in a Monday note |
|----------|-------------|------------------------------|
| Cash, bank balances, approved G-Secs | **0%** | |
| Other secured loans “considered good” | **100%** | Default bucket |
| Consumer credit (retail), **excluding** housing, education, vehicle, **loans against gold jewellery**, MFI / SHG | **125%** | Para 18 table sr. 3(e)(i). Gold is **carved out** of the 125% consumer bucket. |
| Credit cards | **125%** | |
| Loans against gold jewellery | **35%** if LTV ≤ 50%; **50%** if LTV 50–75% (established gold overlay) | This is the CRAR lever. A rupee of 50% LTV gold consumes **one-third** the capital of a rupee of unsecured retail. |
| Gold LTV 75–85% (permitted only on tickets ≤ ₹2.5 lakh from 1 Apr 2026) | **Higher than 50%** — do not assume 50% | New band. Do not under-weight it in ICAAP. |
| Inter-corporate loans / deposits | **100%** | |
| Shares / CPs / MFs | **100%** | |

Off-balance-sheet items get a credit-conversion factor first, then a RW. Co-lending **off-book** is not in your RWA; **on-book** share is. That is why the 7-step GNPA walk and the CRAR walk must use the same on-book perimeter.

**D. ICAAP — ML and above (Chapter V)**

ML is not “15% and we are done.” Chapter V requires a Board-owned **Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process**: credit, market, operational, concentration, liquidity, interest-rate in the banking book, residual gold-price / LTV-breach risk. DoS will ask for the ICAAP before they ask for the press release. Q2 CRAR without an ICAAP sentence is a number, not a process.

**Why It Matters**
- The 14 Nov LODR 52 pack will print CRAR. If gold LTV drifted up in Q2 as prices moved, RWA density rose even if AUM was flat. That is a **denominator** problem, not a profit problem.
- T1 12% is the gold overlay. Mixing it with UL CET1 9% in a peer table is how a Board note gets sent back.
- Sub-debt fills T2, not T1. If T1 is the tight constraint, the answer is retained earnings / equity / lower RWA (more ≤50% LTV gold, less unsecured), not another T2 issuance.
- CPI at 5.54% and bank CRR at 99% from 16 Oct can **reprice liabilities**. They do not reprice the CRAR formula.
