## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

1. **18% GST proposed on NBFC co-lending services.** The GST Council's Fitment Committee has recommended levying 18% GST on services an NBFC supplies to a partner bank in co-lending arrangements (interest on the underlying loans stays exempt). Valuation of the service would be aligned to the RBI-prescribed methodology. Final decision awaited from the GST Council. *Impact: directly affects the economics of every co-lending structure (incl. Muthoot Fincorp–Piramal, Orange Retail–Shriram); higher cost will likely be passed on or renegotiated.*
2. **PNB Housing Finance now AAA across all four rating agencies.** CRISIL upgraded PNB Housing to `CRISIL AAA/Stable` from `AA+/Stable` (announced 7 Oct). This follows upgrades from India Ratings (Nov 2025), CARE (May 2026) and ICRA (Aug 2026) inside 12 months — validation of improving asset quality, strong capitalisation, granular retail mortgage book and PNB parentage. MD & CEO **Ajai Shukla** flagged cheaper funding access as a key benefit.
3. **Shriram Finance upgraded to IND AAA/Stable by India Ratings** — from AA+ — following MUFG's ~₹39,618 crore (20% stake) investment completed 8 Apr 2026. Ind-Ra cites strengthened capitalisation, improved funding outlook and potential overseas funding. Top-of-India AAA rating on India's largest retail vehicle financier.
4. **Gold loans are the standout engine of NBFC credit.** RBI sectoral data (Aug 2026) shows NBFC gold loans surged **69.1% YoY to ₹3.65 lakh crore** (vs 46.8% a year ago), and ICRA reports gold loans led H1 FY27 securitisation, up ~1.5× YoY with overall volumes at an all-time half-yearly high of ₹1.47 lakh crore. Read more in the Gold Loan deep-dive below.
5. **FII selling pressure persists.** FIIs net sold ₹1,708 crore on Mon 12 Oct (net sellers 12 straight sessions; Oct cumulative outflow ₹41,487 crore) while DIIs absorbed with ₹3,045 crore net buying (net buyers 16+ sessions). Nifty closed -0.70% as US yields crossed 5% (DSP flags ~₹75,000 crore FPI outflow on rate concerns).

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive (Tuesday) — Gold Loan Lending: The Year of the Yellow Metal

Gold lending is having its strongest run in years, and the data this week confirms it is **structural, not seasonal**:

- **Credit:** RBI sectoral-deployment data (Aug 2026) shows **NBFC gold loans grew 69.1% YoY to ₹3.65 lakh crore**, accelerating from 46.8% a year earlier — the fastest-growing NBFC retail segment, ahead of consumer durables (+56.4%) and housing (+12.1%). Driven by rising gold collateral values and festival-season household cash needs.
- **Funding / liquidity:** ICRA (12 Oct) — **gold loans led H1 FY27 securitisation, volumes ~1.5× YoY**, helping push total securitisation to an **all-time half-yearly ₹1.47 lakh crore** (+42% YoY; Q2 alone ~₹86,000 crore). Gold loans are effectively the sector's fresh-liquidity workhorse in a market where NCD/bank funding has gotten costlier.
- **Regulatory framework (set, not new):** RBI's **Lending Against Gold & Silver Collateral Directions 2025**, in force since **1 Apr 2026**, unify rules across banks/NBFCs/HFCs and co-ops: **LTV caps of 85% (≤₹2.5 lakh), 80% (₹2.5–5 lakh), 75% (>₹5 lakh)**; mandatory borrower credit assessment above ₹2.5 lakh; **bullet-loan tenors capped at 12 months**; valuation at actual purity (IBJA/SEBI-exchange reference for the preceding-30-day average); standardised assaying, auction transparency, ₹5,000/day compensation for delayed collateral release, and preferential treatment for hallmarked gold. Compliance burden is up, but the harmonised framework also legitimises scale.
- **Competition dynamics:** Jefferies (retains **Buy on Manappuram**, **Hold/caution on Muthoot**) argues the gold battle is "moving beyond the South"; **Antique** initiated coverage with **up to 21% upside on both**; **JM Financial** flags **intensifying rate competition** on gold as a margin risk despite robust volume growth. Co-lending (Orange Retail–Shriram) and bank entry into Tier-2/3 gold are widening the fight.
- **Stock angle:** Muthoot Finance hit a **52-week low (8 Oct)** and is in a downtrend/relief-rally; gold price ($/₹24k) remains the single biggest swing factor for collateral adequacy, yields and NPA comfort across the chain.
- **Read on for Wednesday:** with housing credit +12.1% and NHB doubling refinance, **Wednesday's Housing Finance deep-dive** will look timely.
