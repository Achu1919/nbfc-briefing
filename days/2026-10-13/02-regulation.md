### The prudential floor — IRAC provisions sitting under Ind AS 109

**What is it?**

MFL reports under **Ind AS 109 ECL**. That is the P&L impairment you already book. RBI still requires a **parallel IRAC computation** — classification, ageing, standard-asset provision, borrower-wise NPA — and that IRAC number is the **prudential floor**. If ECL is *lower* than the floor, the gap does **not** go through P&L again. It is appropriated from PAT into **Impairment Reserve**, which is **not CET1** and cannot be withdrawn without DoS permission.

Two numbers, one pack: ECL in the accounts; IRAC as the floor and as the GNPA/NNPA the Board still calls “asset quality”.

**The Technical Details**

**Source:** *RBI (NBFC – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025* — `RBI/DOR/2025-26/356`, `DOR.STR.REC.No.275/21.04.048/2025-26`, 28 Nov 2025, **updated as on 1 Oct 2026**. Live PDF: [rbidocs … 356MD…](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/356MD4F8109CA54BE44A9805C5300601F8A11.PDF). Applies to **all layers** (Chapter II), with ML extras in Chapter IV. HFC MD prevails for MHFL where inconsistent. Restructuring is a sibling MD: `RBI/DOR/2025-26/357`.

**1. Ind AS filers still run IRAC in parallel — paras 34–36 [EFFECTIVE: with the 2025 MD]**

Para **34**: hold impairment allowances as required by Ind AS. **In parallel**, classify and compute provisions as per these Directions — including **standard-asset provisioning, borrower-wise classification, restructured-asset provisioning, NPA ageing**. That parallel run **is the prudential floor**.

Para **35**: where Ind AS 109 ECL **<** the floor, appropriate the difference from **net profit or loss after tax** to a separate **‘Impairment Reserve’**. That balance is **not reckoned for regulatory capital**. **No withdrawals** without prior permission of **DoS**.

Para **36**: the appropriation **counts toward the floor** but is **not reckoned for calculating net NPAs**.

Plain English: ECL can be kinder than IRAC (especially on a well-collateralised gold book with low LGD). RBI does not let you take that kindness into CRAR. The shortfall sits in a locked reserve. NNPA still nets **only specific IRAC provisions**, not the Impairment Reserve and not the 0.40% standard line.

**2. What the floor is made of — ML gold, live MD, not bank IRAC**

Do **not** import **bank** slabs (SS 15% / 25%). The live NBFC MD is:

| Bucket | Rate | Where | In NNPA? |
|--------|------|-------|----------|
| Standard — ML (incl. microfinance) | **0.40%** of outstanding | Chapter IV para **55** | **No** (explicit) |
| Sub-standard | **10%** of total outstanding | Chapter II para **32(1)** | Yes — specific |
| Doubtful — unsecured portion | **100%** | para **32(2)(i)** | Yes |
| Doubtful-1 (≤1 year) secured | **20%** of realisable security | para **32(2)(ii)** | Yes |
| Doubtful-2 (1–3 years) secured | **30%** | same | Yes |
| Doubtful-3 (>3 years) secured | **50%** | same | Yes |
| Loss | Write off, or **100%** if kept on books | para **32(3)** | Yes |

ML Chapter IV **adds** the 0.40% standard line on top of Chapter II paras 28–36. It does **not** replace para 32 with bank rates.

Gold jewellery that is a valid pledge is **secured** for this table. A gold loan that has been NPA ≤12 months is **sub-standard at 10% of outstanding**, not 15%. After 12 months in NPA it is **doubtful** (para **53**): 20/30/50 of the **realisable** ornament value, **100%** of any shortfall. Auction surplus is the borrower’s (KLM Axiva, PR 1181) — it is not a provision write-back you get to keep.

UL (Muthoot Finance, not you) additionally gets a **differentiated** standard table (para **58**): SME/individual housing 0.25%, CRE-RH 0.75%, CRE 1.00%, teaser housing 2.00% then 0.40%, other 0.40%. MFL is **ML** — para **55** 0.40% on the **whole** standard book, gold included.

**3. Borrower-wise is part of the floor (paras 23–25, 51(8))**

Asset classification is **borrower-wise, not facility-wise**. If gold loan A is NPA, gold loan B and any MSME line to the same borrower are NPA too (para **23** + ML para **51(8)**). Upgrade only when **entire arrears of interest and principal on all facilities** are paid (paras **24–25**). That borrower-wise run is inside para 34’s floor. ECL staging can still be instrument-level under Ind AS 109 — another reason the two numbers diverge, and another reason the bridge exists.

**4. What this is *not***

- Not a new circular today.
- Not a rewrite of the 90-day clock (still **>90 days, day-end, account then borrower** — paras 18–19, 51). Last Tuesday covered that.
- Not SNFA. Paras **40C–40D** [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-01] are income on **property taken in extinguishment**. Pledged gold is not an SNFA. Do not re-open 6 Oct.
- Not the 7-step denominator. Denominator for GNPA% remains **gross carrying amount**. Provisioning is the **numerator / P&L / reserve** story.

**Why It Matters**

- The 14 Nov LODR 52 pack will show **ECL** (accounts) and **GNPA/NNPA** (IRAC). If you only print ECL, the notes will not tie to RBI returns. If you only print IRAC, the P&L will not tie to Ind AS.
- A gold book with low LGD often has **ECL < IRAC floor** on standard + early NPA. That gap is **Impairment Reserve**, not free capital. It **does not** help CRAR (yesterday’s lecture) and **does not** reduce NNPA (para 36).
- Using **bank** SS 15% in an internal model overstates the NBFC floor and can fake an Impairment Reserve that the MD does not require.
- Standard 0.40% is a **full-book** prudential charge. It is **outside** NNPA and **inside** the floor. On a ₹X standard gold book that is 40 bps before ECL and before specific NPA provision.
