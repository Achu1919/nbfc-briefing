### Fit and proper, CRO, CCO — Governance Directions Chapter IV (ML)

**What is it?**
The rulebook for *who sits on the Board, who signs risk and compliance, and how they get paid* — separate from how you treat borrowers (that is RBC) and separate from CRAR (that is Capital Adequacy). For an ML gold-loan ICC it is the file DoS uses to test whether the Board is a real control, or a family letterhead.

**The Technical Details**

**Source:** *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Governance) Directions, 2025*, `RBI/DOR/2025-26/344`, `DOR.GOV.REC.No.263/18-10-013/2025-26`, **28 Nov 2025**, [updated as on 24 Jun 2026](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12960). PDF: [344MD…](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/344MD22F0E87F6DD848919A6459236939D76F.PDF). [EFFECTIVE:2025-11-28]. Sole amendment so far: **Governance Amendment Directions, 2026**, `RBI/2026-27/165`, **24 Jun 2026** ([Id=13541](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13541)) — a **government-owned UL** carve-out on para 43 listing. **Not MFL.**

**Layer map (para 3(1)):**

| Layer | Chapters that bind |
|-------|--------------------|
| **BL** | Chapter III only |
| **ML** (MFL) | Chapters **II + III + IV** |
| **UL** (Muthoot Finance, the other group) | Chapters II + III + IV + **V** |

Applies to ICC / Factor / MFI / IFC / IDF / HFC. **Not** NOFHCs. Compensation paras 29–37 do **not** apply to government-owned NBFCs. MFL is a **private ML ICC with customer interface and public funds** → **full II–IV**.

The 2023 SBR “UL = 6 directors, 50% independent, woman director, non-executive chair, CEO age 70” table **does not live in this MD**. Those 2023 Chapter VIII instructions were **repealed** by `DOR.RRC.REC.302/33-01-010/2025-26` dated **28 Nov 2025** (para 44). Companies Act 2013 / LODR still apply on their own facts (public company, equity-listed vs debt-listed). Do **not** quote the old SBR table as if it were today’s Governance MD.

**Chapter II — four Board policies ML/UL must have (para 7):**

1. **Fit-and-proper** of directors at appointment **and on a continuing basis** (para 12).
2. **CCO role and responsibilities** — written, to promote a compliance culture (para 25(2)).
3. **Compensation policy** — misaligned pay / excessive risk-taking (para 29).
4. **Board-review calendar** for the items in para 15.

**Chapter III — every layer, including BL (paras 8–11):**

| Rule | Number | Why it bites |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Bank / NBFC experience on the Board | **At least 1 director** | A gold book with a purely trading/family Board fails para 8 |
| Risk Management Committee | Board **or** executive level; reports to Board; covers **liquidity** | Not UL-only. ML must have one. |
| Change in >**30%** of directors (excl. IDs) | **Prior written RBI permission** via **PRAVAAH**; re-election on rotation is carved out | Still **inform** RBI/NHB of *any* director/management change |

**Chapter IV — the ML operating chapter (this is MFL):**

**Fit and proper (paras 12–14).** Board policy + internal continuing due diligence. RBI can examine fit-and-proper of *any* NBFC’s directors in public interest, **asset size irrelevant**. Process:

1. Due diligence on qualification, technical expertise, track record, integrity → **Annex II** declaration from proposed / existing directors.
2. **NRC** (para 18) scrutinises the signed declaration and accepts or not.
3. **Every 31 March**, a simple declaration that nothing has changed — or the change, forthwith.
4. **Deed of Covenants — Annex III**, in public interest.
5. **Quarterly** statement of change of directors + **MD/CEO certificate** that fit-and-proper was followed, to the **DoS Regional Office** where registered, **within 15 days** of quarter-end. The **31 March** statement is **auditor-certified**.

That last row is an FP&A / CS calendar item: Q2 change-of-directors statement was due **15 Oct**. If it went out, fine; if it is sitting on someone’s desk, it is already late.

**Committees (paras 16–18).** Audit Committee: **not less than 3** Board members; Companies Act s177 powers if that section already applies. Audit Committee owns the **Information System Audit** periodicity in the Outsourcing MD (`id=12941`). NRC: Companies Act s178 constitution / powers / duties. Government-owned NBFCs get a Nomination Committee in lieu.

**CRO (paras 19–24) — asset-size trigger, not layer trigger.** Appoint a CRO if **asset size > ₹5,000 crore**. MFL is well above that. Independence rules:

- Senior official, professional qualification / experience in risk.
- Direct reporting to **MD&CEO or RMCB**. If to MD&CEO, RMC/Board meets the CRO **without** the MD&CEO **at least quarterly**.
- **No** reporting into business verticals, **no** business targets, **no dual hatting**.
- Fixed tenure, Board-approved; premature transfer/removal = Board approval **plus** report to DoS RO; if listed, also to the stock exchange.
- All credit products (retail **or** wholesale) **vetted by the CRO** for inherent and control risk. In credit sanction the CRO is an **advisor**, unless the NBFC uses a committee and the CRO is a decision-maker — then the CRO **votes** and **every** member is **individually and severally** liable, including for the risk view.

**CCO (para 25).** Appoint one, sufficiently senior. Board-approved role paper. This is the person who will certify DLA filings on CIMS, recovery-agent lists, and the fit-and-proper certificate trail.

**KMP dual-office (para 26).** KMP of an ML/UL NBFC shall **not** hold any office (including directorships) in **any other ML or UL NBFC**, except directorship in a **subsidiary**. They **may** take a BL directorship. Explanation is explicit. A Muthoot Pappachan KMP sitting on two ML books is a para 26 problem unless one is a subsidiary of the other.

**Independent directors (paras 27–28).** An ID shall not be on the Board of **more than three** ML/UL NBFCs at once (Companies Act outer limit still applies). BL seats are extra, subject to the Act. The Board must ensure **no conflict** from an ID sitting on another NBFC at the same time.

**Compensation (paras 29–37).** Board policy covering at least (i) fixed / variable principles and (ii) **malus / clawback**. NRC frames, reviews, implements; works with RMC so pay is risk-aligned; checks ICAAP capital retention; owns fit-and-proper of directors / KMP / senior management.

| Pay rule | What the MD actually says |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Variable pay | Share-linked, or cash + share-linked; must be able to go to **zero**; higher jobs → higher variable share |
| Deferral | **Some** variable **may** be deferred to the risk horizon; Board decides the portion and the period — **not** a hard 40% / 3-year floor in this MD |
| Control functions | Risk / compliance / internal audit / financial control: **higher fixed**, still **some** variable so malus/clawback has something to bite |
| Guaranteed bonus | **No**, except a joining / sign-on bonus on new hire, which is **neither** fixed **nor** variable |
| Malus vs clawback | Malus = stop unvested deferred pay. Clawback = take back **already paid / vested**. Trigger: subdued/negative performance **or** misconduct. Criteria + application window covering at least deferral **and** retention. |

Do **not** import the old “variable cap 200% of fixed / 40% deferred 3 years” UL table from 2023 SBR into an ML Board note. That is not what paras 29–37 say.

**Misc (paras 38–40).** Whistle-blower mechanism. Board ensures governance in **subsidiaries** (MHFL sits here). Internal corporate-governance guidelines, Board-approved, **published on the website**.

**Chapter V — UL only (paras 41–43).** Mix of education/experience on the Board; report to supervisors if an ID leaves early; **mandatory listing within 3 years** of UL identification, with listed-company disclosures even before listing. **24 Jun 2026 proviso:** para 43 **does not apply** to a **fully government-owned and controlled** UL. MFL is **not UL**. Muthoot Finance is the UL gold-loan *peer*, not you.

**Why It Matters**
Governance is how RBI decides whether the CRO who vetted the gold product, the CCO who certified the DLA / recovery-agent file, and the NRC that passed the director are real. CoR PRs 1294/1295 (9 Oct) are what happens at the *other* end of the licence — cancellation. Fit-and-proper is the front end. A promoter-family ML with listed NCDs that cannot produce (a) Annex II/III files, (b) the 15-day quarterly director statement, (c) a CRO with no dual hat and a quarterly closed-door with the RMC, and (d) a compensation policy with a written malus/clawback trigger, will not survive a DoS inspection on a 69% gold book.
