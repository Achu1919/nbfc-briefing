### Certificate of Registration — Registration MD Chapters III–IV

**What is it?**
The CoR is not a trophy on the wall. It is the **permission under s45-IA of the RBI Act, 1934** to transact the business of a non-banking financial institution. Without it (or without a specific exemption), you cannot do NBFI business. With it, you are inside SBR. RBI takes it away under **s45-IA(6)** — that is what last Friday-week’s PRs were.

**The Technical Details**

**Source:** *Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2025*, `RBI/DOR/2025-26/339`, `DOR.FIN.REC.No.258/03.10.119/2025-26`, **28 Nov 2025**, [updated as on 1 Jul 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12965). PDF: [339MD…](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/339MD7166A79E96D64572B378949D3972DBD1.PDF). [EFFECTIVE:2025-11-28]. Two amendments that actually moved the perimeter:

1. **Registration Amendment Directions, 2026**, **29 Apr 2026** ([Id=13407](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13407&Mode=0)) — Type I / unregistered Type I. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-07-01]**.
2. **Second Amendment Directions, 2026**, **24 Jun 2026** (`RBI/2026-27/163`) — UL identification is now a hard audited-asset cut of **₹1,00,000 crore**; parametric scoring **repealed**. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-06-24]**.

**Layer map (this is the SBR identity, not a Board-composition table):**

| Layer | Who (from 24 Jun 2026) | NOF | What the CoR buys you |
|-------|------------------------|-----|------------------------|

| **ML** (MFL) | Assets **₹1,000 cr+** and **not** on the annual UL list; also NBFC-D / HFC / CIC / IFC / IDF / SPD **by activity** | ₹10 cr | Full prudential stack |

Once named UL, enhanced rules apply for **at least five years** even if assets later fall. MFL is **ML**. Muthoot Finance is the **UL gold-loan peer, different company**. Do not copy UL listing / CET1 into an MFL Board note.

**Chapter III — getting and keeping the CoR**

- **s45-IA** is the gate. You apply through **PRAVAAH**. RBI issues a CoR if it is satisfied the company will transact NBFI business and the fit-and-proper / NOF / activity tests are met.
- **NOF is a going-concern test**, not a one-time cheque. Fall below the layer minimum and the CoR is in play — that is a capital-planning event for FP&A, not a secretarial footnote.
- **Change of activity / public funds / customer interface** is not a free option. A Type I that wants to take public funds or face customers must seek registration as **Type II** *before* it does either. The 29 Apr Amd says so in terms.

**Type I vs Unregistered Type I (29 Apr Amd, live from 1 Jul 2026) — paras 38A and 65A**

This is the only 2026 rewrite of the *perimeter*, and the 9 Oct surrender PR showed it working.

| Bucket | Assets | Public funds | Customer interface | What happens |
|--------|--------|--------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Unregistered Type I** | **< ₹1,000 cr** (latest audited BS) | None | None | Exempt from **s45-IA and s45-IC**. Must be a **conscious, long-term** business model. Annual **Board resolution** at the start of the FY that it will not take public funds or have customer interface. Disclose status in **Notes to Accounts**. Still inside Chapter III-B for everything else; RBI can still issue entity-specific instructions and use Chapter V penalties. |
| **Registered Type I** | **₹1,000 cr+** | None | None | Must **hold a CoR as Type I**. Same no-funds / no-interface discipline. Statutory auditor files an **exception report** to DoS if either condition is broken. |
| **Type II** (MFL) | Any | Yes and/or customer interface | Yes | Full CoR. This is you. |

Existing Type I / no-funds-no-interface names that qualify for the exemption **may apply for deregistration by 31 Dec 2026** on PRAVAAH (company letterhead + prescribed pack). RBI will only cancel if it is satisfied the model is genuine and long-term. **Aparna Investment Pvt Ltd** (Mumbai) is the first Type I exit to show up in a CoR PR — cancelled **30 Sep 2026**, published **9 Oct** (PR 1294).

**Overseas investment carve-out:** an Unregistered Type I that wants to invest overseas in **financial** services must get a Type I CoR first and take **prior RBI approval**. It **cannot** invest overseas in the non-financial sector.

**Chapter IV — exemptions** are narrow. Do not read “unregistered Type I” as “unregulated”. Exemption is only from **s45-IA and s45-IC**. Conduct of NBFI business still sits under Chapter III-B.

**Why It Matters**
Licensing is how RBI decides who is allowed to sit in the gold-loan / MSME / MFI market at all. Governance (yesterday) is *who sits on the Board of a company that already has a CoR*. Digital lending (2 Oct) is *how that company originates*. If the CoR goes, none of the rest matters. For FP&A the live questions are: (1) are we clearly Type II ML with public funds and customer interface — yes; (2) is any group vehicle sitting in the Type I / unregistered Type I window that needs a 31 Dec PRAVAAH filing; (3) does any vendor / DSA / recovery agent on our panel appear on PR 1294/1295.
