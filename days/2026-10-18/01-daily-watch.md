## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Sunday: Weekly Preview + Sector Health Check

### Where the sector stands into the week of 20 Oct 2026

**1. Growth is back, but the mix has changed.**  
Retail NBFCs are printing mid-20s AUM growth (LTF retail +29%, Bajaj Finance provisional +26.5%, Bajaj Housing +25%). Gold is the star growth engine in brokerage math (~44% AUM growth expected) and in LTF’s own +242% gold-book sprint. Diversified retail is healthy; pure rural/MFI is the cautious sleeve.

**2. Asset quality: two-speed.**  
- *Healing / healthy:* housing (BHFL sub-30 bps GNPA), LTF Stage-3 improvement, personal-loan specialised NBFC vintage repair (CRISIL).  
- *Watchlist:* microfinance in drought states; gold LTV buffers after ~23% price drawdown from peak; unsecured books if rates/inflation squeeze households further (Ind-Ra recently flagged CPI possibly breaching 6% in Oct with further policy tightening risk).

**3. Liability side is the quiet stress point.**  
AAA NBFC bond yields **+54 bps** since June; RBI has delivered a rate hike after a long pause (early Oct). LTF showed CoF can still fall QoQ with mix/FCNR-liquidity help, but Jefferies expects **NIM compression** at Bajaj/Chola. Capital is plentiful at the top (Bajaj ₹17,500 cr plan; Shriram post-MUFG AAA; PNB Housing full AAA suite) — **funding cost**, not solvency, is the near-term earnings swing factor.

**4. Regulatory weather.**  
CoR cancellations/surrenders continue the cleanup of non-serious entities. Revolving-credit clarification, digital-lending conduct, IRDAI distribution economics, and completed 90-day NPA norm define the operating system. Deep circular analysis → **NBFC Guru**.

**5. Competitive map relevant to Muthoot Fincorp.**  
- Banks + AAA diversified NBFCs (LTF, Bajaj, others) scaling **gold** via branches and price.  
- Gold price correction tests **collateral maths** industry-wide.  
- Digital personal-loan specialists re-accelerating after underwriting reset — less direct gold overlap, more household leverage backdrop.  
- MFI stress is a rural cash-flow signal that can spill into gold-loan demand (pledge for liquidity) even as it hurts pure MFIs.
