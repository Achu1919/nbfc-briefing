## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Gold Loan (Tuesday focus)

### Where the book stands
- NBFC gold loans were still printing **~68–70% YoY growth** through mid-2026 (RBI flow data: ~₹3.3–3.41 lakh crore range in May–June prints; July still ~68.5% YoY per Fortune India/RBI).
- Jefferies (recent franchise note): retail gold-jewellery loans **+80% YoY** as of July 2026; all gold-backed (incl. agri) **+50% YoY to ~₹18.6 lakh crore**, ~11% of retail credit. Prime+ share of gold borrowers up **43% → 52%** (CY22–25) — product is mainstreaming, not just distress monetisation.

### Competitive geometry (critical for Muthoot Fincorp)
| Player | Gold posture |
|---|---|
| **Muthoot Finance** | Category leader; ~6,100+ branches (Jefferies Jun’26 ref); defending with ticket/LTV variants + BT products; Jefferies more **balanced/Hold-ish** at ~2.1× book given large base |
| **Manappuram** | Smaller base = higher growth torque; Jefferies **Buy**, TP narrative ~₹430 / ~34% upside in Sep note; +500 gold branches planned FY27 |
| **Bajaj Finance** | Fastest large new entrant: 2,022 gold branches, +321 in Q2 alone; FY27 exit 2,700–2,800; gold AUM path toward multi-tens of thousands of crore |
| **L&T Finance** | Gold book ₹5,043 cr (+242% YoY); path to ~800 branches FY27; Jasprit Bumrah campaign |
| **IIFL Finance** | Gold ~₹65,000 cr, 50–60% of AUM — concentration acknowledged; wants long-term diversification but near-term gold stays core |
| **Tata Capital / ABCL / Godrej** | Documented expansion via branches/tech/M&A interest (Aug coverage) |

**Geography:** South (TN, AP, TS, KA) still ~**70%** of gold loans, but **originations** swinging to UP, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, MP — exactly where diversified and new-entrant branch adds are clustering.

**Unit economics of new branches:** Jefferies — new gold branches opex/AUM **6–8%** initially vs **2.5–3.7%** for Muthoot/Manappuram; ~18-month break-even → **limits near-term price wars**, but local rate teasers already appear in selected markets.

**Collateral / price risk:** Gold had a sharp run then a pullback (~−6% after +15% August move in the Jefferies frame); BL strip still shows extremely high domestic gold levels. Higher prices = better LTV buffers and top-up capacity; a sustained correction would slow AUM growth and test newer, higher-LTV books first.

**RBI operating flexibility:** Earlier 2026 removal of prior-approval for branch expansion by large gold-lending NBFC-ICCs (>1,000 branches) unlocked the Bajaj/LTF/Muthoot-style network race — structural, not one-day news, but it is why FY27 branch math is this aggressive.

### Why it matters at Muthoot Fincorp today
1. **Share defence in South** still franchise-critical, but **North/West density** is where incremental gold AUM is being contested.  
2. **Short-tenor / teaser products** are under scrutiny after Bajaj’s Stage 2/3 gold spike — product governance and LTV discipline > headline rate.  
3. **Funding:** Any CRR-led firming of bank term loans hits pure-play gold NBFCs with thinner liability franchises harder than Bajaj (deposits 14%) or AAA names (LTF).  
4. **Capitalised competitors** (IIFL raise, Piramal ₹3,850 cr, Bajaj internal generation, LTF gold build) mean the “easy gold” phase is over; service speed, appraisal quality, and BT defence matter more than branch count alone.
