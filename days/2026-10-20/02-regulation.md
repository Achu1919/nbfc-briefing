### NPA classification & provisioning for NBFCs (and why gold is not SNFA)

**What is it?**

IRAC is the rulebook that turns a late EMI into a **non-performing asset**, stops you booking interest as income, and tells you how much provision to hold. For NBFCs it lives in one Master Direction — **not** the bank IRAC master circular. Mixing the two is the most expensive numbering error in an NBFC board pack.

**The Technical Details**

**Source of truth:** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025 — `RBI/DOR/2025-26/356`, DOR.STR.REC.No.275/21.04.048/2025-26, **updated as on 1 Oct 2026**. [MD](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12948). Applies to **all layers**, including HFCs (HFC Directions prevail where inconsistent) and NBFC-MFIs (MFI Directions prevail on MFI-specific points). Restructuring sits in a **sister** MD: Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions, 2025 (`357`).

**Classification (paras 18–19):**
- Account-level. An account is **NPA when overdue > 90 days**. Same 90-day clock for BL, ML and UL. There is no “NBFC gets 120 days” leftover from the pre-2022 glide path.
- Classification is a **day-end process**. An operational lag in flagging is treated as **non-adherence**, not a timing difference (Nov 12, 2021 circular DOR.STR.REC.68/21.04.048/2021-22; Sept 14, 2020 automation principles).
- Classes: **Standard → Sub-standard → Doubtful (D1 / D2 / D3 at 12 / 24 / 36 months in doubtful) → Loss**.
- **Upgradation** only when **entire arrears across ALL facilities of that borrower** are repaid. Paying down one gold loan while another facility of the same borrower is still overdue does **not** upgrade the borrower.

**Income recognition:** On NPA, stop accruing interest to P&L. Interest that was accrued and not realised is reversed. Post-NPA accrual sits in **interest suspense** and is **excluded** from GNPA outstanding (para 12.2).

**Provisioning — use the NBFC statute, not the bank one.** Chapter II para 32 applies to all layers. ML Chapter IV **adds** the 0.40% standard line. Bank sub-standard 15%/25% and bank doubtful 25/40/100 are the **wrong statute**.

| Category | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Standard — ML (incl. microfinance) | **0.40%** of outstanding — **not** reckoned for NNPA | ML para 55 |
| Standard — UL | SME/individual housing 0.25%; teaser housing 2.00% then 0.40%; CRE-RH 0.75%; CRE 1.00%; other 0.40% | UL para 58 |
| Standard — BL | 0.25% (not in NNPA) | para 48 |
| Sub-standard | **10%** of total outstanding | para 32(1) |
| Doubtful — unsecured portion | **100%** of amount not covered by realisable security | para 32(2)(i) |
| Doubtful-1 (≤1 yr) secured | **20%** of realisable security | para 32(2)(ii) |
| Doubtful-2 (1–3 yr) secured | **30%** | para 32(2)(ii) |
| Doubtful-3 (>3 yr) secured | **50%** | para 32(2)(ii) |
| Loss | 100% (or write-off) | para 32(3) |
| Ind AS 109 ECL < IRAC floor | Difference appropriated from PAT to **Impairment Reserve** — not CET1; no withdrawal without DoS; counts to floor but **not** to NNPA | paras 34–36 |

There is **no** 1% standard add-on in this IRAC MD for gold LTV >75%. Do not invent one.

**The 1 Oct overlay — SNFA (Specified Non-Financial Assets):**
- Instrument: **IRAC Third Amendment Directions, 2026** — `RBI/2026-27/197`, DOR.STR.REC.162/21-04-048/2026-27, **16 Jul 2026**, **effective 1 Oct 2026**. [Circular](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13575). Twin of the RSA Second Amendment the same day.
- Inserts **paras 40C–40D** in Chapter II:
  - **40C:** Accrued but **unrealised** interest/charges from the *extinguished* exposure, for periods **before** you acquired the SNFA, **cannot** be recognised as income on acquisition. If you already booked it on SNFA stock outstanding as on **30 Sep 2026**, reverse through P&L **latest by 30 Sep 2027**, to the extent still unrealised.
  - **40D:** Cash you actually receive from an SNFA = **non-interest / other income** in the year realised. Upkeep cost = expense in the year incurred. No “interest income” camouflage.
- **What SNFA is:** a **non-financial** asset taken over in resolution of a stressed **financial** asset (the land, plant, building you ended up owning because the borrower defaulted). **Gold jewellery pledged as collateral is not an SNFA.** You did not “acquire” the gold as a substitute asset in a resolution; you hold a pledge. Auction surplus still belongs to the borrower (KLM Axiva fine is the reminder). Do not run a 40C reversal exercise on the gold book.

**GNPA / NNPA — MFL’s 7-step (standalone):**
Denominator is **gross carrying amount**, not AUM, not net of loss allowance.
1. Gross AUM
2. − ARC sold
3. − DA & co-lending off-book
4. + accrued interest
5. − fee amortisation (Ind AS 109 EIR)
6. = “net loans” = gross carrying amount → **GNPA% denominator**
7. NNPA = GNPA − **specific** provisions against NPAs (standard-asset 0.40% is **not** netted).

MHFL (subsidiary) uses on-book AUM directly and skips accrual + amortisation — that will **not** tie to audited notes. Flag it at consolidation. Co-lending partner share was never on book (CLA 2025 para 28: borrower-level SMA/NPA is still binding across REs). Technical write-offs drop out of audited GNPA but stay NPA for borrower-level tracking.

**Why It Matters**

Q2 FY27 numbers are already frozen at **30 Sep day-end**. The 14 Nov LODR 52 pack will print GNPA, NNPA and provisions from this MD. A wrong slab (bank 15% sub-standard instead of NBFC 10%) or an AUM-based GNPA% is a **disclosure defect**, not a rounding difference. SNFA 40C is a **FY28** P&L item for anyone who actually holds taken-over property; it is not a Q2 restatement for a gold NBFC.

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Parameter | Number | Applies |
|---|---|---|
| NPA clock | **> 90 days** overdue | All layers |
| ML standard provision | **0.40%** | ML (MFL) |
| Sub-standard | **10%** of outstanding | All |
| Doubtful secured | **20 / 30 / 50%** at ≤1 / 1–3 / >3 yr | All |
| Unsecured doubtful / Loss | **100%** | All |
| SNFA reverse-by date | **30 Sep 2027** | Only if you hold SNFA stock that had unrealised pre-acquisition interest on 30 Sep 2026 |
| Gold jewellery | **Not SNFA** | MFL gold book |

**Quiz yourself:**
1. A gold-loan borrower is 91 days overdue on one loan and current on another. Can you keep the current loan as standard? *(No. Borrower-level: all facilities follow the NPA. Upgradation only when **all** arrears are cleared.)*
2. You take over a factory in a one-time settlement and had accrued ₹12 lakh interest on the extinguished loan, still unrealised on 30 Sep 2026. What do you do, and by when? *(Do not recognise it as income on taking over the factory. Reverse through P&L by **30 Sep 2027** if still unrealised. Subsequent rent/sale proceeds = other income when cash arrives. **This is SNFA. A pledged gold ornament is not.**)*
3. Q2 GNPA% in the investor deck is on AUM. The LODR 52 notes use gross carrying amount. Which one is the regulatory ratio, and what is the 7-step bridge? *(Notes / RBI returns = book. AUM is a deck convention. Gross AUM − ARC − DA/co-lend off-book + accrued interest − fee amortisation = denominator. Do not net loss allowance in the denominator.)*
