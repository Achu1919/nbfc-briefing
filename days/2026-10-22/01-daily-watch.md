## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Gold-loan stress signals went mainstream — directly relevant to the Muthoot businesses.**
- Piramal Finance MD & CEO Jairam Sridharan said on his Q2 call that **15–23% of industry-wide gross gold-loan disbursements in FY26 went to borrowers already >90 days overdue on at least one other loan** — a flow he valued at ~₹4.7 trillion and called "a shockingly high number." Piramal itself lends to no such borrowers (less than 1% of its October gold disbursals). ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/large-non-bank-lenders-flag-early-signs-of-stress-in-gold-loan-portfolios-126102101390_1.html))
- Macquarie's Suresh Ganapathy: gold loans are now India's **second-largest retail lending category after mortgages (~28% of the non-housing retail book)**, and collections teams at some lenders are effectively directing overdue borrowers to pledge gold and borrow elsewhere to clear NPAs — "their basic ability to pay hasn't changed." ([TOI](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/surge-in-gold-loans-partly-reflects-stress-say-lenders/articleshow/135100957.cms))
- Bajaj Finance **stopped offering short-tenor gold schemes in August** after Stage-2 gold loans rose ₹1,047 cr (to ₹1,099 cr) and gold-loan GNPA rose to 0.70% from 0.35% QoQ; MD Rajeev Jain: "better to manage it through risk rather than manage it through auction." ([EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/Bajaj-Finance-Ends-Short-Term-Gold-Loan-Schemes-as-Credit-Risk-Rises-1757807), [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/bajaj-finance-share-price-q2-result-reaction-fy27-guidance-aum-margin-repo-rate-buy-sell-target-outlook-20015660.htm))
- Context: gold is ~23% off its early-2026 peak (CreditSights, Oct 12) — cushion on loans booked at peak prices is thinner; gold hit a one-week low on Oct 21. Peer gold-financier stocks fell (see Market). ([Finance Intellect](https://financeintellect.com/home-page/home/indias-gold-loan-boom-may-meet-its-first-real-test/))

FY27 CPI forecast raised to 5.2%; GDP growth 7.1%. "Rate cuts are off the table in the near term — policy action ahead can only be a rate hike or a pause." ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63839), [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/rbi-mpc-minutes-signal-rate-hike-or-pause-2-members-oppose-stance-change-14056907.html))
