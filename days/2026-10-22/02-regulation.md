### Responsible Business Conduct: gold auctions, recovery agents, and the SC overlay

**What is it?**

Two different files, often mixed in a board pack:

1. **How you treat the borrower and the pledged gold** — already live in the **Responsible Business Conduct (RBC)** Master Direction. This is Fair Practices, KFS, gold-auction notice / surplus / reserve price, MFI recovery hours.
2. **How you engage a recovery *agency*** — the **6 Aug 2026 Third Amendment**, **effective 1 Jan 2027**. IIBF, Board policy, 08:00–19:00, six-month call recording.

Governance MD (`id=12960`) is *who sits on the Board*. RBC MD (`id=12942`) is *how you treat the customer*. Mixing them is the same class of error as quoting bank IRAC in an NBFC pack.

**The Technical Details**

**Source of truth (live today):** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025 — `RBI/DOR/2025-26/362`, DOR.MCS.REC.No.281/01-01-039/2025-26, **28 Nov 2025**, [updated as on 1 Jul 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12942). PDF: [362MD…](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/362MD26CA543937BA439A97E1BCFC08CF5808.PDF). **[EFFECTIVE:2025-11-28]**. Applies to **all layers** of ICC / Factor / MFI / IFC / IDF / HFC / deposit-taking NBFC **with customer interface**. **Not** CIC, AA, SPD, NOFHC, MGC, or Type I with no customer interface. MFL = **ML ICC with customer interface** → the whole file.

**Coming 1 Jan 2027:** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Responsible Business Conduct) **Third Amendment** Directions, 2026, issued **6 Aug 2026** (`RBI/2026-27/230`). **[EFFECTIVE:2027-01-01]**. Inserts the comprehensive recovery-agent chapter (paras **100A–100AB** in the working draft). Nine parallel entity-wise circulars went out the same day (banks, SFBs, NBFCs, HFCs…). Do **not** wait for a restamp of `id=12942` before starting the project — the amendment circular is the law from 1 Jan.

**Board policies that must already exist (RBC para 7):** FPC (vernacular), grievance redressal, interest/penal-charge principles, **gold and silver collateral**, MFI FPC, floating-rate personal-loan reset, **engagement of recovery agents**, **code of conduct for DSA / DMA / recovery agents**. Board reviews FPC + grievance functioning; a consolidated report goes to the Board at regular intervals (para 8).

**Gold-auction conduct — live now (Chapter III G.6–G.7, paras 60–70).** This is the file KLM Axiva was fined on.

| Rule | Number | Why it bites |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Auction notice to borrower / legal heir, copy + acknowledgement on file | para 60 | If you cannot locate them, **public notice + one month** before you auction |
| Public advertisement in **two** newspapers (regional + national daily) | para 61 | “Branch WhatsApp group” is not an advertisement |
| Auctioneer = trained employee **or** empanelled auctioneer; employee auctions need surprise visits + internal audit | para 62 | Outsourcing MD still applies |
| Reserve price **≥ 90%** of current value; after **two failed** auctions, **≥ 85%** | para 63 | Do not invent a 75% LTV-style reserve |
| **First** auction **physical, same district** as the lending branch. Only after a failed first auction: adjoining district **or** online | para 64 | This is why a wave of simultaneous auctions is an *ops* constraint, not just a price risk |
| NBFC **and related parties shall not bid** | para 65 | Conflict, not a commercial option |
| Full auction particulars to borrower; **surplus refund within 7 working days** of receiving full proceeds | para 66 | **KLM Axiva ₹2.70 lakh, PR 1181, 24 Sep 2026** — this paragraph |
| Collateral return: **same day, max 7 working days** after full repayment | para 58 | Delay attributable to the NBFC = **₹5,000 per day** (para 69) |
| Unclaimed gold/silver **> 2 years** after full repayment → special drive; half-yearly report to CSC / Board | paras 71–72 | Customer-service, not NPA |

Prudential LTV / bullet / assay sit in the **Credit Facilities** MD (`id=12957`), not here. RBC para 75 points you there. Do not quote LTV 75% as an RBC number.

**General recovery (non-MFI) — live now (para 100):** no intimidation, no public humiliation, no privacy intrusion on family/referees/friends, no threatening/anonymous calls, **no calling before 08:00 or after 19:00** for overdue recovery, no false representations. **These hours do not apply to microfinance loans.**

**MFI recovery — live now (paras 89–97):** harsher list, **and a tighter clock** — calling before **09:00** or after **18:00** is deemed harsh (para 91(2)). Recovery at a mutually designated place; field visit to residence/work only after the borrower misses **two successive** designated appointments (para 90). Recovery-agency list on the website; agent carries notice + authorisation + ID.

**Repossession of financed vehicles (paras 26–27):** built-in, legally enforceable repossession clause; notice period; when notice can be waived; procedure; **final chance before sale**; re-delivery procedure; sale/auction procedure. This is the paragraph the **16 Sep 2026 Supreme Court** recovery/repossession judgment sits on top of. **Still no RBI circular.** Chola-style compensation is the working assumption. Enforce the clause *and* the judgment now; do not wait for a restamp.

**Recovery-agent overlay from 1 Jan 2027 (Third Amendment, 6 Aug 2026) — not yet live, 71 days:**

1. “Recovery agency” is functional, not a label — you cannot hide behind “business correspondent” / “collection partner”.
2. **IIBF certification** for all recovery agents; existing agents certify within **one year** of the effective date (i.e. by **1 Jan 2028**).
3. Board-approved recovery policy: triggers, escalation, code of conduct, **borrower compensation for losses**, demise-of-borrower.
4. Agency list on the website, updated within **7 days** of any change.
5. Contact hours **08:00–19:00** unless the borrower expressly agrees otherwise (aligns with live para 100 for non-MFI).
6. Call recording, **6-month** retention.
7. Device-locking prohibited by default (narrow financed-device exception; one-hour reversal after repayment). Gold book is not a device-lock book.
8. Dedicated recovery grievance officer.

**Why It Matters**

DoS does not need a new circular to fine you. **KLM Axiva** (surplus not refunded) and **Hero Fincorp** (excess interest, PR 1180) are 24 Sep facts. Overnight gold-loan *colour* — 15–23% of FY26 industry gold flow to borrowers already NPA elsewhere, Bajaj shutting short-tenor schemes, gold off its early-2026 peak — raises the **probability of auctions**, not the **statute**. More auctions = more para 60–66 files for DoS to sample. First-auction-in-district is a capacity constraint if many branches hit the trigger together.

The 1 Jan 2027 overlay is a **five-month project that now has 71 days**. Board policy, contract renegotiation, IIBF pipeline, website list, call-recording retention. Starting in December is how you get a DoS observation in January.
