- **The MMFSL crash is a margin warning for vehicle financiers.** NIM −30 bps QoQ on excess liquidity and seasonal trade advances, with ~48% floating-rate borrowings and ₹27,000 cr maturing within six months — rising cost of funds is the sector's 2H theme (Emkay sees up to ~50 bps more repo hikes; JM Financial: vehicle financiers ~18% AUM growth but rising credit costs for Shriram/MMFSL). ([Emkay](https://images.moneycontrol.com/static-mcnews/2026/10/20261022044535_Mahindra-Finance-2210026-emkay.pdf), [ETBFSI/JM](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfc-aum-growth-seen-at-20-in-q2fy27-funding-costs-emerge-as-key-risk/134720832))
- **Festive retail is a tale of two bases.** FADA's Navratri read: total retail −6.5% YoY (1.12 mn units) on a shorter 10-day festival and a GST-cut-inflated base; PV −21.9% (182,728 units), 2W −2.79% (834,075). Versus Navratri 2024: total +26.8%, PV +10.4%, 2W +32.4%. Maruti's Ganesh–Dussehra retail +35% (bookings up to 2.7 lakh), Hyundai registrations +30%, Tata delivered 50,000+; dealer inventory 43–45 days. **Diwali is Nov 8 — the second half of the festive season is the real test.** ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/business/automobile/festive-season-auto-retail-sales-fall-6-5-percent-on-short-navratri-high-base/2999675/1), [TOI](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/carmakers-look-to-diwali-to-rev-up-festive-sales/articleshow/135097391.cms), [Autopunditz](https://www.autopunditz.com/post/festive-two-wheeler-vs-car-sales-2026-navratri))
- **EV lending is the structural growth engine.** Festive EV retail +26.8% to 117,437 units — a ~10.5% share of festive sales (vs 7.7% a year ago); September EV retail hit a record ~334,000 units (~13% penetration; PV-EV 8.45%). Shriram Finance now does **~₹300 cr/month of EV finance** (up from ₹50 cr) with "no delinquencies"; Astranova's Novafusion Capital won an RBI NBFC licence (Oct 14) targeting ₹5,000 cr of e-mobility AUM; L&T Finance's 2W book +24% YoY with disbursements +26%. Watch battery/shipping-driven supply constraints and rate hikes hitting affordability. ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/auto/festive-bookings-build-up-auto-dealers-see-strong-oct-despite-high-base-126100600442_1.html), [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/business/2026/Sep/01/shriram-finance-expects-overall-vehicle-finance-business-to-grow-by-15-20), [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/nbfc))
- **Read for Muthoot Fincorp:** two-wheeler/CV financiers are trading margin for growth into a rate-hike cycle; used-vehicle and rural books (Shriram, MMFSL) carry drought tail-risk in pockets — while the secured-collateral theme keeps Muthoot Finance's operating prints strong even as its stock de-rates with gold.
