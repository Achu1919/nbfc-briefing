### Digital Lending Directions, 2025

**What is it?**
If the loan is originated, underwritten, disbursed, serviced or recovered through a **remote, automated, largely digital** process — your own app, a partner’s app, or an LSP — you are inside the Digital Lending MD. Branch gold with physical jewellery on the counter is **not** automatically digital lending. The moment the same loan is pushed through an app / LSP / remote journey, the MD switches on. The MD does **not** care that the collateral is gold.

**The Technical Details**

**Source:** *Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025*, issued **8 May 2025**, [Notification Id=12848](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848&Mode=0), [PR 60403](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60403). **[EFFECTIVE:2025-05-08]**. Applies to **all REs** including NBFCs (all SBR layers) and HFCs. Consolidates the 2 Sep 2022 Guidelines and the 8 Jun 2023 DLG circular, and adds two *new* chapters that did not exist in 2022:

1. **Transparency in aggregation of loan products from multiple lenders** (draft 26 Apr 2024, finalised inside this MD).
2. **Public Directory of Digital Lending Apps** — REs report DLAs on **CIMS**; the public list went live **on or before 1 Jul 2025**. It is *as-submitted by the RE*, **not** an RBI licence of the app.

**Applies to:** All layers. Activity-based, not SBR-layer-based. BL gold-loan NBFC with an app is in. UL without an app is out.

**The six rules that actually bite:**

| Rule | What it means in the branch / on the app | Source |
|------|------------------------------------------|--------|
| **Disbursement & repayment through the borrower’s bank account** | No credit to an LSP, a DSA, a jeweller, a “convenience” wallet, or a third-party current account. Insurance premia / statutory fees are the only named carve-outs. | DLD (from 2022 Guidelines) |
| **KFS before the contract** | APR (all-in), total amount payable, schedule, penal charges, cooling-off, exit rights. Same KFS family as the **15 Apr 2024** KFS circular. | DLD + KFS circular |
| **Cooling-off / look-up** | Borrower can exit by paying **principal + proportionate APR only**. Floor: **≥ 3 days** if tenor ≥ 7 days; **≥ 1 day** if tenor < 7 days. No penalty inside the window. | 2022 Guidelines, now inside DLD |
| **LSP is your agent** | Written contract, due diligence, RE remains fully liable. LSP name is **not** a CoR. LSP must show *your* name and CoR on every screen. | DLD + Outsourcing MD |
| **Data in India** | Store in India. Process abroad only with explicit consent. No silent sharing with the LSP’s group, ad-tech, or a foreign parent. | DLD |
| **DLG cap 5%** | Originating RE may give a default-loss guarantee up to **5% of loans outstanding**. Form: cash / FD / bank guarantee as specified. Not a synthetic securitisation. | DLD (from 8 Jun 2023 DLG circular) |

**Multi-lender aggregator (the 2025 add):** if an LSP shows offers from more than one RE, the borrower must see **every matching offer** on one screen, plus the names of unmatched lenders. No dark-pattern ranking that hides the cheaper RE.

**DLA directory:** if MFL (or an LSP acting for MFL) runs an app that does digital lending, that app’s name must be on RBI’s public DLA list. Missing from the list ≠ illegal by itself (RBI does not validate), but a customer / DoS inspector who cannot find you will treat the app as unauthorised. Update CIMS when you add or kill an app.

**Why It Matters**
DoS fines digital-lending files on **flow of funds** and **KFS**, not on strategy decks. Hero Fincorp’s ₹10L (24 Sep, excess interest) is the cousin of this file. A gold-loan app that credits a DSA or a “gold-valuation partner” is a DLD breach even if LTV, IRAC and auction conduct are perfect.

**Key Numbers / Thresholds**

| Item | Number | Layer |
|------|--------|-------|
| Cooling-off, tenor ≥ 7 days | **≥ 3 calendar days** | All REs |
| Cooling-off, tenor < 7 days | **≥ 1 calendar day** | All REs |
| DLG cap | **5%** of loans outstanding | All REs in DLD |
| DLA public list | Live **1 Jul 2025** | All REs that deploy a DLA |
| Complaint clock (RE / LSP nodal GRO) | **30 days**, then RBI Ombudsman | All REs |
| Digital-lending definition | Remote **and** automated, largely digital, covering acquisition → recovery | All REs |

**Recent Changes**
- **Previously (2 Sep 2022 / 8 Jun 2023):** two circulars. **Now (from 8 May 2025):** one MD, plus aggregator transparency and the DLA directory.
- **Co-lending overlay (from 1 Jan 2026):** see box below. Digital CLA must satisfy **both** MDs. CLA para 5: DLD continues to govern digital lending; digital co-lend is *additionally* inside CLA, “without derogation to the MD-DLD”.

### CLA overlay — what changed on 1 Jan 2026

**Source:** *Reserve Bank of India (Co-Lending Arrangements) Directions, 2025*, `RBI/DOR/2025-26/139`, `DOR.STR.REC.44/13.07.010/2025-26`, **6 Aug 2025**, [Id=12888](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12888). **[EFFECTIVE:2026-01-01]** (or earlier if the RE opted in). Repeals **FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.No.8/04.09.01/2020-21 dated 5 Nov 2020** — that is the circular that invented **80:20**.

**Previously:** bank ≥ 80%, NBFC ≤ 20%, PSL-flavoured. **Now:** each RE under a CLA must **retain a minimum 10%** of each individual loan. The 80:20 split is **gone**. Blended rate to the borrower. Escrow. Partner RE takes its share **on a back-to-back basis, not later than 15 calendar days** from disbursement; miss the clock and the loan stays on the originator and can move only under **Transfer of Loan Exposures**.

| CLA rule | Number | Why FP&A cares |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| Minimum skin in the game | **10% each RE** | No more “originate 20, sell 80” folklore |
| Share hitting partner’s books | **≤ 15 calendar days** | Miss it → TLE, not CLA |
| Borrower-level SMA / NPA | **Same classification both books**, share by end of next working day | Cannot run two DPD clocks |
| DLG by originator | **≤ 5%** of CLA outstanding, *mutatis mutandis* DLD | Same 5% as digital DLG |
| Unrealised CLA profit (NBFCs) | Deduct from **CET1 / NOF** until maturity | Capital, not just P&L |
| Website | List of **all active CLA partners** | Disclosure, not a secret |
| Excluded | Multiple banking, consortium, syndication | Don’t relabel those as CLA |

Applies to: Commercial Banks (**excluding** SFBs, LABs, RRBs) + AIFIs + **NBFCs including HFCs**. MFL as ML-ICC can be originating RE or partner RE. Gold collateral can sit in a CLA; the gold-loan LTV / auction file still applies on top.

**Muthoot Fincorp Context**
- **Branch gold, physical jewellery, cash at counter:** not DLD — but KFS still applies under the 15 Apr 2024 KFS circular, and auction / surplus is the RBC file Thursday taught.
- **Gold-loan app / WhatsApp journey / LSP sourcing:** DLD is on. Disburse **only** to the borrower’s bank account. List the app on the DLA directory. Cooling-off is real even if the gold is already in the safe — exiting inside the window means principal + proportionate APR, gold returned, no penalty.
- **Digital disbursement is not a cash-vs-bank preference.** It is a **prohibition on third-party credits**. A “pay the jeweller directly” flow is a DLD breach.
- **CLA:** if MFL co-lends gold or MSME with a bank, drop 80:20 from every internal note. Model **≥ 10% retained**, blended APR, 15-day partner booking, borrower-level NPA. Unrealised CLA profit does **not** count as CET1 / NOF.
- **Revolving-credit draft (6 Aug)** is a *Credit Facilities* question, not DLD. Gold term / bullet loans are not revolving. Do not mix the two files.
- **FP&A:** zero P&L, zero Q2 restatement from this teaching. The operational ask is a 15-minute check: (1) which apps are on the DLA list, (2) any LSP receiving disbursement, (3) any live CLA still documented as 80:20.

**Quiz yourself:**
1. A 12-month gold loan is sanctioned on an MFL app on Friday. The borrower wants out on Monday without penalty. Allowed? What does she pay?
2. MFL originates a gold loan, a bank takes 90% under a CLA, MFL keeps 10%. Partner’s share is booked on Day 18. Is this still a CLA?
3. An LSP’s current account receives the disbursement “for onward credit the same day”. Does DLD care that the borrower got the money by evening?

*(Answers: (1) Yes — tenor ≥ 7 days so cooling-off ≥ 3 days; she pays principal + proportionate APR only, gold back, no penalty. (2) No — 15-day clock missed; loan stays on MFL and any transfer is TLE, not CLA. 10% itself was fine; the clock killed it. (3) Yes. Same-day onward credit is still a third-party disbursement. Breach.)*
