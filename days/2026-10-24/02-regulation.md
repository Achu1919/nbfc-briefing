## 📚 Daily Teaching: how a public NCD issue actually works (SEBI NCS), using MFL's own Tranche I

Curriculum is in refresher mode (**Day 68**; Day 1 was 18 Aug 2026). Saturday's cycle is **SEBI / IBC**. On 10 Oct we covered LODR Reg 52 and the three 7 Oct NCS circulars at headline level. Today goes one level down into the **primary-issue mechanics** of a retail public NCD. MFL has one open right now, so the prospectus is the textbook. Every number below comes from the Tranche I Prospectus dated 19 Oct 2026 ([Nuvama](https://www.nuvama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Muthoot-FinCorp-Limited-Tranche-I-Prospectus-dated-October-19-2026.pdf)) unless another source is named.

### What is it?
A **public issue of debt securities** sells NCDs to everyone from banks to a retail saver applying through UPI. It runs under the **SEBI (Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities) Regulations, 2021** and the **NCS Master Circular of 15 Oct 2025**. MFL files one **shelf prospectus** for an umbrella amount, then draws it down in **tranches**, each with its own tranche prospectus, coupons and dates.

### The Technical Details

| Building block | Rule (MFL Tranche I as the example) |
|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Draft shelf → shelf** | Draft Shelf Prospectus dated **14 Oct 2026** filed with BSE, open for public comments for **five days**; Shelf Prospectus and Tranche I Prospectus dated **19 Oct 2026** |
| **Shelf size** | Up to **₹4,000 cr** (₹4,00,000 lakh) of secured, redeemable NCDs, face value **₹1,000** |
| **Tranche size** | Base **₹200 cr** + green shoe up to **₹300 cr** = up to **₹500 cr** |
| **Minimum subscription** | **75% of base issue size** (₹150 cr). Miss it and all ASBA money is unblocked within **two working days** of closing, or MFL pays **15% p.a.** interest for the delay. Companies Act s39(3) applies as well |
| **Issue window** | Opened **Fri 23 Oct**, closes **Thu 5 Nov 2026**, 10 a.m.–5 p.m. IST on working days. Early closure or extension is allowed under **Reg 33A** but has to be advertised |
| **Investor categories** | **I** Institutional · **II** Non-institutional (corporates, trusts, co-op banks) · **III** HNI (> ₹2 lakh) · **IV** Retail (≤ ₹2 lakh). UPI bids are allowed up to ₹5 lakh |
| **Allocation ratio** | Institutional **10%** · Non-institutional **20%** · HNI **50%** · Retail **20%** of the tranche limit |
| **Basis of allotment** | **First come, first served** by upload date within each portion; **proportionate** from the date the portion is oversubscribed |
| **Post-issue clock** | T = closing date. Basis of allotment by T+1 (9 p.m.), unblocking 9:30 a.m. T+2, demat credit by 6 p.m. T+2, **trading on T+3**. The prospectus states that the listing timeline moved **from T+6 to T+3** under the NCS Master Circular amendments |
| **Use of proceeds** | **75%** onward lending, financing and repayment of existing borrowings; **25%** general corporate purposes (net proceeds about ₹491 cr if fully subscribed) |
| **Ratings on the offer** | **CRISIL AA/Stable** and **BWR AA+/Stable**, described as "high degree of safety… very low credit risk" |
| **Trust deed** | If the debenture trust deed isn't executed on time, MFL pays at least **2% p.a. over coupon** until it is |

### Why It Matters
- The **75% floor** is a hard gate. Day 1 at 0.55× of base is normal for a two-week retail book, but treasury should track daily build against **₹150 cr**, not against ₹500 cr.
- **The HNI and retail portions together get 70%** of the tranche. That makes these issues a retail-liability product, which is why SEBI is adding the colour meter for exactly this buyer.
- **T+3 listing** means the investor is tradeable, and MFL's money is usable, within about a working week. The cash should land in the ALM ladder around mid-November, not "December".
- **75/25 objects** is a disclosed use. If more than 25% goes to GCP, that's a deviation the monitoring report and the LODR pack will pick up.

### Recent Changes
- **Listing T+6 → T+3**: per the prospectus, under amendments to the NCS Master Circular of 15 Oct 2025.
- **7 Oct 2026: Credit Risk-o-Meter** (circular HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1) adds Chapter II-C to the NCS Master Circular. From about **21 Nov 2026** (45 days after issue) every listed or to-be-listed debt offer document, abridged prospectus, PPM, issuer/OBPP ad and OBPP page must show a six-level colour meter driven by the **lowest** CRA rating. Unsecured paper gets **"unsecured" in bold red**. OBPPs update within **24 hours** of a depository intimation. ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/introduction-of-credit-risk-o-meter-as-an-additional-disclosure-mechanism-for-debt-securities_105081.html) · [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/sebi-credit-risk-o-meter-debt-securities-october-2026))
- **7 Oct 2026: ISIN cap** of 12 vanilla + 5 structured per FY, and **merchant-banker exemption** on ₹10,000-face private placements (filed 8 Oct).

| Meter level | Long-term rating | Colour |
|-------------|------------------|--------|
| Lowest | AAA | Irish Green |
| **Very low** | **AA+ to AA-** | **Chartreuse** |
| Low | A+ to A- | Neon Yellow |
| Moderate | BBB | Caramel |
| Moderate risk of default | BB | Dark Orange |
| High to very high | B and below / C / D | Red |

### Muthoot Fincorp Context
- **Tranche I closes 5 Nov, before the meter goes live.** No retrofit is needed for this tranche's documents.
- **Tranche II (and any private placement listed after ~21 Nov)** needs the meter. On the current ratings the lowest is **AA** (CRISIL), so MFL shows **chartreuse, "Very low"**. A one-notch cut to AA- keeps it chartreuse. A cut to **A+** turns it **neon yellow**, visible on every bond platform within 24 hours. That's a funding-cost risk, not a CRAR one.
- **FP&A asks for this week:** (1) model Tranche I at three sizes (₹150 cr floor, ₹200 cr base, ₹500 cr with full green shoe) in the November ALM and cost of funds; (2) tag proceeds 75/25 from day one so the Reg 52 / monitoring pack reconciles; (3) give CS the rating-mix sheet the meter will read from.
- MFL's equity is unlisted. Its LODR duties come from the **listed NCDs**. Muthoot Finance runs its own, separate NCD programme as a peer. Don't benchmark MFL's subscription against theirs as if it were one book.

### Quiz yourself
1. Tranche I closes on 5 Nov with ₹140 cr of valid bids. What happens to the money, and by when?
2. HNI bids alone reach ₹260 cr on day 4. How are HNI allotments made from that day?
3. Tranche II opens on 1 Dec. CRISIL has moved MFL to AA- and BWR is at AA+. What colour does the meter show?

*(Answers: (1) Below the ₹150 cr minimum (75% of ₹200 cr), so every ASBA amount is unblocked within two working days of 5 Nov; after that MFL owes 15% p.a. for the delay. (2) The HNI portion is 50% of a ₹500 cr limit, i.e. ₹250 cr, so it is oversubscribed and allotment becomes proportionate from that date. (3) The lowest rating, AA-, drives it: chartreuse, "Very low".)*
