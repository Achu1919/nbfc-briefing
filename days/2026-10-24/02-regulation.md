### LODR Regulation 52 — quarterly / half-yearly results for listed debt

**What is it?**

MFL does not need a listed *equity* share to be inside SEBI LODR. Listed **non-convertible securities** (NCDs / bonds) are enough. The moment an NCD is listed, **Chapter V** of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 switches on. Regulation **52** is the results clock. Regulation **54** is the security-cover certificate. Regulation **51** is the “anything material” disclosure. Confusing this with Muthoot Finance’s *equity* LODR (a different company, UL, red brand) is the same class of error as quoting bank CRAR in an NBFC pack.

**The Technical Details**

**Source:** SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, **Chapter V** (obligations of listed entity which has listed its non-convertible securities). Master Circular for LODR compliance dated **30 Jan 2026**. **[EFFECTIVE: ongoing; last NCS circulars 7 Oct 2026 are a different file.]** Applies to **any NBFC with listed NCDs**, all SBR layers. Listing is activity-based, not layer-based. BL with one listed ISIN is in. UL without listed debt is out of Chapter V (though UL listing *obligation* is a separate SBR file MFL does not have).

**The 45-day clock, in this FY:**

| Quarter | Quarter-end | LODR 52 due | What the PDF actually is |
|---------|-------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Q1 FY27 | 30 Jun 2026 | 14 Aug 2026 | Unaudited + limited review. Done. |
| **Q2 / H1 FY27** | **30 Sep 2026** | **14 Nov 2026** | Unaudited + limited review **plus** half-year statement of assets & liabilities **and** cash flow. **This pack.** |
| Q3 FY27 | 31 Dec 2026 | 14 Feb 2027 | Unaudited + limited review. |
| Annual FY27 | 31 Mar 2027 | **30 May 2027** (60 days, not 45) | Audited standalone + consolidated. |

Indian FY is **Apr–Mar**. 30 Sep + 45 calendar days = **14 Nov**. That is a Saturday this year — exchanges still take the filing; do not plan to “file Monday”.

**What must be in the 14 Nov PDF (the bits FP&A actually owns):**

1. **Financial results** — P&L for the quarter *and* year-to-date (H1). Comparative previous-year quarter and previous-year H1.
2. **Limited review** by the statutory auditor. Unsigned review = the exchange will not take the filing.
3. **H1 extras** — statement of assets and liabilities, cash-flow statement. Q1 and Q3 do not need these. Q2 does.
4. **Reg 52(4) line items** that a debt investor actually reads: debt-equity, outstanding redeemable preference shares (if any), net worth, PAT, EPS, and — for an NBFC — **CRAR**, **GNPA**, **NNPA**, and the other ratios the regulation / master circular lists. Denominator for MFL GNPA/NNPA is **gross carrying amount** (the 7-step walk: gross AUM − ARC sold − DA & co-lending off-book + accrued interest − fee amortisation). MHFL uses on-book AUM; do not consolidate that walk without restating.
5. **Security cover (Reg 54)** — a certificate, with the cover ratio, for the listed NCDs. This is CS / treasury / debenture trustee, but the *ratio* has to tie to the 30 Sep books.
6. **Previous / next due dates** of interest and principal on listed NCDs, and whether they were paid on time.
7. **Credit rating** of the listed NCDs, with any change since last disclosure.
8. **Utilisation of issue proceeds (Reg 52(7) / (7A))** — only if a listed issue is still being deployed. Material deviation is a separate disclosure, not a footnote you hope nobody reads. The **13 Oct consultation paper** on monitoring-agency / utilisation is **not live law**; do not rewrite 52(7) off a CP.

**Board-meeting choreography (the bit that slips):**

- Prior intimation of the Board meeting that will *consider* the results (Reg 50) — typically **at least 2 working days** before the meeting for NCS.
- Results must be filed with the exchange **within 30 minutes** of the Board meeting ending (Reg 52 / master circular timing). The 14 Nov date is the *outer* clock; the *inner* clock is 30 minutes after the Board says yes.
- Newspaper publication of the results follows the filing, in the form specified.

**Why It Matters**

A late or incomplete Reg 52 filing is a **SEBI / exchange** event, not an RBI event — but it is the one the NCD holders, rating agencies and the debenture trustee all read on the same afternoon. Fine / freeze / “why was CRAR different from the CIMS return” questions land on FP&A, not on the branch. Hero Fincorp’s ₹10L was an RBI interest-charge file; a LODR miss is a different regulator, same reputational shape.
