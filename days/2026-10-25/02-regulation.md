### The addressee test — how not to treat a bank Basel file as an NBFC event

**What is it?**

RBI’s circular index has a column most people skip: **Meant For**. If that cell says “Commercial Banks”, “All Authorised Dealers”, or is blank *and* the title starts with “Commercial Banks – …”, it is not your file. An NBFC-addressed circular names NBFCs in the salutation (Chairpersons/CEOs of … Non-Banking Financial Companies …) or sits in an NBFC Master Direction. Confusing `297` (bank leverage ratio, 1 Apr 2027) with MFL’s CRAR 15% is the same class of error as quoting Muthoot Finance’s UL listing obligation in an MFL pack.
