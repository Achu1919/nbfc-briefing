## 📚 Daily Teaching — CRAR 15% / gold T1 12% / quarterly profits in Owned Fund

Curriculum is in post-Day-30 refresher mode (**Day 70**; start 18 Aug 2026). Weekday cycle for Monday is **SBR / capital**. Sunday was the **addressee test**. This morning produced **no** new circular, so the pairing is the bank file still sitting at the top of the index (`297`) versus the NBFC capital MD that actually goes into the 14 Nov pack.

### What is it?

CRAR is the NBFC’s capital ratio: **(Tier I + Tier II) ÷ risk-weighted assets**. For MFL as **ML**, the floor is **15%**, of which Tier I must be at least **10%**. If gold jewellery is **≥ 50% of assets**, Tier I lifts to **12%** (the gold overlay) — CRAR stays 15%. That is a *different file* from `297`, which is the **bank** leverage-ratio 2017 Standard, effective **1 Apr 2027**. Quoting `297` on an MFL CRAR slide is the same class of error as quoting Muthoot Finance’s UL CET1 9% in an MFL pack.

The 14 Nov LODR 52 PDF will print a CRAR. The numerator of that CRAR may include **H1 profits** — but only after the **10 Mar 2026** Owned-Fund amendment. That is the number FP&A actually owns this week.

### The Technical Details

**Source (floors):** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025 (`RBI/DOR/2025-26` series, [id=12959](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12959)), read with the SBR layering in the Registration / SBR Directions as amended **24 Jun 2026** (`RBI/2026-27/163`). **[EFFECTIVE: ongoing; UL cut ₹1,00,000 cr from 2026-06-24.]** Applies to **ML / UL** for CRAR; BL uses a leverage cap, not CRAR.

**Source (quarterly profits in Owned Fund):** `RBI/2025-26/226` `DOR.CAP.REC.No.416/21.01.002/2025-26` dated **10 Mar 2026** — Capital Adequacy **Second** Amendment Directions, 2026. Addressed to **All NBFCs**. **[EFFECTIVE:2026-03-10, immediate.]** Replaces paragraph 9(iii) of the MD. [Circular](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13311&Mode=0).

**The floors (do not mix with bank Basel):**

**The 10 Mar formula — this is what H1 CRAR is allowed to add:**

Owned Fund’s “free reserves” now expressly **include quarterly profits**, but only if **both** gates open:

1. The financial statements are subjected to **limited review / audit on a quarterly basis by the statutory auditors**.
2. Profits are haircut for average dividend of the last three financial years:

> **EP<sub>t</sub> = NP<sub>t</sub> − 0.25 × D × t**

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **t** | Quarter number of the current FY. H1 = **t = 2**. Q3 = 3. Year-end = 4. |
| **NP<sub>t</sub>** | Net profit **up to** quarter t (cumulative, not the quarter in isolation). |
| **D** | Average dividend paid for / pertaining to the **last three financial years**. |
| **EP<sub>t</sub>** | The amount that may be **reckoned** in Owned Fund / free reserves. |

**H1 worked (t = 2):** EP₂ = NP₂ − 0.25 × D × 2 = **NP₂ − 0.5 × D**. You do **not** add the full H1 PAT to CRAR. You add PAT minus half of the three-year average dividend.

**Losses:** “Losses in the current year shall be **fully deducted** from Owned Fund.” Asymmetry is deliberate — profits are haircut, losses are 100%.

**Without a statutory-auditor limited review, H1 profit is not in the CRAR numerator.** That is why the 14 Nov limited-review booking is not just a LODR 52 hygiene item — it is the **gate** for the capital number the same PDF prints.

### Why It Matters

A colleague who forwards `297` and says “RBI tightened capital” is reading the **wrong MD**. The NBFC capital MD did **not** move this weekend. What *does* move the 14 Nov CRAR is (a) 30 Sep RWA, (b) whether gold jewellery is still ≥50% of assets (T1 12% vs 10%), and (c) whether the auditor’s limited review is in, so EP₂ can sit in Owned Fund.

Getting (c) wrong in either direction is a filing error: adding full H1 PAT without the dividend haircut **overstates** CRAR; omitting eligible EP₂ when the limited review is done **understates** it. Both are Board-pack problems, not P&L restatements.
