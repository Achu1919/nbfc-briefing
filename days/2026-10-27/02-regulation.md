## 📚 Daily Teaching — SMA / 90-day NPA / borrower-wise upgrade (the 14 Nov GNPA clock)

Curriculum is in post-Day-30 refresher mode (**Day 71**; start 18 Aug 2026). Weekday cycle for Tuesday is **IRAC / NPA**. Last Tuesday taught **SNFA 40C–40D** (gold jewellery is not SNFA). Monday taught **CRAR**. This morning produced **no** new circular, so teaching stays on the cycle’s *operational* half — the day-end process that actually prints GNPA / NNPA in the 14 Nov pack.

### What is it?

An account does not “become NPA when the branch notices”. It becomes overdue, SMA, then NPA as part of the **day-end process on a calendar date**. SMA is still **standard** (stressed, not NPA). NPA starts the morning after **90 continuous days overdue**. Classification is **borrower-wise, not facility-wise**: one gold loan slipping past 90 days pulls **every** facility of that borrower into NPA. Upgrade is the mirror image — **all** arrears on **all** facilities must be paid; a partial catch-up on the gold ticket does not lift the MSME ticket.

That clock, not AUM, is what the 14 Nov GNPA% is allowed to show.

### The Technical Details

**Source:** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025 — `RBI/DOR/2025-26/356` `DOR.STR.REC.No.275/21.04.048/2025-26`, [updated 1 Oct 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12948) (SNFA 40C–40D live; **not today’s topic**). Origin of the day-end / SMA illustration: circular `DOR.STR.REC.68/21.04.048/2021-22` dated **12 Nov 2021**, now paras **18–19** of the MD. **[EFFECTIVE: ongoing for ML; 90-day NPA fully live.]** Applies to **all layers** for Chapter II; ML Chapter IV **adds** the 0.40% standard line.

**Applies to:** MFL as **ML** NBFC-ICC. HFC (MHFL) follows the HFC Directions where they conflict. NBFC-MFI portfolio is carved out of paras 21–27 (para 20) — MFI Directions prevail on that book.

**1. Overdue is a day-end flag, not a branch opinion (paras 18–19)**

- Flag overdue as part of the **day-end process for the due date**, irrespective of *when* that process is run.
- SMA and NPA classification is the same day-end process. The SMA / NPA **date** is the calendar date for which day-end ran.
- Consumer-education duty (para 17): website examples of overdue / SMA / NPA / upgrade, with specific reference to day-end. Front-line officers must explain this at sanction / disbursal / renewal.

**Illustration in the MD (para 19):** due date **31 Mar 2021**, dues not received before day-end → overdue date = **31 Mar 2021**. Still overdue:

| Calendar date of day-end | Continuous overdue | Tag |
|--------------------------|--------------------|-----|
| 31 Mar 2021 | Day 0 (due date missed) | Overdue / SMA-0 window starts |
| 30 Apr 2021 | 30 days | **SMA-1** |
| 30 May 2021 | 60 days | **SMA-2** |
| 29 Jun 2021 | 90 days | **NPA** |

A gold **bullet** due on 31 Mar that is not repaid before that day’s day-end is overdue on 31 Mar. The 90-day clock does **not** wait for auction. Auction is recovery conduct (Thursday’s file); classification is IRAC.

**2. Borrower-wise, not facility-wise (para 23)**

> Asset classification shall be **borrower-wise and not facility-wise**. All the facilities granted by an NBFC to a borrower **and investment in all the securities issued by the borrower** shall have to be treated as NPA / NPI and not the particular facility / investment or part thereof which has become irregular.

One 91-DPD gold loan + one current MSME loan to the same PAN = **both NPA**. Co-lending does not save you: CLA 2025 para 28 makes borrower-level SMA / NPA **binding across all REs**. Partner-serviced accounts must still be classified on **own** day-end DPD.

**3. Upgrade is all-facility, all-arrears (paras 24–25)**

- Para 24: NPA → standard only if **entire arrears of interest and principal** are paid.
- Para 25: more than one credit facility → upgrade only upon repayment of entire arrears **pertaining to all the credit facilities**.
- Para 22: rescheduling by itself does **not** upgrade. Restructuring follows the Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions, 2025 (`id=12947`), not a handshake.

Paying the gold overdue and leaving ₹1 of MSME interest unpaid keeps **both** NPA.

**4. What the 14 Nov GNPA / NNPA is allowed to be**

No MD prescribes a formula. House convention (MFL standalone, 7-step):

1. Gross AUM
2. − ARC sold
3. − DA & co-lending off-book
4. + accrued interest
5. − fee amortisation
6. = **gross carrying amount** (pre-loss-allowance) = denominator
7. GNPA% = NPA outstanding ÷ that denominator. NNPA nets **specific** NPA provisions in the **numerator only**. Standard-asset 0.40% (ML para 55) is **not** netted.

MHFL uses on-book AUM and skips accrual + amortisation — restatement needed before any consolidated / audited tie-out. Classification comes from **LMS day-end DPD** (paras 18–19), never from AUM.

Post-NPA accrual goes to interest suspense and is **excluded** (para 12.2). Technical write-offs drop out of audited GNPA but stay NPA for borrower-level tracking (12 Nov 2021).

**5. Provisioning that sits under those tags (do not import bank IRAC slabs)**

| Tag | Rate | Source |
|-----|------|--------|
| Standard (incl. SMA) — **ML** | **0.40%** of outstanding — **not** reckoned for NNPA | ML para 55 |
| Sub-standard | **10%** of total outstanding | para 32(1) |
| Doubtful — unsecured | **100%** of amount not covered by realisable security | para 32(2)(i) |
| Doubtful-1 (≤1 yr) secured | **20%** of realisable security | para 32(2)(ii) |
| Doubtful-2 (1–3 yr) secured | **30%** | para 32(2)(ii) |
| Doubtful-3 (>3 yr) secured | **50%** | para 32(2)(ii) |

| Ind AS 109 ECL < IRAC floor | Difference to **Impairment Reserve** from PAT — not CET1; no withdrawal without DoS; counts to floor, **not** to NNPA | paras 34–36 |

Bank SS 15%/25% and bank doubtful 25/40/100 are the **wrong statute**. Gold security is “realisable security” for the doubtful *secured* slab; it does **not** stop the 90-day NPA clock.

### Why It Matters

The 14 Nov PDF prints GNPA / NNPA. The number is a **day-end, borrower-wise** count as at **30 Sep**, not a management overlay. Three errors that DoS and the auditor both catch:

1. **Facility-wise NPA** — gold ticket NPA, MSME ticket still “standard”. Para 23 forbids it.
2. **Partial upgrade** — borrower pays the gold overdue, MSME ₹500 short, both tickets flipped to standard. Paras 24–25 forbid it.
3. **AUM denominator** — GNPA% off AUM instead of gross carrying amount. Looks close on monthly-rest EMI; will not tie to audited notes / RBI returns.

Overnight gold-loan *stress colour* (Piramal / Bajaj / Macquarie, 21–22 Oct) is a **volume / SMA** story, not a new IRAC circular. It makes the borrower-wise 90-day rule *hotter*, because a borrower already >90 DPD **elsewhere** is a credit-decision issue; it does **not** let you restatement-shop SMA for 14 Nov.
