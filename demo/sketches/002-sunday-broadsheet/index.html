A traffic light for NBFCs.

RBI spent 31 July folding years of circulars into five Master Directions. Buried in the first of them is something new: Prompt Corrective Action, the tool it already uses on banks, now pointed at the large NBFCs.

Filed 22 August, 10:41 · NBFC Guru · nbfc-lerner

Today’s Daily Watch did not print. The 7:00 AM job failed. A paper that keeps dates will show you that. A chat window will not.

The five directions cover miscellaneous supervision, cybersecurity, statutory audit, the compliance function, and supervisory returns. Same substance, one shelf. The new substance is PCA.

If CRAR, Tier 1 or NNPA cross published lines, RBI can stop dividends, demand capital, and — if it wishes — say so in public. Exit takes four clean quarters and the supervisor’s comfort that profits will last.

What it asks of Fincorp

Upper Layer, many branches: CFSS is no longer a project you can postpone. Quarterly progress goes to RBI. Interest must run from disbursement, not sanction. The PCA tripwires to stay above: CRAR 15%, Tier 1 10%, NNPA 6%.

Also this fortnight: Muthoot MCred, Pappachan group, fined ₹3.10 lakh for upgrading NPAs without full arrears. IRAC is being read across the group. Do not upgrade without every rupee of overdue interest and principal, on every facility.

FP&A, four things

1. Monthly CRAR / Tier 1 / NNPA, not just quarter-end.

2. Internal amber at least 200 bps inside each official line.

3. No NPA upgrade without full arrears — see MCred.

4. Board pack: PCA indicators every quarter.
