## Variant: Evening folio

### Design stance
A bound pamphlet. Chapters, not cards. Read at night, not scan at 7:05.

### Key choices
- Layout: single narrow column, contents then chapters
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond headings, Outfit body
- Color: warm near-black, ivory, one gold line
- Interaction: contents page jumps to a chapter; missing Watch is named, not faked

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: long-read regulation notes
- Weak at: tables, many days, morning speed

### Best for
People who would rather read the PCA note like an essay than like a dashboard.
