Chapter I · Regulation · 22 August

RBI has a new way to say stop.

For years PCA belonged to banks. On 31 July it arrived for the large NBFCs, tucked into the first of five Master Directions that otherwise mostly tidy the cupboard.

Think of a traffic light. Green is ordinary life. Amber is caution: dividends stop, promoters are asked for equity. Red starts cutting capex and variable cost. The supervisor may print the name.

The lines for a non-CIC: stay above 15% CRAR, 10% Tier 1, and keep NNPA under 6%. Four clean quarters to leave, one of them the annual audit.

Muthoot MCred was fined ₹3.10 lakh for calling an NPA standard before the arrears were gone. The group is being read.

Fincorp, specifically

CFSS, because you are Upper Layer with a real branch net. Interest from the day money leaves, not the day a file is sanctioned. Internal ambers at least 200 basis points inside the official lines. A PCA page in the quarterly board pack.

The list

Seventeen names for 2026–27. Muthoot Finance is on it. That is not this company. Do not let a sentence in a note pretend otherwise.

Chapter II · Daily Watch · 19 August · gold Tuesday

Sixty-nine percent, and a market that would not look up.

Gold-jewellery loans at the NBFCs ran to ₹3.42 lakh crore in June. Fastest book in the system. Sensex, that Monday, still fell three hundred points.

Muthoot Finance — again, the cousin — printed Q1 PAT of ₹2,825 crore, up 43%. Fitch kept BB+, stable. Jefferies had already cut FY27 profits on a quieter gold price and banks walking back into the trade.

Gold overtook vehicles as the largest securitised asset class in the quarter. NBFCs wrote almost all of it. That is an ALM tap if you want it, and a reminder that the bid can move.

Around the sector

Manappuram’s PAT quadrupled. Chola will raise ₹55,000 crore of NCDs. Microfinance: only 2% of borrowers now sit with four or more lenders. The remaining 1.35 million are still the book to watch.

The news desk only flagged the revolving-credit draft. The argument lives in Chapter I and in Friday’s regulation file — comments due 28 August.
